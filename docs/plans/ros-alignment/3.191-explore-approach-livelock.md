# 3.191 Explore's approach livelock: a goal where the robot stands, re-sent every tick

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.191; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.70.M2's run 1 (`evaluations/find-last-seen-370/rows.jsonl`, the
unsurveyed arm, k 19, shoes, detector seed 37040, start seed 3702,
`complex_house`, `InflatedNav`) ended `out_of_budget` after 1,200 s having
driven 1.96 m and sent 4,662 goals: from the 11th event on, an `approach`
goal 0.1 m from the robot was sent on every other tick and ended
`succeeded` after 0 s and 0 m, 4,657 times, while the shoes were 3.8 m
away. The M2 note inferred the straight-line fallback of `_approach_point`;
that inference was wrong (below).

**Reproduction (2026-11-20, `dev` `098669bc`).** `find()` of
`evaluations/find-last-seen-370/sweep.py` on k 19, unsurveyed, `inflated`,
re-run in process: the same row (4,662 goals, 1.96 m, `out_of_budget`).
Traced with `_send_approach` instrumented (first 200 s: 662 goals):

* the robot stands at (4.72, 6.95); the sighting it approaches is at
  (4.29, 7.51), 0.70 m away (the lidar's range at the detection's bearing
  hits something short of the shoes);
* `_at_target()` (within `APPROACH_FAR_M` + 0.1 = 0.70 m) is **False** by a
  hair, so `_send_approach` does not take its "already here" branch and
  asks `_approach_point` -> `_band_point`;
* `_band_point` returns the reachable cell nearest the robot by path whose
  **centre** lies 0.35-0.60 m from the sighting: the robot's own cell,
  centre (4.65, 7.05), 0.12 m from the robot and 0.58 m from the sighting;
* nav2 (the stand-in; nav2 likewise, inside its xy tolerance) ends that goal
  `succeeded` at once without moving;
* `_goal_ended("succeeded")` for an approach queues a FACE and sets
  `_approach = None`, so on the next tick the same sighting is "moved"
  (no approach on record) and the same goal is sent again. Nothing counts a
  succeeded approach that never left against the place.

**Root cause, to the line:** `brain/explore.py` `_goal_ended`, the
`succeeded` branch for `kind == "approach"`: it clears `_approach` and
charges nothing, whether or not the robot moved; with `_band_point`
answering the robot's own cell (in band by its centre while the robot is
just outside `_at_target`'s band), the approach is re-sent forever.

**The fix (step A).** An approach goal that ends `succeeded` with the robot
less than `STUCK_MOVED_M` (0.15 m) from where the goal started has made no
progress: it counts one retry failure on the sighting
(`RetryBook.fail`), so the sighting cools (`RETRY_COOLDOWN_S`, 30 s) and is
dropped at `RETRY_LIMIT` (3), as a stalled approach already is. The face on
arrival still runs. Nothing else changes. *Review round 1 (before any sweep
row was kept):* inside `_at_target()`'s band such an approach is not charged
but kept on record (`_approach`), so the next frame closes in (3.80.G) or
counts it in sight and unfound (3.106), each bounded by `CLOSE_IN_REFUSED`,
as 3.85 keeps a refused send in the band.

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. **Red first:** a unit test in `tests/test_explore.py` that reproduces the
   geometry (a sighting 0.70 m out, the band cell under the robot, a nav
   that succeeds without moving) fails on `dev` (more than `RETRY_LIMIT`
   approach goals to one place) and passes with the fix.
2. **k 19 stability:** re-run in process (`InflatedNav`), approach goals
   that end `succeeded` having moved < 0.15 m, per sighting place
   (within `RETRY_RADIUS_M`, 0.6 m): **at most 3**; all goals in the
   mission **at most 300** (`dev`: 4,657 and 4,662).
3. **k 19 progress:** the mission ends without a wrong `found`
   (0.9 m rule), with 0 contacts, and **drives at least 10 m**
   (`dev`: 1.96 m) -- it went on searching; `found` is recorded, not barred
   (`InflatedNav` aborts much of `complex_house`, 3.175.A).
4. **Sweep stability:** 3.70.M2's 20 unsurveyed finds (its plan: the same
   starts, targets and detector seeds), under **both** `inflated` and
   `fake`, fix and `dev` (`098669bc`'s `brain/explore.py`) on the same
   inputs: in every mission, no-move succeeded approaches per place
   **at most 3**, and 0 missions above.
5. **Sweep progress:** per navigator, the fix's `found` (right place) is
   **at least `dev`'s**; wrong `found` 0 and contacts 0 in both; every
   mission whose outcome differs from `dev`'s is listed with its reason.
6. Frozen references keep their exact answers (the suite passes unedited).
