# 3.198 The final approach on whole-move verbs: c-1's 40 pivots, and docking by margin

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.198; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.195's live run c-1 (`evaluations/nogo-3195/c-1.json`, C3) found its
target and did not dock. The return ended `failed: not_aligned` 225 s after the
find. The section was opened (the orchestrator, on the user's delegation,
2026-11-21) as a race: the find ended while the approach's nav2 goal was
still active, and the robot server refused the return's goal `preempted`.
3.156.D's live run 2 failed the same way (40 verbs, 225 s, `not_aligned`), and
its cause was never found. The slug keeps the race name the section opened
with. The trace below shows the race is not the cause.

## The cause, traced (3.198.A)

**From c-1's ground truth** (`samples`: the true pose every 0.25 s, with the
SLAM pose beside it). The dock is the start pose, (1.95, 1.35) facing east
(90 deg). The staging point is 0.30 m behind it, (1.65, 1.35).

| t (s) | What the truth shows |
|---|---|
| 37.8 | the find stops the robot at (6.20, 1.45) |
| 37.8-41.3 | standing: the return's goal is refused, then taken; the robot moves home from 41.3 s |
| 65.3 | nav2's goal ends at (1.754, 1.366): **0.104 m short of the staging point, on the dock's side** (it came from the east, and nav2's goal checker ends a goal at `xy_goal_tolerance` 0.10 m) |
| 65.3-71.0 | standing: a pivot to face the dock (a turn does not move x, y) |
| 71.0-74.1 | one creep of **0.30 m**, to (2.0525, 1.3439): **0.1025 m past the dock**, outside `DOCK_TOL_M` 0.10 |
| 74.1-263 | x, y unchanged to 0.1 mm for 189 s; then `return failed: not_aligned` (no numbers: `MAX_ACTIONS`, 40 verbs) |

**The refusal at 37.8-41.3 s is not the failure.** The robot stopped for 3.5 s.
`ReturnTrip._goal` retries a refused goal every `GOAL_RETRY_S` for
`RETURN_WAIT_S` (3.156 A6: after the brain's own stop it holds the robot until
its authority lapses). The goal was taken and nav2 drove the robot home. The
failure is in the last 0.3 m.

**Cause 1: a creep is whole 0.30 m moves.** `robot/ros_drive.py` `moves_for()`
turns a straight verb into `max(1, round(speed/100 * 2 * duration))` moves of
0.30 m (the sim's cell rule, kept so verbs mean the same with or without ROS).
`ReturnTrip`'s creep asks for `min(1.5 s, along / v)`, here 0.197 m. It gets
0.30 m, the smallest move there is. So the dock error is nav2's stop error at
the staging point, mirrored to the far side of the dock. All 10 of 3.195's
c-runs ended **0.088-0.103 m** past the dock (truth). The 9 that docked passed
3.156's 0.10 m bar by **0.000-0.014 m**, and so did 3.194's live dock
(0.086 m) and 3.156.E's G3 (worst 0.098 m).

**Cause 2: a livelock in `brain/dock.py` `_approach()`**, `dev` `b5c46876`.
* `creep` (line 549): `_step_m`, what the last creep closed, is set from
  `_last_along` and **never cleared**. Entering `final` clears `_last_along`
  only.
* In `final` (line 571), with the robot 0.1025 m past the dock and facing it:
  the heading is fine and the error is above `DOCK_TOL_M`, so it goes to
  `turn`.
* `turn` (line 538): over 0.05 m away, it faces the dock's bearing, a
  180-degree pivot.
* `creep`: `abs(along)` is 0.1025 and `0.5 * _step_m` is 0.15, so the
  overshoot guard sends it straight to `final` with no move.
* `final` turns 180 degrees back to the dock's heading. The error is the same.
* Two pivots a cycle, about 5 s each, at one place, until 40 actions:
  `not_aligned` with no numbers.
* Any residual between `DOCK_TOL_M` (0.10) and half a move (0.15) does this.

**Reproduced in process** (`tests/test_dock_quantised.py`, red on `dev`
`b5c46876`): `ReturnTrip` on a body whose straight verbs are whole 0.30 m
moves, and a navigator that ends a goal when the robot is within `stop_m` of
it. With c-1's 0.104 m stop: `failed`, `not_aligned`, the last verbs `RIGHT`,
`LEFT`, `RIGHT`... all at (2.054, 1.348). Stops of 0.0-0.10 m dock. 0.104 and
0.11 fail.

**What the design must do (3.198.B).**
* **D1. No pivot cycle.** When `final` finds the error above `DOCK_TOL_M`
  and the next creep would overshoot by more than it closes (the creep's own
  guard), it does not turn back to the dock. It goes to D2. With D2 spent,
  the return ends at once: `not_aligned: <m> m, <deg> deg`.
* **D2. A residual the moves cannot close is closed by nav2.** nav2 moves
  continuously and ends a goal on its checker's circle. The brain sends one
  goal **past the dock**, along the line from the robot through the dock, by
  how far short of its last goal nav2 stopped. That distance is measured on
  this return, not copied from nav2's config, so no fact is added on both
  sides of the ROS wall. nav2 then ends on the dock. Then `final`: the dock's
  heading, and the judge.
  * At most `FINE_TRIES` (2) such goals.
  * The goal is a nav2 goal like the trip's own: refused while the brain's
    last verb holds the robot, retried every 1 s (A6), never a new motion
    path.
* **Nothing else changes.** Continuous verbs (the car under `drive: direct`,
  the sweeps' `exact_verbs`) close the residual with the creep, as before,
  and send no extra goal. Nothing changes in `robot/` (arbitration, safety,
  `ros_drive`) or `service/`.
