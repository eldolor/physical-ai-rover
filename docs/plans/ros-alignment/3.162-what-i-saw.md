# 3.162 What I saw: a mission's objects with photos and places, on the twin

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.162; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the user, 2026-11-06: features of a robot vacuum (Dreame) worth leveraging, for full autonomy; 3.158) A vacuum's app shows a photo of each obstacle it avoided, on
the map; people trust what they can check. Here every mission already keeps
an object inventory (3.46: labels placed by the lidar) and frames, but
nobody reads it, and the twin shows only an end state. For a find, the
person wants to see the frame it called `found`; for a survey, what it saw
in each room.

**Scope.**
* The end of a mission or survey: a list on the twin of what it saw (from
  the inventory and the arrival frame), each with a thumbnail, its room
  (3.160's names where given) and its place on the map; tap to see the
  frame.
* Frames stay on the robot (local); nothing new is uploaded unless the user
  says so (3.46's decision: labels and positions only).
* Bounded: thumbnails kept per mission with a cap, deleted with the mission.

**Criteria (written in step A, before building).** At least: the found
frame shown for every `found` (100%), taps to it (bar), storage per
mission (bar), and phone-size evidence.

## 3.162.A: criteria and design (written 2026-11-07, before building)

**What it rests on.** 3.46's inventory (each mission's landmarks: label,
lidar-placed position, belief, looks; `GET /mission/inventory`; the S3 copy
carries labels and positions only, 3.46's decision, stripped to the
mission's own landmarks since 3.50.D5), 3.50.D5's `frame_id` (positions in
the saved map's frame), the runner's arrival frame (`MissionRunner` keeps
it for the identity check and 3.53's late confirmation), 3.70's end states
and report (rooms entered, m² mapped), 3.160's room names, 3.99's mission
id, and the twin's mission card on Home (3.132 Stage 3).

**What it can show, honestly.** On the simulator's frames the inventory
names every object it sees. **On real frames it names only the target**:
3.46's namer for camera frames was closed by the user on 2026-10-10 (no
namer reached the 75% judge bar), to be reopened on the Rover's frames. So
on the car the list holds the found (or `arrived_unconfirmed`) frame and
the target's sightings; other objects join it only if that namer is built.
The criteria below say which of them the car can be held to.

**D1. A mission's report on disk.** One directory per mission,
`<report_dir>/<mission_id>/` (`brain.report_dir`, default under the brain's
data dir), written by the brain off the tick thread (a queue and a writer
thread, as 3.99's log; a write that fails is logged once and never fails a
mission):
* `report.json`: the end state, job, target, times, rooms entered (3.160's
  names where given, else the model's label marked `source: model`), the
  `frame_id`, and one row per landmark: label, position, room, belief,
  looks, and its thumbnail's file;
* `found.jpg`: the frame the arrival's identity check judged, as sent (its
  SHA-256 in `report.json`), with the target's box; for `found` and for
  `arrived_unconfirmed` (marked so);
* `thumbs/<n>.jpg`: one per landmark, the crop of its best-belief look,
  160 px on the long side.

**D2. Bounded.** At most 60 thumbnails a mission (the highest-belief
landmarks kept); each at most 12 KB, `found.jpg` at most 150 KB, so a
mission is at most **1 MB**; the last 50 missions kept (**50 MB** at most),
the oldest deleted first; a person deletes one from the twin, every file
with it.

**D3. Local only.** Frames and thumbnails never leave the robot's disk except
to the person's own phone over the twin's authenticated route
(`require_secret`; through the tunnel as Drive via brain's frames already
travel); never to S3, CloudFront, git or a log. 3.46's S3 inventory upload
is unchanged: labels and positions, no image.

**D4. Routes and the twin.** `GET /mission/reports` (the list),
`GET /mission/reports/{id}`, `GET /mission/reports/{id}/found.jpg` and
`/thumbs/{n}.jpg`, `DELETE /mission/reports/{id}`, all behind the secret,
each with its route-guard entry (CLAUDE.md section 6). On Home, an ended
mission's card shows the found frame's thumbnail; one tap opens it full
size; "What it saw" opens the list (thumbnail, label, room, a dot on the
map); a tap on an item opens its frame. Operator words in `strings.js`.

**Not a safety veto.** It reads and shows; nothing it writes reaches the
robot server, the bridge or nav2. Rule 4 unchanged.

**Ties to 3.158.** C1/C2's truth: the user judges each `found` against the
found frame shown here (beside the robot in the room). D: after an
unattended stretch, the user reviews every mission's found frame here.
