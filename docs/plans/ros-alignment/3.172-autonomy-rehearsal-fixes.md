## 3.172.A: F1 + F2 (criteria written 2026-11-09, before any measurement)

**The defect.** At the trap the robot's rear corner is 1.2 cm from a chair: a
pivot is refused, REVERSE is refused, and `_creep_ends()` returns nothing
because the forward end is over the bar. Truth geometry (at `B/DIAGNOSIS.md`'s pose): moving forward 3-15 cm frees a full 360 degrees of
swing; 20 cm or more is worse. So the creep must be **bounded** (about 10 cm),
and an unbounded creep to the 3 cm line would not do.

**The change.** (F1) When the escape's REVERSE is refused and the front is not
open enough for 3.85's FORWARD, one forward creep of 3.3 s at creep speed (10 cm),
at most once per 0.30 m of the mission's track, through `_verb` and so through the
same `check_and_execute`, at the same 3 cm creep bar. (F2) The return treats 45 s
of nav2 with under 5 cm of travel as a stall: it cancels its own goal, backs off,
and on a refused back-off makes the same creep, then sends the goal again; after 3
such rounds at one place it ends `failed` (`no_motion`) instead of waiting 600 s.

**Instrument.** `evaluations/rehearsal-fixes-3172/trap_sweep.py`: 20 poses
sampled around the trap (seed 3172, +/-3 cm, +/-5 deg), each truly trapped
(truth gap >= 1.1 cm, the rehearsal's lowest, and < 180 deg of swing), in
`home_first_floor_stairs`, the real `ExploreAgent` or `ReturnTrip`, the real
`SafetyController`, drop guard on, `MockRobot(exact_verbs=True)`, on simulated
time. Arms: `off` (the change's constants zeroed), `on`.

**Amendments (written before the runs they affect).**

   no world, so the nudge could not fire: not a measurement of the change):* of the
   20 poses, 11 had the forward end under the bar, and the existing creep freed

## 3.172.D: F6, not built (2026-11-10; the orchestrator's choice A)

**Why not.** F6 was "a survey ends `exhausted` when every room was entered and the
remaining frontiers have stalled". Nine surveys (3.172.B's 2, 3.158.B's 7) say it
would not fire, and that no end rule fixes what fails:
1. **The surveys are slow, not stalled.** Their last new room came at 1,133-1,637 s
   of 1,800, with 11-38 frontier candidates still open. Small frontiers (< 0.4 m)
   found new rooms as fast per second as large ones (26 rooms in 4,751 s against 39 in
   7,076 s, about 180 s a room each): leaving them out costs rooms.
2. **Laundry and pantry cannot be reached by any goal a survey sends.** On truth
   geometry a centre kept 0.3265 m from walls and things (3.43's goal clearance) reaches
   neither (nor den or half bath, which surveys do enter on their way through); at
   0.25 m all 13 rooms are reachable. Finds enter laundry by an approach to a sighted
   target; no survey has entered laundry or pantry (0 of 9). "Every room entered" never
   holds, and any rule that ends a survey before 1,800 s ends one still finding rooms:
   it would relabel `out_of_budget`, not fix it.
   *Corrected by 3.173.A:* that reach required the whole path to keep the goal
   clearance; explore's paths need 0.15 m, so both rooms are reachable. A survey
   lacks a place inside them for a goal to stand (inferred on SLAM's map).

**3.158.B's C4 (surveys `exhausted`, 4 of 5) is not addressed by 3.172.** Small rooms
are 3.173's (`3.173-surveys-small-rooms.md`), steps and bars only, not built.

**Built instead** (the orchestrator's ask, so B4's cause can be read next rehearsal):
`goal_sent` names `set_aside_at`, at most `SET_ASIDE_LOGGED` (20) candidates the choice
skipped for a place carried set aside (not this mission's own cooldown), in both the
rule's and the pooled chooser's path. `tests/test_set_aside_3172.py` (two tests; the
first fails with the note switched off). No line of `robot/` changes.
