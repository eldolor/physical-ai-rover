## 3.172.A: F1 + F2 (criteria written 2026-11-09, before any measurement)

**The defect.** At the trap the robot's rear corner is 1.2 cm from a chair: a
pivot is refused, REVERSE is refused, and `_creep_ends()` returns nothing
because the forward end is over the bar. Truth geometry (at `B/DIAGNOSIS.md`'s pose): moving forward 3-15 cm frees a full 360 degrees of
swing; 20 cm or more is worse. So the creep must be **bounded** (about 10 cm),
and an unbounded creep to the 3 cm line would not do.

**The change.** (F1) When the escape's REVERSE is refused and the front is not
open enough for 3.85's FORWARD, one forward creep of 3.3 s at creep speed (10 cm),
at most once per 0.30 m of the mission's track, through `_verb` and so through the
same `check_and_execute`, at the same 3 cm creep bar. (F2) The return treats 45 s
of nav2 with under 5 cm of travel as a stall: it cancels its own goal, backs off,
and on a refused back-off makes the same creep, then sends the goal again; after 3
such rounds at one place it ends `failed` (`no_motion`) instead of waiting 600 s.

**Instrument.** `evaluations/rehearsal-fixes-3172/trap_sweep.py`: 20 poses
sampled around the trap (seed 3172, +/-3 cm, +/-5 deg), each truly trapped
(truth gap >= 1.1 cm, the rehearsal's lowest, and < 180 deg of swing), in
`home_first_floor_stairs`, the real `ExploreAgent` or `ReturnTrip`, the real
`SafetyController`, drop guard on, `MockRobot(exact_verbs=True)`, on simulated
time. Arms: `off` (the change's constants zeroed), `on`.
