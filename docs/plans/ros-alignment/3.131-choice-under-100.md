# 3.131 F-large's whole goal choice <= 100 ms p90 on the Jetson under the full stack (after 3.130)

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.131; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** `explore_goal_policy` defaults to `rooms` (3.125, the user's call
2026-10-31), and F-large's whole choice on the Jetson at 15 W under the full
stack is 132.9 ms p90 against 3.96 criterion 6's 100 ms (3.125.B: map work
98.6, room facts 21.8, ten facts 14.8, network 1.9). The user (2026-10-31)
asked for the map work made fast enough that the whole choice meets the bar,
with exactly today's answers. 3.130 (another session, open) is rewriting
`brain/frontier.py`'s map work with a relative bar (0.70x `dev`'s, quiet
board) and reports the whole choice without a bar. This section builds on it:
the parts of the choice outside `brain/frontier.py`, then the absolute bar on
the board once 3.130 is merged.

**Profile (desktop, quiet, 3.96's benchmark: six live 0.05 m maps, 120
poses, `time_choice.choice`).** Whole choice p50 43.1 / p90 51.8 ms (the
board ran 2.4x slower in 3.125.B). Under cProfile, `np.asarray` is 13% of
the choice: the map's cells arrive as a Python list (~100-160k ints) and are
converted three times a choice outside `frontier.py` -- once by
`goal_context()` (2.8 ms) and twice by `room_features()`'s `coarse()` (5.5 ms,
once for the dtype and once for the data) -- on top of `frontier.py`'s own
`_grid()` (1.5 ms, 3.130's file). The three are the same array: explore
builds `goal_context(m, seen)` first in `_pool()` and calls
`goal_policy.extend()` -> `room_features()` -> `coarse()` on the same map
object later in the same choice.

**Caching per `map_version`: not adopted.** It cannot be credited honestly on
the benchmark (its 20 poses a map would hit a per-map cache 19 times in 20,
where a live choice, made at a goal's end while the map changes ~0.6/s
(3.124), mostly sees a new version); whether live choices repeat a version
is reported from criterion 3's run, not assumed.

**The change (written before building).**

* `goal_context()` keeps the int array it already builds (`ctx["cells"]`).
* `coarse(m, cells=None)` converts the list once, not twice; given `cells`
  (that map's array) it converts nothing.
* `room_features(..., ctx=None)` and `RoomGoalPolicy.extend(..., ctx=None)`
  pass a choice's context down; a context built from another map object is
  refused, as `features()` refuses one.
* `brain/explore.py` `_pool()` passes its `ctx` to `extend()`.
* The board timing mirrors explore: a copy of `jetson_time.py`'s F-large
  choice and parts that passes `ctx` as explore now does
  (`evaluations/choice-3131/`), the same maps, poses and seed.

Out of scope: `brain/frontier.py` (3.130's), explore's `seen_cells`
comprehension, `segment()` (3.118's frozen answers), `lidar_gain` (not in
F-large's choice).

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. **Exactly the old answers.** `dev`'s `coarse()` and `room_features()`
   frozen as `tests/room_features_reference.py`; 0 differences (the whole
   coarse map dict; every room feature row, compared with `==`, in order)
   between the new functions with and without a context and the frozen copy,
   on: 3.96's benchmark (six maps x 20 poses, each pose's trail), every map
   explore sees in the 4 sim houses and 20 ProcTHOR houses of
   `test_frontier_fast_sweep.py`'s missions, and 200 random grids (sizes,
   resolutions 0.05/0.10/0.30 m, odd cell values). Plus: the `rooms`
   policy's whole pool features from `_pool()` equal the frozen path's on
   every choice of those missions.
2. **On the board** (after 3.130 merges, rebased on it): 3.125.B's method
   (`board_time.sh`'s two phases, the board venv, board lock held,
   `headroom_identity.sh RUNTIME=trt` with VE's engines and
   `vision-picar-ros:latest`), timing this section's explore-mirroring copy:
   **map work p90 <= 100 ms and F-large's whole choice p90 <= 100 ms under
   the full stack.** Valid only if the bench ends inside the window and the
   window has 0 late wheel ticks, nav2 all goals succeeded and 0 OOM; an
   invalid run is run again, not scored. Quiet numbers reported, no bar.
3. **Live:** one `tests/demo_explore.py` explore mission, `home_first_floor`,
   `rooms` policy, ROS stack (`vision-picar-ros:latest`), at desktop load
   below ~4: ends `found` with 0 contacts by its ground-truth rule. Reported:
   how many of its choices saw the previous choice's map version.
4. **`ready` passes**, and the sweeps that pin explore and goal-policy
   numbers pass unchanged.
