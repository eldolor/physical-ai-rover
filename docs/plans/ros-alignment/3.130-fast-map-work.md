# 3.130 Explore's map work in numpy: reach, frontiers and view gaps with exactly the old answers

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.130; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** After 3.109, 3.118 and 3.120, explore's goal choice on the
Jetson (15 W, quiet) is 123 ms p90 for F-large, and the largest part is
the map work, ~88 ms: `reach()`, `find_frontiers()` and `find_view_gaps()`
in `brain/frontier.py` (3.103's `jetson_time.py` split; room facts 20 ms,
the ten facts 14, `lidar_gain` 13, the network 1.5). The user's go
(2026-10-31) to look at it, after the proposal below.

Profiled on the desktop (quiet, 3.77's six live SLAM maps, 20 poses each,
`fc.LEAVE`), p50: `reach()` ~12 ms, `find_frontiers` ~10 ms,
`find_view_gaps` ~5 ms. Two findings:

* `reach()` is already numpy: a level-at-a-time BFS, ~330 levels a call,
  whose fixed cost per level (a `np.unique` sort among them) is the time.
  There is no per-cell Python loop. Taking the sort out (a stamp array and
  `np.minimum.at`, the first occurrence kept) gave the same order and
  distances, 11.8 -> 9.7 ms. A larger cut would need a compiled loop.
* **The benchmark understates the map work.** `jetson_time.py` (and 3.96's
  `time_choice.py`) pass `seen=set()`, but explore passes the camera's seen
  cells. With a seen set of 12-16k cells (`frontier_compare.seen_set`, 30
  poses), `find_view_gaps` is ~21 ms p50 on the desktop, not 5: the
  largest of the three. Its time is the camera rays per patch
  (`_ray_cells`, `_Sight.sees`), `_ranked`, `_components`' Python
  union-find and the Python loop over `seen`. `find_frontiers`' is
  `_clusters` and `_split` (Python graph walks over frontier cells) and
  `_shortlist`.

**The change (written before building).** `brain/frontier.py` only,
in this order, each step kept only if it gives exactly the old answers:

1. `find_view_gaps`: the unseen mask from `seen` in numpy; rays and
   rankings batched across patches where the old per-patch decision order
   allows it; components labelled without a Python union-find.
2. `find_frontiers`: `_clusters` and `_split` in numpy, keeping the exact
   cluster order, the order of cells in each group and the piece order (they
   decide ties and `first_visit`).
3. `reach()`: the sort taken out of each level (as prototyped).

Out of scope: `brain/explore.py`'s own `seen_cells` comprehension, the
goal facts, room facts, `lidar_gain`, moving the choice off the tick, and
any compiled extension.

**Measurement.** A new `evaluations/fast-map-3130/time_map.py` times the
map work (`reach` + `find_frontiers` + `find_view_gaps`, as
`jetson_time.py`'s `parts()` calls them) at 3.103's poses (the six maps, 20
poses each, `random.Random(fc.SEED)` consumed exactly as `jetson_time.py`
consumes it), in two arms: **empty** (`seen=set()`, comparable with
3.103/3.109's records) and **seen** (a camera-seen set per map from 30
poses, drawn with `random.Random(3130)` so the poses are unchanged). Both
the new `brain/frontier.py` and a frozen copy of `dev`'s at this section's
fork (`tests/frontier_dev.py`) are timed at every pose,
in alternating order, so load reaches both alike. One warm-up pass, then
timed.

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. **Exactly the old answers.** 0 differences against the frozen
   `tests/frontier_reference.py` in `frontier_compare.differences()` (the
   whole `Frontier`/`ViewGap` lists, `reachable`'s dict in order) on:
   `test_frontier_fast.py` and `test_frontier_fast_sweep.py` as they stand
   (the six live maps x 20 poses x two seen sets; every map explore sees in
   4 sim houses and 20 ProcTHOR houses), plus a new comparison of **every**
   `reach`/`find_frontiers`/`find_view_gaps` call made during those 24
   in-process missions against `frontier_dev.py` (not only the first
   15/10), and the six live maps x 20 poses with seen sets from 8, 30 and
   100 poses. 0 differences on every one.
2. **The tests catch a planted bug.** Each of these mutations of the new
   code, applied one at a time, fails at least one fast-tier test: (a) the
   BFS neighbour order changed; (b) a later occurrence kept instead of the
   first in `reach`'s level step; (c) `_clusters`' cluster order reversed;
   (d) `_split`'s piece order or start cell changed; (e) one seen cell
   dropped from the unseen mask; (f) the ray step changed by half a cell;
   (g) a tie in `_ranked` broken the other way; (h) `_components` joining
   4-connected runs only. A mutation that survives is recorded and a test
   added for it before results.
3. **Faster on the board.** `time_map.py` on the Jetson, 15 W, quiet (the
   stack as it stands, nothing benched beside it), under the board lock,
   the board's venv: map work p90 of the new code at most **0.70x**
   `frontier_dev.py`'s in the same run, in **each** arm (empty and seen).
   (The user, 2026-10-31: -30% on both arms.)
4. **Reported, not a bar:** F-large's whole choice p90 by `jetson_time.py`
   on the board, before and after, quiet.
5. **Nothing else moves.** `ready` passes; `test_frontier_fast_sweep.py`
   and the explore and goal-policy sweeps that pin numbers
   (`test_goal_policy_sweep.py`, `test_goal_features_sweep.py`, the
   ProcTHOR explore pin of 99) pass unchanged.
6. **Live.** One `tests/demo_explore.py` mission on the ROS stack
   (`vision-picar-ros:latest`, unchanged here; `PICAR_ROS_NETWORK=host`),
   `home_first_floor`, at desktop load below ~4: ends `found` with 0
   contacts by its ground-truth rule.
