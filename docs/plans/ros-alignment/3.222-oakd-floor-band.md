# 3.222 OAK-D Lite depth driver and floor band on the Rover

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.222; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the user, 2026-11-26, through the orchestrator) The D500 scans
at its own height and cannot see a sock, a cable, a chair foot or a stair
edge. 3.74 built the floor band (`get_depth_grid()["floor"]`, judged by
`SafetyController.floor_clearance()`) and 3.159 the drop block (`["drop"]`,
`robot/drop_guard.py`), but only the simulator publishes either: no depth
driver exists in `robot/`, so on the car both read `no_floor_band` /
`no_drop_sensor` and the guards are off. Until this lands, autonomy on the
Rover is unsafe. 3.140's L2.7 row waits on it.

**Scope.** A driver for the OAK-D Lite (Intel Movidius 03e7:2485 on the
Jetson's USB) in the robot server, outside ROS (rule 3: a sensor that feeds
a veto), handed to `HardwareRobot` by `robot/factory.py` as the lidar and
camera are. It turns each stereo depth frame into the two blocks the
simulator already publishes, in the same shape, so `robot/safety.py` and
`robot/drop_guard.py` do not change. Not here: explore's
`frame["detections"]` (3.76 E), perception on real frames; a depth cone in
the top-level `zones` (the lidar's swept corridor and the band cover the
corridor, and a second veto over the same space only adds false stops).

| | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|

| Pitch | **0 deg** (level) | cross-checked against the floor plane on the board (C) |
| Lens ahead of the chassis centre | 3.75 in = **9.5 cm** | the sim assumes 6.5 |
| Sideways | **centred** | |
| Pan | fixed to the body (not on the pan-tilt): `pan_deg` 0, always | |

**Blind range, predicted before measuring** (C measures it): the stereo at
400P with extended disparity reads from MinZ ~18-20 cm; a level lens 14 cm
up with a 58 deg vertical field sees the floor from 14 / tan 29 deg =
25.3 cm ahead of the lens. The bumper is 12.65 cm ahead of centre, the lens
9.5, so:
* a 5 cm object: its top enters the view at 16.2 cm, inside MinZ, so MinZ
  binds -- first seen ~18-20 cm from the lens, **~15-17 cm past the bumper**;
* a folded sock (3 cm): ~20 cm from the lens, **~17 cm past the bumper**;
* the floor itself (the drop block's `near_cm`): **~22 cm past the bumper**.

3.74's gap stands: nothing is carried across the blind range, and a creep
after a band stop can drive a low object into it.

## Design

* `robot/oakd_depth.py`. **Geometry** (pure numpy, tested on synthetic
  frames): every depth pixel is put in the body frame by the measured
  mounting -- height above the floor, ahead of the lens, bearing
  clockwise-positive. Per column (96 over 73 deg, the sim's
  `FLOOR_BAND_COLS`):
  * **floor band**: the nearest point standing more than `floor_tol_cm`
    above the floor plane and below the robot's height, at a horizontal
    range from the lens within [min, max] (20-150 cm, the sim's), with the
    bearing it lay at; enough points needed, so one speckle is not a sock.
    No such point and enough valid pixels -> `NO_TARGET`; too few valid
    pixels (a dropout, or a thing inside MinZ) -> `UNUSABLE` (M3).
  * **drop block**: the column's pixel rows walked from the bottom of the
    image upward, i.e. outward along the floor. Floor continues while a row
    reads floor; it ends at a row that reads a thing (`thing`), below the
    floor plane by `drop_cm`, or no return where floor should be (`edge`,
    3.159's two past-edge modes). Floor seen out to `far_cm` -> `NO_TARGET`.
    A column whose first floor row is unseen -> `UNUSABLE`.
* **Device**: depthai 2.x (the user, 2026-11-26: `depthai==2.33.0.0` and
  Luxonis's udev rule on the board), 400P mono pair, extended disparity,
  left-right check, median filter, depth aligned to the rectified right
  camera; one reader thread computes the blocks and the vet reads the last
  ones. The frame's device timestamp (host-synced monotonic) is its age.
  A frame older than `STALE_S` publishes **both blocks with every zone
  unusable, never omitted**: an omitted block would switch the drop guard
  to `no_drop_sensor`; an unusable one keeps it on, and its memory (floor
  never seen is never clear) stops a forward move at the end of remembered
  floor. Unplugged: it reconnects every 2 s.
* `HardwareRobot(depth=...)`: `get_depth_grid()` adds the two blocks to the
  body's grid; `sensor_age_s()` takes the band's age (3.74 B4), and only
  while fresh: a stale camera's blocks are unusable and never compared, so
  its age must not shrink the lidar's clearances. `depth_status()` for
  `/health`, description only (M5).
* `robot/factory.py` `_depth()`: `ROBOT_DEPTH=oakd` (or `hardware.depth`),
  mounting from `hardware.depth_mount` with env overrides; none -> no
  driver, today's answers exactly.

## Criteria (written 2026-11-26, before any code or measurement)

| # | Metric | Bar | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | `[car]` Low objects across the path, each floor type in the house: a 5 cm box and a folded sock (~3 cm), 5 approaches each at 0.2 m/s, gap to the bumper by tape at rest | **0 runs under 18 cm** (3.74 B1/B3's bar, on ground truth) | stability |
| K1c | `[car]` A flat cable, same runs | measured and recorded, **not barred** (3.74 B3: depth is expected to miss it; that is part C's job) | record |
| K2 | `[car]` 10 minutes of driving per floor type with nothing low on it | **0** floor-band vetoes and **0** drop vetoes (`floor_band`, `drop_*` reasons in the robot log) | stability |
| K3 | Progress, paired with K1-K2 | K2: **>= 20 m** of path per 10-minute run; K1: every stop **<= 45 cm** from the bumper (a stop near the band's 150 cm reach is a false-early stop) | progress |
| K4 | Blind range, static, by tape on the board | the nearest distance past the bumper at which the 5 cm box, the sock and bare floor read in the band / drop block, **recorded** against the prediction above | record |
| K5 | Dropout | lens covered, USB unplugged and a stalled pipeline each read **`unusable` within 0.5 s, never `NO_TARGET`**, with both blocks present; unplugged, FORWARD is refused at the end of remembered floor (`drop_*`); reconnects by itself | stability |
| K6 | Jetson cost, with `picar-stack` running | robot server CPU (one-core %), GPU % and RSS with and without the driver, **CPU and GPU in separate rows** (P24); bars: **0 late wheel-loop ticks** over 10 minutes, RSS **+<= 250 MB**, CPU **+<= 25% of one core** | stability |
| K7 | Age and mode | 400P with extended disparity (3.74 B4); band age at the vet **p95 <= 150 ms**; counted in `_aged()` | stability |
| K8 | In code | synthetic frames: a 5 cm box at 30-140 cm reads within **+/-3 cm** of truth at its bearing; bare floor reads `NO_TARGET`; zero-depth columns `UNUSABLE`; a drop reads `edge` within **+/-5 cm**; a robot with no depth driver gives today's grid exactly; the contract suite green | stability |

K1-K2 and K5 are the stability half, K3 the progress half: a band that
vetoes everywhere passes K1 and fails K2-K3. A failed criterion is recorded
as failed. K1-K3 need the user's go and presence (motion); K4-K5 need the
user to place objects and pull the cable, no motion.

### Amendment 1 (2026-11-26, step B, before any measurement)

1. **K7's resolution is 480P, not 400P.** The OV7251 pair is 640x480; 400P
   crops the vertical field to about 50 deg, which moves the first floor
   seen from 25.3 to ~30 cm ahead of the lens (the blind range grows 5 cm).
   Extended disparity stays (3.74 B4's point: MinZ). C confirms the field
   from the calibrated intrinsics; if 480P costs past K6's bars, 400P is
   measured and the blind range re-recorded.
2. **The floor tolerance is a guess until K2:** 1.5 cm, widened 1 cm per
   metre of range (a 3 cm sock reads to ~1.5 m; a 1 cm cable never does).
   K2's false vetoes decide it; a change is written here before the run it
   affects.
