# 3.206 Qwen's direction answer with the target in view, taught by a frontier model on the frames the local tiers are silent on

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.206; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** The user, 2026-11-21: "Steering or steering with the target in
view: suggest a plan to use a frontier model to fine tune Qwen", then "Add
it to the plan."

**What is known (3.80.I, the 60 real in-view frames S1; recorded, not
re-tested here):**

| Tier | Right, of 60 | Answered | Right when answering |
|---|---|---|---|
| GPT-6 Astra, `target_direction` | 56 (93%) | 60 | 93% |
| Opus 5.5, `target_direction` | 49 (82%) | 49 | 100% (`not_visible` on 11) |
| Opus 4.5, `target_direction` | 46 (77%) | -- | -- |
| YOLOE + CLIP, its box's third | 45 (75%) | 49 | 92% |
| Qwen VE, the identity box's third | 42 (70%) | 46 | 91% |
| The local order: perception, then Qwen's box (not a pre-stated arm) | 51 (85%) | 55 | 93% |
| Qwen VE, the direction word | 28 (47%) | 60 | FORWARD on every frame |

* **The local tiers are right when they answer; their misses are mostly
  silence.** The local order is silent on 5 of 60 and wrong on 4. The best
  teacher is 5 frames ahead.
* **Qwen's direction word is broken, not weak:** FORWARD on all 142 frames
  asked (3.80.F, 3.80.I), under VE and under the full engine alike.
* **Out of view there is nothing to distil.** No model, local or cloud,
  gave a usable search direction (I2 failed by every tier; Astra 29%, under
  the majority class). Explore chooses where to look (3.80.G). This section
  is steering with the target in view only.
* **3.92's rule on a paid steering replay came out close on phone frames**
  (perception silent on 20.4% of in-view frames, line 20%; VE's box covers
  56% of those, line 50%: not proposed) and says to re-read it on the
  Rover's walks. This section does not replay walks through the cloud to
  steer them; it buys labels once, for training.
* **A better steerer makes the look-alike worse unless identity gates it:**
  3.91 B6's replay steered toward the taupe bag on 54 of 54 frames after
  the local "no".

**What this section is.** The frontier model labels the direction (left /
centre / right by thirds, 3.80.I's truth) of in-view frames. Qwen's
direction answer is fine-tuned on those labels, so it can serve as the
last fallback of the local order, on the frames where perception and
Qwen's own box are silent. The teacher is never called at run time.

**Why a teacher here when 3.188 forbids one.** For identity the cloud is
less precise than Qwen (3.80.Q: 56-68% against 97%), so its answers would
teach mistakes. For direction with the target in view the cloud is ahead
(93% against the local order's 85% and the word's 47%), and the truth is
geometric, so a person can audit a label in seconds.

## Design (A may amend before any paid call or training)

* **Test set first, by a person, never the teacher.** S1's 60 frames grow
  to about 200 in-view frames, drawn as S1 was (uniformly from the 535
  labelled in-view frames of 3.92's pinned 21 walks, never from a tier's
  detections), labelled with the thirds drawn (`make_sets.py`'s method).
  Pinned in a test. These frames never enter training.
* **Teachers.** Astra and Opus 5.5 are measured. Claude Fable 5.2 is not;
  it joins B only after a real `converse` call with a frame confirms its id
  and region (CLAUDE.md section 6). Each is asked 3.80.I's
  `target_direction` question, each frame also mirrored.
* **A label is kept only when the teacher is sure:** the mirrored answer
  is the original with left and right swapped, and, where two teachers
  ran, both agree. `not_visible` is never a label. What is dropped is
  counted.
* **Audit before training:** a person checks 50 kept labels drawn at
  random against the frame with the thirds drawn.
* **Training.** LoRA on the language model, vision encoder frozen, as
  3.188: the direction question exactly as `/direction` asks it, the
  answer one word, the score the probability over the three words. Every
  training frame also mirrored with its label swapped: a model that says
  one word both ways cannot fit the pairs.
* **One engine.** The board runs one Qwen. Direction and identity are
  trained as one adapter with 3.188's identity data, on 3.188.B's host and
  script; identity is judged by 3.188's bars, unchanged. If 3.188.B has
  not run, this section's D waits on it.
* **Folds by object, as 3.188** (`split.json`'s six): each object's
  frames are judged by a model that never saw that object.
* **Where it acts.** Behind a config key, off by default: the local
  order's last fallback, after perception's box and Qwen's box, and only
  after an identity yes (never toward a candidate the local tier refused).
  No new movement path; every move still goes through the safety check.

## Criteria (written 2026-11-21, before any labelling, call or training; CLAUDE.md section 7)

**A, free:**

1. **The headroom, measured:** on the ~200-frame test set, the local
   order's share right, share silent and share wrong-side, and the stock
   direction word's. **If the local order is already >= 90% right, the
   section stops here** and records why.

**B and C, paid (a 2-call pilot reports the real figure first; B stops at
$5, B and C together at $30, each reported before more is asked):**

2. **A teacher worth copying:** on S1 (human truth), the kept-label rule
   leaves a teacher or pair with **>= 95% right on what it keeps** and
   **>= 70% of frames kept**.
3. **The audit:** >= 48 of the 50 audited labels right, or D does not
   start.

**D, on the GPU host, each object judged by its fold's model on the test
set's frames of that object, against the stock model in the same runtime:**

4. **Progress, the word alone:** direction right on **>= 80%** of the test
   set (3.80.I's I1; stock 47% on S1).
5. **Progress, where it is used:** the local order with the new answer as
   its last fallback right on **>= 90%**, and above A's figure by more
   than the test set's 95% interval half-width (stated in A).
6. **Stability, the wrong side:** the local order's wrong-side answers
   **<= A's count**. A silent frame that becomes a wrong side is a loss.
7. **Mirror consistency >= 90%** on the test set, with both words used
   (a fixed word passes vacuously and counts as failed).
8. **No object worse:** on every held-out object, the local order's right
   count >= A's for that object.
9. **Identity untouched:** 3.188's criteria 1-5 on the joint adapter.
10. **Cost reported:** teacher dollars per kept label and GPU dollars,
    measured.

**E, the board and the real path:**

11. The rebuilt engine: direction p90 <= 2.0 s (3.80.F's K3), 3.188.C's
    memory and decision bars, and >= 98% agreement with its own BF16 model
    on the test set's direction answers.
12. Off is off: with the key off, the simulator's tiered sweep and 3.91
    B6's four replays are unchanged (0 differences), pinned in a test.
13. On the Rover: 3.92's criterion 5 re-read on its walks first; then one
    closed-loop find with the key on and one with it off on the same
    layout: found both times, 0 contacts, and no steering toward a
    candidate the local tier refused. A replay is open loop and cannot
    stand in for this.

A failed criterion is recorded as failed, not re-scoped.

**Why now.** 3.214 recorded that VE's identity question is a grounding
question (`tools/jetson/bench_identity_llama.py` `PROMPT`; on the board

### What changes

* **The trained answer is the box.** The student is asked VE's grounding
  question and trained to reply with the box, or `[]` when the target is
  not visible. `/direction`'s word is not trained and not used. The box is
  written in the student's own format; the teacher's coordinates (Qwen3-VL
  writes 0-1000 relative ones, 3.214 amendment 2) are converted, and the
  conversion is pinned by a test before C labels anything. Mirroring stays:
  every training frame also mirrored, its box mirrored with it.
* **Who labels what.** The teacher draws the box on training frames that
  **people labelled in view**, and only those. Frames people labelled not
  in view are trained as `[]` on the people's label; the teacher is never
  asked about them and never supplies a "no" or a "yes". An in-view frame
  where the teacher draws no box, or whose mirrored box does not land on
  the mirror of the original (IoU under 0.5), is dropped from training and
  counted; it is never trained as `[]`.
* **Where it acts.** Nothing is added to the local order (perception's
  box, then Qwen's box). The fine-tuned engine sits behind a config key
  naming the engine, default the stock one, as 3.188.D. The earlier "last
  fallback, only after an identity yes" is moot: a box is the yes.
* **The risk moves to identity.** A model that draws more boxes says yes
  more often. So 3.188's identity bars judge the same adapter (criterion
  9, unchanged), and D does not ship on steering's numbers alone.
* **The teacher is Qwen3-VL-235B on Bedrock** (`qwen.qwen3-vl-235b-a22b`,
  us-east-2; B1's runner), **if step B2 below passes.** B's paid cloud
  arms (Astra, Opus 5.5, Fable 5.2) are dropped: they answer a direction,
  not a box. The 72B and Florence-2 stay recorded as measured; neither is
  the teacher unless B2 fails and the user says so.
* **Border frames.** A frame is `border` when the target's centre, by the
  person's label, lies within 5% of the frame's width of a line between
  thirds (`labels-s1.json`'s flag, set at labelling, before any model is
  scored). Criteria 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7 are judged on frames **not** flagged;
  border frames are reported apart, with either adjacent third counted
  right. A person re-checks `basket-20260913-115703/frame-0184`, whose
  label disagrees with its own centre; only a person changes a label.
* **B0 and B1 stay FAILED as recorded.** Their arms were judged by the

  already known to be right on every frame it keeps, so S1 cannot test the
