# 3.165 Inventory landmark points bounded

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.165; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.76 G2 (latent, the user's call): every placed observation adds
a point to its landmark (`Landmark.points`), for the whole mission, and
every detection scans every point (`near()`, under the runner's lock); the
position (`x`, `y`) re-sums every point each time it is read, which
`_misses()` does for every landmark on every frame. G2 measured 2.2 ms a
frame at frame 100, 23 ms at 2,400 and 73.5 ms at 4,800 (10 detections a
frame, laptop). It was unreachable while real frames carried no
detections; 3.157 showed perception gives explore sightings on real
frames, which was its trigger (the user, 2026-11-07: "Yes").

## 3.165.A: the design and the criteria (written before any code)

**Design.**

* **A member is a weighted point** `[x, y, label, weight]`. An observation
  within `FOLD_M` (0.05 m) of a member of its own label is folded into it:
  the member moves to the weighted centroid and its weight grows by one.
  No new member.
* **At most `MAX_POINTS` (32) members a landmark.** Past it, the two
  closest members of one label are folded the same way (and if no two
  share a label, the two closest, under the heavier one's label). This
  keeps the spread of members, not just the newest: single linkage
  (`near()`) needs the landmark's extent, which a "newest only" cut would
  lose.
* **The fold keeps the mean.** A weighted centroid conserves
  Σ weight·position, so the position, kept as running sums, is the mean of
  every observation the landmark ever took, as before. The label fallback
  (no votes) counts members by weight. The report's `points` field stays
  the number of observations (the total weight), so the report's fields
  mean what they did.
* **Viewpoints are unchanged.** `_hit_pose` and `_miss_pose` are already
  one each, and 3.60's rule that a merge keeps the newest is untouched.
  `_looks` (frame -> vote) stays exact: it grows by one per counted look (a
  new viewpoint), not per frame, and 3.60's union-by-frame needs it. If
  criterion 2 shows label reads costing time, the votes are kept as a
  running count beside `_looks`, with the same answers.
* **Only linkage can move:** a folded observation's own position is gone,
  so `near()` reads the member up to `FOLD_M` (or, at the cap, the folded
  pair's spread) from where the observation was. Criterion 4 measures
  whether that changes any outcome.

**Criteria.**

1. **The bound holds.** After every frame of criterion 2's benchmark and of
   criterion 4's sweep, no landmark holds more than `MAX_POINTS` members,
   and each landmark's position equals the mean of every observation it
   took, within 1e-9 m. The mean comes from a recorder outside the
   inventory: each placed point, followed through merges by landmark id.
2. **The cost is flat over a long mission.** A benchmark,
   `evaluations/inventory-3165/long_mission.py`:
   * 4,800 frames x 10 detections, straight into `Inventory.observe()`
     (G2's scale);
   * 20 objects round a room, the robot circling slowly with a 400-frame
     stop every 1,200 frames, 3 deg bearing noise, and a scan built from
     the objects.

   The median `observe()` time over frames 4,701-4,800 is at most 1.5x the
   median over frames 101-200, in one run. `dev`'s curve on the same
   benchmark is recorded beside it.
3. **Memory over a long mission.** The same benchmark: the inventory's
   allocated size (`tracemalloc`, the Inventory object graph) at frame
   4,800 is at most 25% of `dev`'s at frame 4,800. The curve is recorded at
   frames 1,200, 2,400, 3,600 and 4,800.
4. **Progress unchanged on the seeded sweep.** This is
   `python -m tests.inventory_sweep --seed 3463` (3.60's sweep: 20 frontier
   missions in `complex_house`, detector errors on), run on `dev` (this
   branch's base) and after. Each of these is within 0.5 points of `dev`'s:
   recall, small recall, precision of reported and precision of all.
   Duplicates are within 0.01, placement median and p90 within 0.01 m, and
   every 3.60 bar is still met. `hits_over_frames` stays 0. The landmark
   reports of the 20 missions are compared field by field (label, x, y to
   0.001 m, belief, hits, misses), and the number that differ is recorded.

   The sweep's look counter (`_LookCounter`, test only) tagged points by
   identity, which a fold breaks. It is changed to tag members by every
   frame in which their weight grew. A folded pair keeps only the
   survivor's frames, so this undercounts frames and can only report MORE
   violations: a 0 stays a real 0.
5. **3.50.D5's carry is unchanged.** `tests/test_map_frame_carry.py`,
   `tests/test_inventory.py`, `tests/test_inventory_mission.py`,
   `tests/test_inventory_label.py` and `tests/test_review_376g.py` pass
   with no assertion edited. A carried landmark is still one member at its
   saved position, with its weight-1 point.
6. **Red first.** A test that holds a landmark for 500 looks from one spot
   asserts at most `MAX_POINTS` members, and fails on `dev`.

**Not here:** the number of landmarks (bounded by the house's objects), and
`_looks` per landmark (one per new viewpoint, kept exact; see Design).
