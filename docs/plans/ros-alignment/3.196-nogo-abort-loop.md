# 3.196 Explore gives up a goal that keeps aborting beside a no-go zone

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.196; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.192.B's F10 failed under the shipped `rooms` chooser: at
`procthor:val:15` s0, 5 explore goals 0.15-0.26 m outside zone `z20` ended
`aborted` (bar: at most 3 per mission per zone), and `dev` before 3.192
already had 4 there. The mission still found its target. 3.192 left "giving
up a frontier once its goals abort next to a zone" to a later step; this is
that step (the orchestrator, on the user's delegation, 2026-11-20).

**Why the navigator aborts.** The true map round the zone (0.3 m cells; `#`
wall, `o` object, `Z` a cell whose centre is in `z20`, `G` goal 1, `R` the
robot):

```
44 #######...####      the only door, x 51-53, opens straight onto the zone
43 oo.....ZZZ..##
42 oo.o...ZZZG.##
41 ...oo..ZZZ..##
```

The zone fills the room's only door. The stand-in navigator (`ZoneNav`,
4-connected, lethal where a cell's centre is in a zone) has no path, and
nav2 has none either: the gap between the zone's corner (16.199, 13.14) and
the wall cell at (16.2-16.5, 13.2-13.5) is about 0.06 m, for a robot 0.24 m
wide. **The brain's reach finds one**: `brain/frontier.py`'s `reach` walks
8-connected and steps diagonally from the door cell (53, 44) to (54, 43)
between the wall (54, 44) and the masked zone cell (53, 43) -- a corner
both of whose sides are blocked, a zero-width gap. The same squeeze on the
door's west side, (51, 44) to (50, 43). So explore keeps choosing frontier
goals behind the zone that no navigator can reach.

**Root cause, to the line:** `brain/explore.py` `_zone_map` masks a zone's
cells (grown by the half-width) but leaves the diagonal step past a masked
cell's corner open, and `reach`'s diagonal step needs only its two end cells
free. Without zones the same corner rule is old and harmless here: a
house's walls in the simulator are cell-aligned, and a zone is the only
thing that puts a blocked cell diagonally across a door's corner.

**Why the retry book did not catch it sooner.** Each abort comes back at
once with the robot unmoved, so `_goal_ended` takes it for a wedge (the
never-left rule, 3.38/3.85): the first failure at a place is forgiven, and a
place counts as the same only within `RETRY_RADIUS_M` (0.6 m, strict `<` in
`again`). Goals 1-3 are three places 0.6-0.9 m apart round the zone, so 1, 2
and 3 were each forgiven; only 4 and 7 (repeats) were charged. 3.175.E's
result applies: a retry at a structurally unreachable place repeats the
failure (there, a point 0.3 m over met it again). So the retry book is not
the place to fix this; the reach is.

## Design (step A, written before any build or run)

**D1. The zone's diagonal pinch is closed.** After masking (`_zone_map`),
any 2 x 2 block whose two diagonal cells are free while its other two are
both not free, at least one of them a zone cell, has both free cells set to
the zone value too, repeated until none is left (a short fixed loop). No
diagonal step then passes a zone's corner through a gap with no width.
Only explore's masked map changes; with no zones `_zone_map` is not called
and nothing changes. `brain/frontier.py`, the frozen references,
`robot/`, `world/` beyond a new helper in `world/nogo.py`, and
`service/slam/` get no diff.

*Amendment 1 (2026-11-20, before any kept sweep row; a first sweep of the
rooms finds on D1 as written was stopped and discarded).* That sweep lost
`procthor:val:9` s0 (`out_of_budget`; `dev` `found`). The trace: D1 closed a
"pinch" at a door whose cells below lie in the zone's 0.12 m grown band but
not in the zone itself. The navigator passes such cells (`ZoneNav` is lethal
where a cell's centre is in the zone; nav2's keepout is lethal to the
robot's centre only), so that gap is open, and closing it changed the goal
choice; the mission then fell into a frontier/approach cycle (56 rounds of
the same three goals, 4.88 m from any zone). **D1 now counts a side as
closed only when it is a wall or a cell whose centre lies in a zone itself,
at least one of the two the latter**; one pass, since a closed cell is not a
zone cell. val:15's door (zone cells proper beside the walls) is still
closed; val:9 is again `found` at 328.0 s, as on `dev`. The bars are
unchanged.

**D2. An abort beside a zone is the place's failure, not a wedge.** A goal
whose point lies within `NEAR_ZONE_M` (0.5 m, F10's distance) of a zone
and that ends `aborted` or `rejected` with the robot unmoved is charged to
its place at once (the retry book's `fail`), not forgiven as the first
wedge. A backstop for the car, where nav2 can refuse a goal beside a zone
for reasons D1 does not see (inflation, the bridge's rasterisation, 3.192
F9). The escape still runs, as for any never-left end. Goals farther from
every zone, and every goal without zones, keep the old rule.
