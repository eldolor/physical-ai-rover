# 3.235 Frontier gain: does a frontier's predicted gain hold on the Rover's real maps?

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.235; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** The user, 2026-11-28, asked whether the rover predicts what will
happen next in frontier exploration, then asked for this section, to wait
for the Rover's data. The learned goal choosers are recorded as "closed
until the Rover's data" (`docs/guides/MERGED-STATE.md`, Explore and goal
choice). This section names what that data must answer.

**What is already known (do not repeat it in sim).**
* 3.101: `lidar_gain` (the unknown cells a raycast from the goal can see,
  within 3 m) is the one new fact that carried weight. Without it the
  clone's agreement drops 14 points. 3.110's ladder found one step, at
  `lidar_gain` (+3.78 points).
* 3.103 already ran 3.101's named follow-up, F-large's 17 facts plus
  `lidar_gain`. It FAILED criterion 1 (-0.041 against F-large). F-pool
  (+0.043) FAILED too, and every arm costs 316-341 ms p90 on the Jetson
  against a 100 ms bar.
* 3.111, 3.114, 3.115, 3.117 and 3.119: copying the oracle better does not
  search better, and learned choosers sit about 0.22 under the oracle.
* `lidar_gain` lives only in `evaluations/`, never in `brain/`.

**The question only real data answers.** Every number above comes from
the simulator's clean 0.30 m maps. A real map has clutter, glass and mirrors,
specks of unknown, and lidar noise, all of which could make a raycast gain
promise floor that is never revealed. Before anyone trains a chooser on
real data again, measure the **prediction** itself: does `lidar_gain` at a
choice predict the floor actually revealed by going there?

**Maps are a home's floor plan** (section 6): the snapshots in A stay on
the board or the desktop. They are never committed or uploaded without the
user's word. Only derived numbers land in `evaluations/`.

## Criteria (written before measuring; CLAUDE.md section 7)

**A. Record real choices** (records)
* **A1.** At each goal explore sends on the Rover: the candidate pool, each
  candidate's `lidar_gain` (3.103's numpy computation, unchanged), the
  pick, and the map right then. The map again when that goal ends.
  Off by default, behind a key.
* **A2.** At least **5 surveys on the Rover in the user's house, 150+ goal
  choices**, the user's word on where the snapshots are kept.

**B. Predicted against realised gain** (offline; the same pipeline on sim
and real)
* Realised gain is the cells unknown at the choice that are known when the
  goal ends.
* **B1, sim reference.** The same measure on 3.103's selection houses under
  the rule chooser. The Spearman rank correlation, within a choice, of
  predicted against realised gain. Recorded, not barred: it is the
  reference B2 is judged against.
* **B2, the real map.** The same correlation on A's choices:
  **>= 0.5**, and no more than **0.15 below B1's**. Both are needed to
  call the prediction transferable.
* **B3, reported.** Where the real prediction is wrong most often (by the
  candidate's distance, glass or mirror cells if known, map speckle), as
  input for a fix.

**C. Only if B2 is met, and the user decides whether it starts.** A chooser
comparison on real maps (replayed snapshots, or live surveys). Its bars are
written in an amendment before it runs, on 3.101's advice: paired
per-house goals or finds, which the noise can resolve, not whole-mission
reward.

If B2 FAILS, the record is the answer: a raycast gain does not transfer,
and the chooser line stays closed until a fix for B3's causes exists.
