# 3.137 The battery ladder: low, reserve, a clean power-off; and running on the charger

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.137; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** The user (2026-11-01), sizing 3.135's soaks: "think about how long
a jetson will be able to run on batteries. I'm hoping the safety layer
gracefully shuts down the jetson based on battery levels." Today it does not:

* **Built:** 3.70 E4's mission floor. Two distinct board samples under
  `battery_floor_v` (10.5 V; M2.7's `BATTERY_SAMPLE_S`) end the mission
  `out_of_budget`, and the robot stops.
* **Not built:** refusing motion at a lower level, saving state, or powering
  the Jetson off. The pack's own cutoff would cut the power, protected only
  by the atomic saves (3.135 F14). The Rover's cells (3x Molicel P26A) are
  unprotected.
* `HARDWARE-BOM.md` section 6 specified this before the chassis was chosen:
  warn at ~10.5 V; stop the motors and start a clean shutdown at ~10.0 V
  under load, filtered so a motor-start sag does not trigger it; an "unplug
  the battery" prompt, because a software shutdown leaves the ESP32 board
  draining the pack. It also names the Jetson's input floor as **9 V**, so a
  power-off much below 10.0 V risks a brownout first under motor load.
  `HARDWARE-READINESS.md` 5.6 lists it as "specified, not implemented"; this
  section builds it.

The user's follow-up (2026-11-01): whether one input can take both the
battery and direct power. The kit's Waveshare UPS Module 3S already feeds the
Jetson from its charger when one is plugged in, and from the cells when not
(`JETSON-BOM.md` 9.3), but whether it carries the whole stack on the charger
without a dip at plug and unplug is unmeasured. That is step D. If it passes,
the long soaks and any future dock run on the charger, and the ladder matters
only off it. If a separate Jetson pack is ever fitted (9.5), both supplies
reach the Jetson only through an ideal-diode OR module (>= 5 A, 9-20 V,
MOSFET, not Schottky) and are never joined directly.

**Runtime, estimated (to be measured: 3.136 P14 on the board, step E on the
Rover).** Pack: 3S P26A, 10.8 V x 2.6 Ah = ~28 Wh; ~20 Wh usable to 10.5 V
after the UPS's losses. Estimated draw: ~16 W parked with the full stack,
~20 W searching. That is **~1.0-1.25 h a charge** (range 0.8-1.5 h), ~3-4
missions at the 1,200 s limit; with 9.5's separate Jetson pack (~44 Wh
usable) ~2.5-3 h. One power-on on battery is therefore ~1.5 h at most,
unless the robot runs on its charger.

## The ladder (written before building)

The robot server decides (rule 4: it owns the board's reading and safety);
the brain is told and obeys, never the judge. Levels are on pack volts
from the board's `v` (`T:1001`), each crossed only by **two distinct board
samples** under it (M2.7's rule, extended), read **with the wheels at rest
where possible**: at Reserve and Power-off the robot server stops the wheels
and reads again before acting, so a sag under motor load cannot trigger a
step on its own.

| Level | Volts (placeholders until step E) | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Low | 10.5 (`battery_floor_v`, exists) | The mission ends `out_of_budget` (built). New: no autonomous mission may start below it (`POST /mission/start` refused, the reason named); the twin shows "Battery low". |
| Reserve | 10.2 (3.4 V a cell) | The robot server refuses all autonomous motion (brain verbs, wheels, `brain`- and `ros`-driven goals) with a reason `battery_reserve`. A person may still drive by D-pad at reduced speed, to bring it to the charger (the user's call; recommended). The brain, the ROS bridge (the map: 3.50.D2's save) and the inventory save their state. The twin shows "Battery reserve: shutting down in N s". |
| Power-off | 10.0 under load (`HARDWARE-BOM.md` 6), or Reserve held for `battery_reserve_max_s` (proposed 120 s) | A clean power-off in order: the brain's mission ends, the robot server stops the wheels and stops sending, the ROS container stops, the local VLM stops, the services flush, then `systemctl poweroff`. With the host silent, the board's 1.5 s heartbeat holds the wheels at 0. Nobody may override it. The twin's last word is "Powered off: unplug the battery". |

* **Permission to power off.** The robot server does not run as root. A
  polkit rule on the board lets only the stack's user run `systemctl
  poweroff` (nothing else). Installing it needs `sudo` on the board: approved by the user
  (2026-11-01), installed by `install_services.sh` in step C.
* **On the charger** (step D's result): if the UPS reports nothing to the
  Jetson, the board's `v` reads the charger's 12.6 V and the ladder never
  fires; that is correct.
* **Blind spot, recorded:** with the robot server dead, nothing reads the
  pack, and the hard cutoff is the backstop (3.135 F14's atomic saves). Step
  D checks whether the UPS exposes anything to the Jetson directly.
* **In the sim:** the fake board's drain (`SIM_BATTERY_V`, `drain_v_per_s`,
  3.70 E4) drives the ladder; `systemctl poweroff` is replaced by a stub
  that records the call (a `BATTERY_POWEROFF_CMD` set to a harmless command;
  the default on the board is the real one).

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7; confirmed by the user 2026-11-01, with the levels as placeholders until step E and a person's reduced-speed D-pad allowed below Reserve):**

1. **The ladder in the sim** (fake board draining 0.02 V/s from 11.0 V,
   tiered explore missions, 20 runs, in process and through the live local
   stack once):
   * (a) each level fires within 2 board samples of its crossing and never
     on one low sample; a 1 s sag of 1.0 V under motor start, 20 times, fires
     nothing (the planted-sag test);
   * (b) Reserve: 0 autonomous motion after it (ground truth: true pose
     moves <= 2 cm after the refusal); a person's D-pad moves at reduced
     speed; the map, inventory and logs saved and parseable;
   * (c) Power-off: the stub called exactly once per run, after the brain,
     ROS and VLM are down and the wheels at 0; the order logged;
   * (d) progress: a mission started at 11.0 V that finds before Low ends
     `found` as it would without the ladder (0 of 20 differ).
2. **No regression:** with the pack never under Low, 0 differences in the
   explore and tiered sweeps' outcomes; `ready` passes.
3. **On the Jetson, once** (from the desktop, under `board_lock`; the polkit
   rule installed with the user's yes): the stack in sim mode, the fake
   board draining, the real `systemctl poweroff`. The board powers off
   within 60 s of the Power-off crossing; after the next boot, every saved
   file parses, `picar-stack` comes up and a mission runs.
4. **The twin** shows Low, Reserve (with the countdown) and "Powered off:
   unplug the battery", one UI test each at 390 px, red first, and a
   phone-size screenshot.
