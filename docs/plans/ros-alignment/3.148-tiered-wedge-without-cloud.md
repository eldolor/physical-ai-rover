# 3.148 A tiered mission with no cloud wedges at a jamb and ends blocked (3.135.B F11, F12)

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.148; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.135.B's rows F11a, F11b and F12a FAILED on the Jetson
(2026-11-04, 15 W, sim mode, ground truth). In 6 of their 15 runs the
tiered mission ended `blocked`, which is not an end any of their bars
allows. 3.135 criterion 3 makes it its own section. The fix and its criteria
are this section's to write.

* *The runs:* 3.135 amendment 5's cloud stack. That is a tiered mission for
  the red backpack in `scaled_house`, with no ROS and no local tier
  (`LOCAL_VLM_URL` empty), and `SIM_ARRIVAL_CONFIRM=cloud`. Each row has a
  different cloud:
  * F11a: refused from the start;
  * F11b: the stand-in answers one `/navigate`, then is killed;
  * F12a: the cloud hangs (`hang_cloud.py`).
* *The numbers:*
  * F11a: 1 of 5 runs ended `blocked`;
  * F11b: 3 of 5;
  * F12a: 2 of 5.
  * The other 9 runs ended `arrived_unconfirmed` at 95-111 s, as the bars
    want, after ~460 cm of path.
  * Every `blocked` run ended with "FORWARD refused 5 times in a row by the
    safety layer, 3 times over". That happened at one of two places:
    * 4 runs at (2.97, 1.72) after ~227 cm, at 178-184 s;
    * 2 runs at (4.48, 1.66) after ~376 cm, at 152-154 s.
  * In both places the robot faced east (heading -0.05 to +0.03 rad) at a
    true gap of 19.9-21.9 cm. The refusals were real `safety_distance`
    vetoes off the scan footprint, not 3.144's stale bundle.
* *What held, in every run:*
  * never two paid calls open at once;
  * F12a's arrival parked in 3.8-15.6 s (bar 30 s);
  * F11b's late confirmation reached a final state;
  * 0 contacts, 0 late ticks, 0 OOM, nothing driven after the end;
  * the board was left as found.
  * F11c (`vision`, refused) was met 5/5.
* *Cause, not yet shown:*
  * With no tier able to steer (the cloud down, no local VLM, no nav2),
    the tiered policy drives FORWARD into the same two spots and the stuck
    breaker ends the mission. `brain/tiered.py`'s `STEER_BAND_DEG` note
    records an earlier form of this: drift into a door jamb.
  * The desktop's single shipped run of each row passed (3.135
    amendment 5's planted checks), so this is a rate, not a certainty.
  * No fault-free control was run: whether a tiered mission with the cloud
    up wedges at the same spots is this section's first measurement. If it
    does, the fault is not the cause, and the rows' mover is the problem,
    not the cloud's guards.
* *Records:* `evaluations/jetson-fmea-3135/board/rows/F11a-20261104-1029/`,
  `F11b-20261104-1040/`, `F12a-20261104-1056/` (on
  `plan/3.135.B-single-faults` until 3.135.B merges), and BOARD-BENCH's
  fault table.
* *Re-test:* `run.py row F11a|F11b|F12a --runs 5` (board). Risky by
  CLAUDE.md section 6 if the fix touches retries, timeouts or paid calls.
