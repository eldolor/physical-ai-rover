# 3.202 S7: chaos and soak in the simulator

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.202; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** S7 (`PLAN-sim-hardening.md`, "Chaos and soak") is the last
unbuilt phase of the simulator plan, listed "Not built" in CLAUDE.md 3b and
Stage 5. 3.135 wrote the board's version (F19, its criterion 2, step
3.135.D, Jetson-only and still open) and built single-fault injectors for
it. This section is the simulator-only version: no Jetson, no Rover, no
spend. A long run of back-to-back missions with faults injected between and
during them, judged on the simulator's ground truth, so a failsafe that only
holds the first time, or a process that grows over hours, shows here before
the board or the car meets it. It does **not** duplicate 3.135.D: it borrows
3.135's fault list, its stack (`evaluations/jetson-fmea-3135/stack.py`,
`truth_body.py`) and its measures (`measure.py`) by import, and adds S7's
own link faults (a fault proxy between the brain's `RemoteRobot` and the
robot server: latency, loss, resets, duplicates) and a mocked cloud.

**Where it runs.** Whatever machine runs the harness: a Claude Code cloud
container (4 cores, no GPU) for this section's first runs, the desktop
otherwise. The stack is 3.135's sim-mode stack in this section's ports
(`plan_section.py env`): the split body with the ground-truth recorder, two
sensor servers, the fake D500 on a pty, the shipped robot server
(`SIM_MOTOR_BOARD=fake`) and the shipped brain. No ROS in steps B-C (no
`drive: ros`, no nav2): missions are the `frontier` policy (rule-based, $0,
geometry identity at arrival, 3.98) and, for the cloud faults, the
`vision` policy against a local stub cloud. The cloud is never reached
(`VISION_URL` is the stub or a closed port). Timing-dependent numbers are
taken under the desktop lock, exclusive (`tools/desktop_lock.py`), where
that machine has one.

**Criteria (written 2026-11-21, before building the harness and before any
run; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. **Each fault holds its bar, every time** (stability). The soak draws at
   least **40 faults** from K1-K11, seeded, uniform over the kinds, one every
   **2-4 minutes** (uniform; ~2-2.5 h with the missions between). Every
   drawn fault meets its row's bar on ground truth. A fault that could not
   be fired (no FORWARD lasted 0.15 s in 10 tries) is recorded `unfired`
   and drawn again; more than 10% unfired FAILS this criterion (the
   harness, not the robot, is then what is wrong).
2. **0 contacts over the whole run** (stability): samples with true
   `gap_cm` < 1.0 (`measure.CONTACT_CM`) across the full truth log, fault
   windows included, and no truth hole over 0.2 s anywhere (a hole cannot
   vouch for a contact).
3. **Recovery without a person** (progress, paired with 1): after every
   fault the harness restarts what the fault killed (systemd's job on the
   board) and a new mission is driving within **90 s** of the fault's end;
   at the run's end every process of the stack is alive and `/health`
   answers on both servers.
4. **Missions complete, and no Found is wrong** (progress, paired with 2):
   * (a) missions with no fault in their window, plus K7 and K8 missions,
     end `found` at **>= 90%** (3.135's bar; the in-process frontier sweep's
     rate on the same house and target is reported beside it);
   * (b) **0 wrong Found**: no `found` from a mission whose fault forbids it
     (K3, K9, K10, K11), and every `found` has the robot's true centre within
     **1.0 m** of the target object's true position (the simulator's map) at
     the mission's end;
   * (c) no mission is left running (hung) when the harness's 600 s
     per-mission limit passes; one that is, is recorded and stopped, and
     FAILS (a).
5. **No unbounded growth** (stability over time, paired with 4's mission
   count): RSS of every stack process sampled every 5 s. For each process
   life (a restart begins a new life) of at least 30 minutes, the
   least-squares slope of its per-mission minimum RSS **<= +20 MB/h**; the
   body and sensor processes (never killed) grow **<= 50 MB** from the
   run's first 30 minutes to its last 30 (median of each window); 0 OOM
   kills. The simulator's own logs stay bounded (`GridWorld.log` is a
   `deque(maxlen=LOG_KEEP)`, 2,000 since S4; checked, not assumed, by the
   body's RSS).
6. **The watchdog's stop latency is reported** for K1, K2, K6, K11 (median,
   max, n) beside criterion 1's bar, and the robot server's wheel-loop
   `late_ticks` over the run. Reported, not barred: a cloud container's
   timing is not the board's (3.136 is the board's).
7. **The harness tells the truth** (3.135 criterion 4's rule), before the
   scored run: each injector run once against a build with the guard it
   tests switched off must FAIL its bar -- K1, K2, K6, K11 with the
   watchdog off (`robot_plant.py`'s `watchdog`), K3 with the board's
   heartbeat off (`heartbeat`), K4 `lidar_fresh`, K5 `feedback_fresh`, K9
   and K10 with the brain's cloud classification off (`cloud_class`), and
   criterion 4(b)'s wrong-Found check fed a planted `found` far from the
   target. An injector whose plant passes is not scored until the harness
   is fixed. K7 and K8 have no guard to switch off (they measure the
   system's tolerance); they are recorded as such.

A failed criterion is recorded FAILED with its numbers, not re-scoped. A
real bug found in `robot/`, `control/` or `brain/` is recorded here and
reported; it is fixed in this section only if it is small and its file is
not another session's.

  "caught but not discriminating" (3.135 amendment 2's rule): it is scored,
