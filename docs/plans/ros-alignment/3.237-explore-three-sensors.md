# 3.237 Explore with lidar, depth and the pan-tilt camera

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.237; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** The user, 2026-11-28, through the orchestrator: plan frontier
exploration that uses the depth camera and the pan-tilt camera as well as
the lidar, ahead of the queue. The design was recommended by the
orchestrator and approved by the user; this file refines it against the
code.

**The design.**
* **The lidar map still makes the frontiers.** The other two sensors change
  whether a goal is safe to reach and what is learned on arrival.
* **Depth (the OAK-D floor band) is a veto** for low things the lidar's
  12 cm plane misses (3.74 B in the sim; 3.222 on the Rover, whose K7
  failed 155.6 vs 150 ms and whose K1-K5 need the car). **New here:** the
  band's returns also enter explore's map as low obstacles, so a frontier
  behind a shoe pile or a cable is unreachable, not open.
* **The pan-tilt camera steers and never vetoes** (3.74 C, 3.231): a fixed
  downward tilt while a goal is driven, for floor hazards (3.231's decided
  open-loop tilt; 3.223: servos cannot be read, so commanded angles are
  trusted), level again for 3.227's look after each goal, and room labels
  from the same pans (3.70).
* **Judged on exploration outcomes, not per sensor.** Four arms over the
  same houses and seeded starts: (1) lidar only, the Rover today; (2) +
  depth veto and low obstacles in the map; (3) + pan-tilt floor hazards
  (lidar and camera, no depth); (4) all three.

**What the code says, 2026-11-28 (refinements, with why).**
1. **Most of the sim half of A exists** (3.74 A, met 2026-10-12): objects
   carry heights by label (`GridWorld.object_heights`); `low_cells` are
   solid to the body and absent from the scan, the level row and the map
   (`lidar_solid_cells`); the depth grid carries a `floor` block, 96 rays,
   20-150 cm from the lens; `SIM_MAP=complex_house_hazards` holds 16 low
   things; a seeded stand-in detector (`SIM_HAZARD_RECALL`/`_FP`) thins
   their detections. `SIM_HAZARDS` was planned and became that house name.
   A reuses all of it.
2. **What A still lacks:** (a) the camera's vertical geometry. The sim's
   `_detections()` reports a low object anywhere from half a cell to the
   render range, whatever the tilt (`MockRobot.tilt_deg` is a reading only,
   3.220.C); a real camera 0.210 m up (the xacro, CAD) sees no floor nearer
   than h / tan(pitch + vfov/2) and, with the optical axis level, nothing inside 0.49 m (amendment 1: the mount already pitches it 15 deg down). Arm 3
   would be flattered without it. (b) One hazard house is too few for a
   four-arm comparison: low things scattered on other houses, seeded.
3. **In the sim the depth veto is always on** (`MockRobot` always publishes
   `floor`; `forward_clearance()` takes it in series). Arm 1 needs a way to
   turn the band off, as the car is today (`no_floor_band`).
4. **Explore always sends goals** (`MissionRunner` refuses an explore
   mission without a navigator). In process the goal path is a nav2 stand-in
   (refinement 5); the robot server executing goals under
   `drive: verbs` exists only on the live stack, so `drive: verbs` is
   judged in C, not B.
5. **`FakeNav` cannot judge this section** (found 2026-11-28, before any
   measurement): it teleports to the goal and plans over `g.objects`, low
   things included, so nav2-in-miniature is omniscient about exactly what
   the real nav2 cannot see, and no low contact can happen on a goal. B0
   and B therefore stand nav2 in with **`LidarNav`**: 3.159's VettedNav
   (`tests/drop_sweep.py`: every goal driven through
   `vet_wheel_velocity()` then `advance()`, aborted after 3 s held), with
   its planner over walls and `lidar_solid_cells` only. Its one leftover
   omniscience: a goal whose own cell holds a low thing is refused, as
   VettedNav refuses any object cell. Recorded, not fixed: it can only
   lower arm 1's contacts.
6. **The hazard list and explore's goal rejection are 3.231.C** (Touches
   `brain/`, `brain/explore.py`, the bridge). Arm 3 is 3.231.C's sim half;
   it is not rebuilt here (see "Overlaps").

**Criteria.** Written before any build or measurement (CLAUDE.md section
7). A failed bar is recorded as failed. Contacts are judged on ground truth
by `tests/footprint_sweep.py`'s polygons (chassis rectangle from the true
pose, a cell's square), sharing no code with `robot/safety.py`, the floor
band, the scan or explore. A contact is the chassis within 1.0 cm of a
low object's square; an event is counted once per approach (it must leave
3 cm before it counts again).

### A -- the sim's low things, honest to every sensor at the Rover's geometry

* **A1. Nothing changes without heights or tilt.** The sim fingerprint
  (`tests/test_sim_fingerprint.py`) is equal, byte for byte, on every house
  with no heights; on `complex_house_hazards` with the camera level and the
  scatter off, the scan, level row, floor block and map are equal to `dev`.
* **A2. Each sensor sees what its geometry lets it, judged by an
  independent function.** On `complex_house_hazards` and five scattered
  houses, 500 seeded poses each, for every low object: the lidar's scan
  returns it **0 times**; the floor band reports it at its true range
  +/- one cell exactly when its nearest face lies inside 20-150 cm and
  +/- 36.5 deg of the lens (**100 % agreement**); the camera's detections
  report it exactly when its floor-contact point lies inside the camera's
  vertical and horizontal view at the commanded tilt and inside render
  range, unoccluded (**100 % agreement** with the test's own geometry, the
  stand-in detector off).
* **A3. Red first.** With the camera level, a low object nearer than
  h / tan(vfov/2) is **not** detected (today it is: the test fails on `dev`).
* **A4. A scatter never makes a house impossible.** `SIM_LOW_SCATTER=<n>:<seed>`
  places n low things (3.74's labels and heights) on free cells, the same
  for the same seed; never within 1 m of the start or on the target; the
  free cells reachable from the start, less the placed cells, are
  unchanged by the placement (a BFS in the test), so no doorway is closed.

**Amendment 1 to A (2026-11-28, before any A code or measurement).**
1. **"Level" means the optical axis level** (total pitch 0). The xacro
   mounts the camera on a 15 deg downward wedge (`camera_pitch`,
   `[PLACEHOLDER]`, "10-20 deg, unmodelled"), so the commanded tilt 0 is
   15 deg down. The sim takes the camera's pitch as that mount less the
   commanded tilt (up positive, 3.220.C), lens 0.210 m off the floor (the
   xacro's `wheel_radius` + `pan_z` + `camera_up`, CAD), vertical field 46.8 deg (the sim
   camera's 60 deg horizontal on a 4:3 frame; `[PLACEHOLDER]` until
   3.231.A1). A3 is tested with the mount set to 0.
2. **A2's three checks, stated so that each judges the new code and
   nothing else:**
   * the lidar: the scan with the low things in the house is **equal** to
     the scan with them removed (stronger than "never returns them");
   * the floor band: each zone's reading equals the test's own ray march
     along that zone's published bearing, from the lens, to the nearest
     wall or object (low or tall), within one cell; no reading exactly
     when the march finds nothing in 20-150 cm;
   * the camera: the detected low things equal those that pass the test's
     own vertical-and-range rule (depression angle of the cell centre from
     the lens inside the pitch +/- half the vertical field) among those the
     existing 3.32 occlusion rule (`renderer.visible_bearing`) reaches.
     Occlusion is 3.32's, already measured, and is not re-judged here.
3. **Only low things are gated by the vertical rule.** A tall thing
   reaches from the floor past the horizon, so some part of it is in any
   view the sim's pitch range allows; tall detections stay as they are.

**As built.** The vertical rule is the pinhole's: a floor point's angle
below the axis is atan(h / f) - pitch, f its distance from the lens along
the camera's horizontal axis (the lens `pan_x` + `camera_x` from the
centre, CAD); the sideways offset does not enter. It gates `detections`
and `objects_visible` alike, after the stand-in detector's draw. The tilt
lives on the world (`GridWorld.camera_tilt_deg`),
which `MockRobot.tilt_deg` reads and writes, so a look verb's levelling
reaches the detections. `SIM_FLOOR_BAND=0` withholds the `floor` block.
`SIM_LOW_SCATTER=<n>:<seed>` in `build_world`, on open floor only; a
label the house holds is never reused, so the hazard house refuses it.

**Found on the way.** The test's band check first used a fixed-step march
and failed at a ray clipping a wall's corner for under 0.005 cell, which
the exact caster counts; then at a ray through a corner exactly, missed
when recast from the bearing rounded to 0.001 deg. The test now traverses
cells exactly from the zone's unrounded centre (one ray a zone) and
counts a corner crossing as a hit. Nothing in the band changed.

**Review (`/code-review`), nine findings; fixed before the numbers
above:** the vertical rule used the straight-line distance from the
centre (now the pinhole's forward distance from the lens; the test
computes it on its own); `objects_visible` was not gated (now is); the
scatter could copy a label the house holds, a find's target among them
(now skipped); a flood fill alone let a shoe narrow a doorway (open floor
only); the gate shifted the stand-in detector's seeded draws (it now
draws first); a house change reset the tilt (kept); the spec said every
look verb levels the tilt (`look_step` does not; the spec says so);

here:** `tests/test_explore_three_sensors_a2.py` (~70 s) belongs in
`SLOW_FILES`, which is `tools/plan_section.py`, inside 3.138.B's Touches:
