{"detail":"timed out"}`, raised in `control/mission_runner.py`

window, 4 s after VE's kill at 683 s, so the squeeze around a kill stalled the
robot server's frame read past its deadline.

* the robot server and the brain answered `/health` on all 810 samples;
* no process but VE was killed;

mission as `failed`, and no health bar sees it. On the car the camera is
`robot/camera_stream.py` (3.90 A: newest frame, stale -> 503), not the sim's
frame. Which path stalled here, and whether one 503 should fail a mission
under B3.2's budget, is this section's to find (the orchestrating session,
