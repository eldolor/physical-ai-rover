# 3.142 The soak's per-mission memory minimum falls 21 MB/h (3.135.C (b))

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.142; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.135.C's 4 h memory soak on the Jetson (criterion 1 of
[3.135](3.135-jetson-fmea.md); run 1, 2026-11-02 21:43 -> 2026-11-03 01:48,
rev `dd0a7c9f`, 15 W, `evaluations/jetson-fmea-3135/board/run1/`) FAILED its
bar (b): the least-squares slope of each mission's MemAvailable minimum
against the mission's midpoint, over the 13 missions that start after the
first hour, is **-21.08 MB/h (bar >= -10)**. The other half of (b) was met: no
role's per-mission minimum RSS grew over 50 MB from hour 1 to hour 4 (none
grew more than 5.6 MB; perception -72.6, the simulator body -32.7). A failed
row becomes its own section (3.135, criterion 3's rule, applied to criterion 1).
This section holds the finding; nothing is fixed or re-scored here.

**What the data says (read from the committed records, 2026-11-03; not yet
a test of any cause):**

* Each mission's minimum is a transient at its start: 14 of the 19 lows fall
  27-42 s into the mission, while `brain.text_encoder` children (MobileCLIP
  for YOLOE and CLIP RN50's text, 3.81.C; which process starts them is not
  read here) encode the new target's text; at the low the `other: python`
  role holds 709-1,139 MB. 3.81.E recorded this as the new-target low.
  Mission 0's low (+23 s) is the perception loop's first load (1,342 MB).
* The missions whose 5-s samples hold no encoder child have high minima:
  k2, k4, k9 (a target repeated from an earlier mission: red backpack,
  plunger) and k3. Mission 9 (`procthor:val:3` / plunger, 1,253 MB) is the
  first point of the fitted window; **without it the same fit is +5.0 MB/h.**
* Over the same 13 missions the median MemAvailable rises (+33 MB/h).
* So as measured, the slope tracks whether a mission's start encoded a new
  target's text (and whether the 5-s account caught it), not a level that
  falls across the hours. This is a reading of one run, not a proof that
  nothing leaks; the bar stays as written and FAILED.
* The run's lowest sample overall (700 MB, 1-s sysfs; (a) met, bar 500) was
  4.4 s into mission 0, at the perception loop's first target switch.

**Open (for whoever takes this up):** whether to measure the trend at a
point that does not depend on the start transient (e.g. a per-mission steady
minimum, or every target encoded once before the soak), and whether a second
4 h run on the board reproduces -21 MB/h. Any change to how (b) is read is an
amendment to 3.135 written before the run it affects; no bar moves after the
fact.

## The cause, read from run 1 (2026-11-03, before any fix or run)

The low's process, and why five missions have none. Read from the committed
records (`memory.jsonl.gz`, `telemetry/sysfs.jsonl.gz`, `perception.log.gz`)
and a listing of the board's text cache (`~/.cache/vision-picar/text-features`,
read-only, 2026-11-03). No board time was used.

* **The `other: python` process is the perception loop's text-encoder
  child.** `tools/jetson/perception_loop.py` switches its pipeline at the
  mission's start (`switch done` 5.2-6.4 s after `switch start` in every
  mission: the build only). YOLOE takes its new prompt on the first predict
  after that, so `brain.text_encoder`'s CPU child (MobileCLIP, then CLIP
  RN50's text if YOLOE boxed something) runs 7-42 s into the mission. In the
  simulator the brain loads no model (3.81.F), so nothing else spawns it.
* **The text cache decides which missions pay for it.** `brain.text_encoder`
  caches every encoding on disk (3.81.C). The perception loop runs with the
  default cache, the board's `~/.cache/vision-picar/text-features`, which
  outlives every bench. On the board, "red backpack" has been cached for
  both models since 2026-10-14 (3.81's benches). In run 1 the plunger's text
  was written by mission 7 (23:16) and reused by mission 9. So missions 0, 2,
  4 and 9 encoded nothing. Mission 3 (basketball) encoded MobileCLIP only:
  there is no RN50 "basketball" file, because CLIP is asked only of a box.
  Amendment 3's schedule skips only a repeat of the *previous* mission's
  target. **So criterion 1's "a new target every mission, so 3.81.E's
  new-target path runs every time" did not hold for 5 of 19 missions.** This
  is the harness falling short of the written workload; the system did
  nothing wrong.
* **The two kinds of mission, on the 1-s sampler (lowest MemAvailable, s
  after the start):**
  * encoded: 713-1,042 MB, at 26-41 s (k3 at 2.9 s, MobileCLIP only);
  * cached: 977-1,153 MB at 4-5 s, the switch itself, except k0's 700 MB
    (the run's first switch).
  * Mission 9 is the first point of the fitted window and a cached one, so
    it tilts the fit.
* **Slopes over the 13 missions after hour 1.** These are readings of run 1,
  chosen after seeing it, so they are descriptions, not tests:

  | Reading | Missions | Slope (MB/h) |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | (b) as written (5-s minimum) | 13 | **-21.08** |
  | the same, missions that encoded only (5-s) | 12 | +5.0 |
  | the same, missions that encoded only (1-s) | 12 | +7.8 |
  | all, the 1-s sampler | 13 | -13.7 |
  | steady minimum: the lowest sample from 60 s in (5-s) | 13 | +36.8 |
  | steady minimum (1-s) | 13 | +35.9 |
  | median MemAvailable | 13 | +33.0 |

  Nothing that lives the 4 h grows: (b)'s per-role half was met, and the
  steady level rises. The transient's depth does not deepen across the
  hours either: the encoded missions' lows are flat. As measured, the
  failure is the mix of encoded and cached missions, mostly mission 9.

## Criteria (written 2026-11-03, before building and before run 2)

The fix is to the harness: the workload must be the one criterion 1 says.
No system code changes, and no bar of 3.135 moves. Run 1's (b) stays FAILED
as recorded.

1. **The soak encodes every mission's target (`soak.py`).** The perception
   loop runs with `PICAR_TEXT_CACHE` set to a directory of the run's own,
   under its out directory (never the board's `~/.cache`). The harness
   empties it before each mission's target is written to the switch file.
   After each mission, the record (`missions.jsonl`) lists the models whose
   features were written into that directory during the mission
   (`encoded`: e.g. `["mobileclip:...", "open_clip:RN50:openai"]`). Pinned in
   `test_soak.py`, red first: the test fails on today's `soak.py`.
2. **The harness still tells the truth (3.135 criterion 4's spirit).**
   `test_soak.py` passes whole. The planted check (`soak_plant.py`, the scorer
   unchanged) still fails the planted leak and passes the steady stand-in.
   Run once on the desktop.
3. **Run 2 on the board (3.135.C's soak, 4 h, overnight), workload check.**
   Every mission whose stack came up lists MobileCLIP in `encoded` (YOLOE's
   prompt: every mission). RN50 is reported, no bar (it is asked only of a
   box). A mission without MobileCLIP fails this criterion, and the run's (b)
   is then read as for run 1: recorded, not a test of this fix.
4. **Run 2: 3.135 criterion 1 (b) exactly as written and read by amendment
   3.** That is: the slope of each mission's 5-s MemAvailable minimum over the
   missions after the first hour, **>= -10 MB/h**; and no role's per-mission
   minimum grows **over 50 MB** from hour 1 to hour 4. (a) must hold too
   (**>= 500 MB**, over the 1-s and 5-s samplers): every mission now pays the
   encode. (c) and (d) are scored and reported, but they are 3.141's and
   3.143's.
5. **Reported beside run 2, no bar:**
   * the same slope on the 1-s sampler's per-mission minima;
   * the steady minimum (the lowest sample from 60 s in);
   * the encoder's peak per mission (`other: python` RSS at the low);
   * whether the transient deepens over the hours.

   If (b) fails with criterion 3 met, the -21 MB/h was not only the workload
   mix. It is then a finding for this section, read the same way.

**Amendment 1 (2026-11-03, step A's review, before run 2; no bar moved).**
Criterion 3 judges only missions that ran at least 60 s without an error.
The encode lands 7-42 s into a mission, and `encoded` is read at the
mission's end, so a mission that errored or ended sooner can show `[]`
having done nothing wrong. Such missions are listed apart (`not_judged`).
`soak_score.py` reports the check as `workload` in `summary.json` and its
printout, outside 3.135's `met`.

*Why this and not a different reading of (b):* the bar was written for the
workload criterion 1 describes. Restoring that workload tests the bar as
written, while a new reading (a steady minimum, the 1-s sampler) would pick
the statistic after seeing run 1. The disk cache is right on the car (a
repeated target costs nothing there). Emptying it only in the soak keeps the
soak the worst case it was meant to be.
