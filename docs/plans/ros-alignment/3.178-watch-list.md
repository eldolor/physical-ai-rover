# 3.178 A watch list: a few named valuables noticed on every mission

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.178; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the user, 2026-11-12: features of similar products and projects worth
building while the Rover waits on Waveshare.) The one shipping product near
this project's purpose is a robot vacuum (Narwal Flow 2, 2026): while cleaning
it notices wallets, keys and jewellery on the floor, alerts the person and pins
them on its map. It does not name everything; it watches for a short list.

That is the gap 3.46.C left. Naming **every** object on real frames failed its
75% bar with every namer tried and was closed by the user on 2026-10-10, so on
the car the inventory, 3.162's report and 3.172.C's recall know only the
target of the day. A short fixed list is an easier problem than open naming:
the shipped pipeline (YOLOE crops, then CLIP, gate P >= 0.8) is already asked
one name a mission; this asks it a handful. If it holds, every mission and
survey fills the memory that 3.172.C's recall and 3.175's "where did you last
see it" read, for the things people actually lose.

**Scope.**
* A list the person sets (default: keys, wallet, phone, glasses, remote; at
  most 8), watched on every mission and survey beside the target; sightings go
  to the inventory by the lidar as any detection does.
* A precision problem first: a wrong pin costs trust and, through recall, a
  drive. The gate may be stricter than the target's.
* Memory and time on the board are bars: more prompts through the text cache
  (3.81.C), no new model, the 250 ms perception budget kept.
* A: what the recorded corpus can say for free (which list items appear in the
  labelled walks at all). The real measurement needs the Rover's frames with
  list items placed on the floor.

**Criteria (written in step A, before measuring; CLAUDE.md section 7).** At
least: per-item precision and recall on a pinned frame set, with precision the
harder bar; pins per mission that are wrong (bar near 0); perception p90 and
`MemAvailable` on the Jetson with the list on against off; the target's own
find rate unchanged with the list on (stability).
