# 3.204 A landmark pinned where the object never was

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.204; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Status: a recorded finding, no code changed** (2026-11-21). The cause is the
inventory's ranging rule, which 3.178.F diagnosed and 3.178.G holds. The user
decided on 2026-11-17 that `dev`'s ranging and the target's inventory stay
unchanged. Changing them is the user's call (see "What is left").

**Why.** 3.70.M3's live find (`dev` `65f6e841`,
`evaluations/find-last-seen-370/live-m3.jsonl`) ended with a second "guitar"
landmark at (13.66, 10.53), belief 0.982. The real guitar was at (6.45, 1.65),
moved there from the study, and the survey had one guitar, at (17.40, 5.39). A
later find for the guitar on that inventory would go there first. The question
was whether a false detection was pinned, or a sighting merged at the wrong place.

## How it was traced (2026-11-21, a cloud session: Docker, 4 cores)

* **Live, with traces:** M3's live find re-run as written (`stale.py live`'s
  `live_run`, the exclusive desktop lock, image `vision-picar-ros:plan-3.70`
  built here by `build-cloud.sh`, the base image pulled through `mirror.gcr.io`
  after Docker Hub answered 429). Two recorders changed nothing the brain did:
  * a `sitecustomize` hook in the brain process logged every inventory frame:
    the pose, the frame's true room, each detection's body bearing, its scan
    range and where it was placed, and each landmark it created;
  * the harness sampled `/world/truth` and `/world/pose` every 0.5 s.
* **In process:** `evaluations/phantom-landmark-3204/sweep.py`, 3.46's survey
  (`tests.inventory_sweep.run_one(survey=True)`, explore with FakeNav, truth
  pose) on `dev`, 20 starts (seed 3462), with the sim's own detector (clean, as
  the live stack's) and with 3.46's erring detector.
* **A phantom** is a landmark a later mission would carry (belief at least
  `CARRY_MIN_BELIEF`, 0.5) farther than `MERGE_M` (0.5 m) from every instance
  of its class (distance to the nearest cell).

## What was found

**The sim's detector does not invent a guitar.** Its only injected false
positives are hazard labels (cable, sock, shoe, toy; `LowObjectDetector`), and
only in `complex_house_hazards`. The guitar is one cell.

**The live re-run made a different guitar phantom, and traced it to its first
frame.** The survey (`map-847cae5e`, 1728 s, 54 reported) and the find
(found 0.835 m from the moved guitar in 150 s; returned to the dock) left three
guitars: the survey's (17.50, 5.31), a true pin at the new place (6.44, 1.79),
and a candidate at (17.38, 6.14), belief 0.701. That candidate was born in the
survey, at step 584:
* the robot was 0.83 m from the guitar, pivoting at 35 deg/s;
* the detection sat at the frame's edge (body bearing -29.7 deg);
* the scan's nearest return within 2 deg was **1.568 m**. The frames before it
  ranged the guitar at about 0.67 m;
* the SLAM pose's heading trailed truth by 19 deg (212.2 against 231.7). The
  scan, the frame and the pose were read at different instants of the pivot,
  so the gate missed the guitar and took the wall behind it.

It was carried into the find as a candidate (0.701). One more look from a new
view would report it.

**The survey's report held 11 phantoms among its 66 carried landmarks, 2 of
them reported:** a kitchen counter 1.03 m off (0.928) and a laptop bag 1.00 m
off (0.948). The other nine were candidates (0.51-0.70). In the live survey,
15 of 88 landmark births were more than 0.5 m from any object of their label
(`live-births.json`). Two mechanisms, both in ranging, explain them:
1. **A nearer edge in the gate** (most of them). An object partly hidden behind
   a door jamb or a piece of furniture is detected at the bearing of the part
   that shows. That bearing sits beside the edge, and the nearest return within
   `RANGE_GATE_DEG` (2 deg) is the edge. Examples (true range, then the range
   taken):
   * the mug through the hall's doorway: 1.88 m, then 0.72 m;
   * the pet bowl: 3.42 m, then 0.97 m;
   * the toy car: 2.51 m, then 0.69 m.

   In these cases the pose was within 0.15 m of truth and the robot was not
   turning.
2. **Pivot skew**, live only (step 584 above). The in-process sim stands still
   between reads.

Both are bounded by `MAX_RANGE_M` (4 m) from the robot.

**In process, `dev` makes the same phantoms (mechanism 1).** The pet bowl
lands at (10.2, 6.6) in the sweep as in the live survey:

| Detector | Phantoms (reported) | True carried (reported) | Recall (3.46's) | Off 0.5-1 / 1-2 / 2-4 / >4 m |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clean (the sim's own) | **50 (7)** | 1580 (1203) | 0.989 (884 of 894) | 2 / 24 / 24 / 0 |
| 3.46's erring detector | 236 (28) | 1571 (1130) | 0.933 | 72 / 59 / 47 / 58 |

With the clean detector, every phantom is a ranging error (all within 4 m). The
58 beyond 4 m under the erring detector are its injected relabels, which are
working as designed.

**M3's own phantom was not reproduced.** It was 7-11 m from anywhere the guitar
was, and the robot's range to it was at most 4 m. So the pose the brain used
when it was placed must have been off by at least 3 m. **Inferred, not
measured:** M3's find mis-localised for a stretch, which fits its other symptoms:
* the recall goal aborted after 0.3 m;
* the return to the dock was `nav2_aborted`;
* the brain's goals in the saved frame failed, while frontier goals (drawn on the
  same live map) worked.

This re-run's find was short (150 s, no aborts), and its pose error never passed
1 m in 2,588 frames. M3's brain log did not survive its session.

**It is not 3.178's parked pin rule.** No watch list is involved. It is the
ranging rule beneath every landmark. That is 3.178.F's finding ("the nearest
return within `RANGE_GATE_DEG` (2 deg) is then a nearer edge"; true sightings
ranged 0.59-1.21 m off), and 3.178.G is where its fix was put: open, stopped
until real frames, with "dev's ranging rule, the target's inventory and recall"
unchanged (the user, 2026-11-17). `tests/test_inventory.py` pins the
nearest-return rule on purpose (a thin thing in front is what the robot would
hit).

## What is left (each the user's decision, none built)

* **A ranging rule for every landmark, the target's included.** One candidate
  that 3.178.F did not try leaves a detection unplaced when its gate straddles
  a depth edge, for example adjacent beams differing by more than 0.3 m. It
  would be held like a bearing with no return, so it still spares a miss. It
  would need bars written first, against this sweep's baseline above:
  * phantoms down;
  * recall and true pins not lost;
  * the pinned thin-thing test either kept or knowingly changed.

  It reaches the target's memory, which the user kept unchanged in 3.178.G.
* **Pivot skew:** frames taken while the body turns fast could be left out of
  the inventory, or the scan read at the frame's instant. This is live only, so
  it is judged live.
* **M3's mis-localised placement:** a long live find with these traces on (or
  the brain logging the pose it ranged with) to measure it, then a rule, for
  example no placement while the pose jumps or while nav2 aborts in the saved
  frame.
