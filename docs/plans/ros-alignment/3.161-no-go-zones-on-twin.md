# 3.161 No-go zones a person draws on the twin

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.161; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the user, 2026-11-06: features of a robot vacuum (Dreame) worth leveraging, for full autonomy; 3.158) A vacuum's person draws virtual walls and no-go areas (a
cable nest, a pet's bowl, a rug it gets stuck on). Here nav2's keepout
filter already exists (3.83/3.85, `nav2.yaml`), but only the robot marks
it (wedges, 3.68's refusals). Letting a person draw zones is small work on
that layer, and it also covers a known stairwell before 3.159 is proven.

**Scope.**
* The twin draws rectangles (and lines as walls) on the map; the brain
  stores them with the saved map (`map_id`) and the bridge writes them into
  the keepout mask, beside the robot's own marks (which expire; a person's
  do not).
* Explore never picks a goal inside one; nav2 never plans through one.
* A zone is not a safety veto (rule 4 is unchanged): the robot may still
  be inside one after a re-map and must be able to leave it.
* Local only, never uploaded (a home's floor plan).

**Criteria (written in step A, before building).** At least: 0 goals or
paths into a zone over the sweeps, entries into zones live (bar 0), a zone
surviving a reload, explore's finds unchanged where a zone does not wall off
the target, and phone-size evidence.

## 3.161.A: criteria and design (written 2026-11-07, before building)

**What it rests on.** The keepout layer as it is: the robot server marks
wedge spots (3.68/3.83, `robot/wedge_watch.py`), `RosWorld.set_keepout()`
posts the whole set to the bridge's `POST /nav/keepout`, and the bridge
draws discs into one `/keepout_mask` for nav2's stock KeepoutFilter (100
inside, -1 elsewhere, 3.76 K5), spots expiring after 300 s and cleared at a
mission's end (`DELETE /world/keepout`, K5.3). 3.50.D's saved map and
3.50.D5's `frame_id` (one id per saved map, kept through reloads and
re-maps). Explore's candidate pool (3.65's `_pool()`), the twin's map on
Drive (tap-to-goal, Re-map now) and 3.132's operator voice and bars.

**D1. A zone.** An axis-aligned rectangle in the saved map's frame (a
"wall" is a rectangle 0.10 m wide drawn as a line), at least 0.20 m a side,
at most 30 zones a map, each `{id, x0, y0, x1, y1, kind: area|wall, set_at,
by: "person"}`. No expiry: a person's zone stays until a person deletes it.

**D2. Where they live and how they reach nav2.** The brain keeps them, one
local file per `frame_id` (`<nogo_dir>/<frame_id>.json`, atomic and fsynced,
beside 3.160's names), never in git, never uploaded (a home's floor plan).
They reach nav2 by the existing door, two layers apart:
* the brain sends the whole set for the current `frame_id` to the robot
  server (`PUT /world/nogo`, at a mission's start and on each edit), which
  keeps a copy and passes it to `RosWorld`, which posts it to the bridge
  (one new route, `POST /nav/zones`, registered in the wall linter's route
  cap);
* the bridge draws **zones and spots into the same mask** (their union);
  `POST /nav/keepout` still replaces only the spots, and the mission-end
  clear (`DELETE /world/keepout`) clears only the spots: zones survive it.
With no `frame_id` (no saved map, the sim's own world) zones cannot be
drawn, and the twin says why ("Survey first: zones are kept with the saved
map").

**D3. Explore never picks a goal in one** (the brain's own check, so it
does not depend on nav2's mask): a candidate whose point lies inside a zone
grown by the robot's half-width (0.12 m) is dropped from the pool, and a
frontier cell inside a zone is no frontier. A survey's coverage counts only
floor outside zones.

**D4. Leaving a zone.** KeepoutFilter makes a zone lethal to the robot's
centre, so a robot inside one (placed there, or after a re-map moved the
map under it) could not plan out. The bridge leaves out of the mask any
zone the robot's pose is inside or within 0.30 m of, until the robot is
0.30 m clear of it, then draws it again (logged `zone_suspended` /
`zone_restored`, shown on `/health`'s `nogo` block). The brain's first goal
in that case is the nearest free point outside the zone.

**D5. A person and a zone.** A person's goal tap inside a zone is refused
by the robot server with reason `no_go_zone` (the twin words it: "That's in
a no-go zone"); the D-pad is not refused: a zone is not a safety veto.

**Not a safety veto (rule 4 unchanged).** `robot/safety.py`, its vets and
the arbitration get no change; a zone shapes where autonomy plans, nothing
else. A zone drawn over a stair is a convenience, never the protection:
3.159's drop rule is (3.158 C8).

**D6. The twin (operator mode, Drive's map).** "No-go zones": Draw (drag a
rectangle), Wall (drag a line), tap a zone to delete it, Done; zones drawn
in a token colour under the robot's own marks. Strings in `strings.js`,
both voices.

**Ties to 3.158.** The house sheet's no-go areas are drawn with this; C5's
rooms entered do not count a zone's floor as missed; C7/C8 do not credit a
zone with safety; D runs with the house's zones in place.
