# 3.193 The bridge's no-go mask: a zone's whole edge, and its redraw after leaving

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.193; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the orchestrator, on the user's delegation, 2026-11-20) Two faults
seen in 3.192's live run (`3.192-nogo-reads-as-floor.md`, F9 and "Left"):
* **EDGE.** One ground-truth entry, 2 mm inside zone `za15d23feaa38`'s south
  edge (centre (4.898, 1.388), y0 = 1.386), for one 0.5 s sample. The
  bridge's `keepout.mask` marks a 0.05 m cell only when its centre lies
  inside a zone, so up to half a cell (0.025 m) along a zone's edge stays
  passable to nav2.
* **REDRAW.** In the same run's Z3 part (3.161 Z3, D4: a zone around the
  robot, then a survey) the robot left the zone in 7.7 s, but the script
  never saw it drawn again (`restored: null`).

## 3.193.A: criteria and design (written 2026-11-20, before building)

**REDRAW traced first (from the code and 3.192's `live.json`, before any
change).** 3.161's live Z3 (2026-11-19) saw `zone_restored` at 0.311 m; 3.192's
(2026-11-20) did not. Between them 3.161 Part 2 merged (E4: *a zone covering
the mission's dock is not in force*). `live.py`'s Z3 draws a 0.8 m zone around
the robot where it stands and then starts a survey. With `return_to_dock: true`
(`config/robot.yaml`) and no saved dock, the mission's dock is its start pose
(`MissionRunner._nogo_dock`), which is inside that zone, so at the survey's
start the runner leaves the zone out of what the robot server and the bridge
are sent (E4, `dock_refused`). The bridge's `_zones_step` then drops the id
from `zones_suspended` with no event (a zone that is no longer in the set is
neither suspended nor restored). 3.192's record fits: `z3_bridge_at_draw` holds
4 zones with this one suspended (the add, with no mission running, sent all
four), and nothing later. **So the hypothesis is that the bridge has no fault:
the script's Z3 became a dock-conflict case when E4 merged.** It is checked
live (R1 below) before the script is changed.

**D1. EDGE: a cell a zone touches is marked.** `keepout.mask` marks every
0.05 m cell whose square overlaps a zone polygon with positive area: the cells
whose centre is inside (as now), plus every cell a zone edge passes through
(found per column along each edge, so the work stays on the boundary). A zone
edge lying exactly on a cell boundary (within 1e-6 m) marks only the side the
zone is on. So every point inside a zone lies in a lethal cell, and no cell is
marked that the zone does not overlap. **Spots (the robot's wedge discs) keep
their centre rule**: they are drawn by a separate loop that shares no code
with the zones' (`mask`'s `for s in spots`), and 3.193's brief scopes them
out unless they share the path; `tests/test_wedge_watch.py` stays unchanged.

**D2. REDRAW.** If R1 confirms the trace: no bridge change; `live.py`'s Z3 is
changed so its zone is not a dock conflict: the zone is drawn around the
robot **while a survey is running**, once the robot's true centre is at least
1.0 m from the mission's start (so the 0.8 m zone stays clear of the dock leg
by E1's 0.18 m), and the bridge's suspension and restoration are read from
`/health`'s counters per poll and from the bridge's own log lines
(`zone_suspended <id>` / `zone_restored <id>`), not from `last` alone (one
event can hide another between two polls). If R1 shows the zone in force at
the bridge and still never restored, the fault is the bridge's and is fixed
there instead (D4's rule unchanged).

**What does not change.** D4's suspension rule (`keepout.suspend`, LEAVE_M,
SUSPEND_DEPTH_M), the spots' drawing, the bridge's routes (no new route; the
wall linter's cap unchanged), `robot/safety.py`, `robot/server.py`,
`brain/` (`brain/explore.py` above all), `control/`. The in-process sweeps
(`evaluations/nogo-3161/sweep.py`'s `ZoneNav`) read `keepout.suspend`, never
`mask`, so they cannot see this change; progress is judged live.

**Criteria (CLAUDE.md section 7).** Live runs: `evaluations/nogo-3193/live.py`
(3.161's `live.py` with D2's Z3 and the truth read every 0.25 s instead of
0.5 s), `scaled_house`, a fresh stack per run from this branch's image
(`vision-picar-ros:plan-3.193`, built with `build-cloud.sh`),
`PICAR_ROS_NETWORK=host`, the brain on the shipped chooser (`rooms`), the same
three zones as 3.192's F9 (`sweep.place_zones("scaled_house", 0)`).

## 3.193.A amendment 1 (written 2026-11-20, during the build, before any live run)

E2 as written ("the existing mask tests unchanged and passing") cannot hold
for one of them: `tests/test_no_go.py::test_a_rotated_zone_is_drawn_rotated`
asserts the marked area of a rotated 1 m square is within 0.08 m2 of 1.0, the
centre rule's area. D1 marks every cell an edge crosses, so the area is 1.1375
m2 by design (about 0.034 m of each 1 m edge's length on average). Seen when
the unit tests first ran on D1's code. **E2 is recorded as failed for that
test**; the test is changed to D1's reading (the area at least 1.0 and at most
1.0 + 4 x 0.05 = 1.2, every marked cell within the old bounds), and the other
mask tests stay unchanged. Touches widened to `tests/test_no_go.py` for it.

## 3.193.A amendment 2 (written 2026-11-20, after both reviews of the first build, before any live run)

Both reviewers (`/code-review`, two rounds, and the Thermos pass, all on
Sonnet) found that D1 breaks D4's premise: with every touched cell drawn, a
robot only millimetres inside a zone can have its own cell and every
neighbour drawn, so NavFn (which frees only the robot's own cell) cannot plan
out, while D4's 0.05 m depth does not suspend the zone. A first fix (depth to
the nearest undrawn cell, Euclidean, on a 0.05 m lattice of the map frame)
still failed in two ways the second round found: NavFn's A* is 4-connected
(a diagonal free cell is no exit), and the costmap's cells follow the SLAM
map's origin, not that lattice. **D3 (new). The mask's lattice is the SLAM
map's** (`keepout.phase_of` of each `/map` origin, so a mask cell is a
global-costmap cell; a change of phase redraws the mask), and **D4 also
suspends a zone that boxes the robot**: its cell and four neighbours all drawn
by that zone (`keepout.boxed`), besides the 0.05 m depth as before. This is a
bridge change (`bridge.py`: the phase, two calls, `/health`'s `nogo` gains
`lattice_phase_m`; no route changes), so Touches widen to `bridge.py` and E5's
"the bridge's routes" stays as written. Also from the reviews: a zone with no
width (area over longest side under 1 mm) is refused 400 (its edges alone
would draw a line of cells); `live.py`'s Z3 credits only its own zone's
restore and stops after one refused add.

New bar, beside E1: **E6** (unit, on three lattice phases): while a zone stays
drawn under the robot (not suspended), the robot's cell or one of its four
neighbours is undrawn; **E7** (live): `lattice_phase_m` recorded at each run's
end and the map's phase the same from the first zone drawn to the end of the
run (a phase that moves is recorded, not failed: the bridge redraws on it).
