# 3.156 Return to the dock: a mission or survey ends at the charger

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.156; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** The objective is full autonomy: give the robot a mission or send it
on a survey and it does what it needs to do (the user, 2026-11-06, who
marked this item important and asked for it in the plan). Today every
mission ends where it stops. 3.137's battery ladder stops the robot safely
at Low/Reserve/Power-off, but nothing brings it home first; 3.50.D's saved
map already assumes the robot starts on the dock (a reload localises from
it; off the dock 10/10 fall back to a fresh map), so a robot that does not
come back also breaks the next mission's start.

**Scope.**
* The dock is a known pose on the saved map (where a session that reloaded
  started, 3.50.D3).
* After `found`, `exhausted`, `out_of_budget` or a survey's end, the robot
  drives back to the dock (a nav2 goal), then a final approach and
  alignment. A person's Stop and 3.84's latch still win; the safety path is
  unchanged (CLAUDE.md rules 1-4).
* A return reserve: a mission ends early, by time or battery, while there
  is still enough to get home (a budget from the distance home, not a fixed
  number), so Low is never first met far from the dock.
* What "docked" means on the car (charging seen on the board, 3.137.D) is
  the Rover's; the simulator has a dock stand-in.
* Recharge and resume (added 2026-11-06, from a robot vacuum's "recharge
  and resume", the user's call to leverage it): a mission or survey that
  reaches the reserve goes home, charges to a level, then drives back and
  resumes where it stopped (the survey's covered rooms, the find's searched
  places), within its overall time limit. Step D.
* Out of scope: the dock hardware itself.

## Design (3.156.A, 2026-11-07)

Facts this rests on: 3.50.D3 (the dock is the saved map's `dock_pose`,
(0, 0, 0), in `meta.json`; a reload localises from it within 0.5 m and
15 deg or falls back to a fresh map), 3.70 (every end goes through
`MissionRunner._finish`, which cancels the nav2 goal, stops the wheels
and shuts the halt gate), 3.137 (Low 10.5 V is reported only and is the
brain's `battery_floor_v`; Reserve 10.2 V latches the server against all
autonomy; Power-off is nobody's to override; all three placeholders until
3.137.E), 3.84 (a person's Stop latches autonomy out until a mission
starts) and nav2's goal tolerance (0.10 m, heading not asked).

**D1. The dock pose.** Recorded once, at mission start, in the house frame
the brain already sends goals in (`world/ros_world.py` `set_goal`):
* under `world: ros` with a reloaded map, the saved `dock_pose` through the
  session anchor (the bridge reports it; one new field on its existing
  status, no new route);
* under `world: ros` on a fresh map (a reload that fell back, or the first
  session), the session's start pose: 3.50.D3 makes the dock "where the
  first session began", so the fallback's start is the best known dock,
  and it is marked `dock_source: start` so a report never calls it
  verified;
* in the simulator (`world: sim`), the start pose.
`status.dock` carries `{x_m, y_m, theta, source}`. With no pose the return
is skipped (`return: skipped`, `no_dock`), never guessed.

**D2. When to go home.** A new phase, `returning`, between the mission's end
decision and `_finish`. The outcome is decided first and never changed by
the return; the return is its own field, `return`:
`docked` | `failed` (with a reason) | `cancelled` | `skipped` (with a reason).
* Returns after `found`, `exhausted`, `out_of_budget` (time, calls,
  battery floor or the return reserve), a survey's end,
  `arrived_unconfirmed` and `blocked` (routing is nav2's, 3.148, so a
  straight-line wedge is no reason to stay).
* No return after `cancelled` (a person's Stop or D-pad: the person has the
  robot), `failed` (a fault wants a person), or a battery at Reserve or
  Power-off (the server refuses autonomy; the brain does not ask).
* No nav2 (`drive`/`world` not `ros`, or its `hold` never clears within
  `return_wait_s`, 30 s): `skipped`, `no_nav2`. The return never drives
  in straight lines on verbs; routing stays nav2's.
* `return_to_dock` in `brain:` config, default on; off reproduces today's
  end exactly (the paired progress check, C6).

**D3. The return reserve.** The search ends early, as `out_of_budget` with
note `return reserve`, at the first tick where either budget left is
less than what the trip home is predicted to cost, with a margin:
* **Time:** `elapsed + T_home > mission_time_limit_s`, where
  `T_home = k * L_home / v_obs + t_dock`. `L_home` is the length of the
  shortest path from the robot to the dock on the brain's own map
  (inflated as nav2's is, 0.35 m; A* on the grid it already has), `v_obs`
  this mission's own driving speed (metres travelled / seconds driving,
  floored at 0.05 m/s, 0.10 m/s until 30 s of driving), `k` = 1.5 and
  `t_dock` = 30 s (the final approach). The limit covers the return: a
  1200 s mission is docked by 1200 s.
* **Battery:** the voltage predicted at the dock,
  `v_now - k * r * T_home`, falls under `battery_floor_v` + 0.1 V. `r` is
  the mission's drain rate in V/s over distinct board samples (3.137's
  rule: two samples apart, never one), taken as 0 until two samples 60 s
  apart exist. Below the floor the return still drives (Low is
  report-only); Reserve stops it, and C5 measures that it never does.
* Recomputed once a tick, cheaply: `L_home` only when the robot has moved
  0.5 m since the last search or the map changed (the 304 rule of 3.87).
These numbers (k, t_dock, the 0.1 V) are design constants, held to the
data by C4/C5; 3.156.C re-fits `r` against 3.137.E's discharge curve.

**D4. The final approach and alignment.** Two parts, both on paths that
exist:
1. a nav2 goal to a staging point 0.40 m in front of the dock along its
   heading (a goal like any explore goal: driver `brain`, 3.154's
   supersede rule, its id recorded);
2. the brain turns to the dock's heading and creeps the last 0.40 m with
   guarded verbs (3.22), closing on odometry against the staging point,
   then a final turn. Every verb goes through `robot/safety.py` as today.
"Docked" in the simulator: the true pose within 0.10 m and 10 deg of the
dock. On the Rover it is charging seen on the board (3.137.D), step C. A
physical dock is an obstacle to the cone and the corridor, so the last
centimetres may be vetoed; whether the car's dock needs a reversed entry
or a dock-aware exemption is a safety decision for step C with its own
criteria, not made here (rule 4 stands: no path around `SafetyController`).

**D5. Stop, the latch and safety are unchanged.** The return is the brain
driving: every verb and goal is `x-driver: brain`, so a person's Stop
latches it out (3.84) and ends the return `cancelled` with the robot left
where it stopped; a D-pad preempts it as it preempts a mission; Reserve
refuses it (`failed`, `battery_reserve`). The three failsafes cover the
phase because it runs inside the tick loop (B3.1-B3.3 unchanged), and the
halt gate still shuts in `_finish`, after the return. `robot/safety.py`,
`robot/server.py`'s arbitration and the bridge's cancel rules get no
change in B (C8 checks the diff).

**D6. The simulator's dock stand-in.** The start pose, painted on the
twin's map; no solid object (the stand-in is a pad), so it tests the
route and the alignment, not a physical entry (D4's step C question).
`status.return` and the twin's mission card show the phase; the twin's
change is a string and a row (3.132's tokens), with its screenshot.

**D7. Recharge and resume (3.156.D, sketched; its own criteria before its
build).** At the return reserve with the battery the cause, the mission
does not end: it is `charging`, its time limit paused (as 3.88's pauses
are), under a separate wall limit (`mission_wall_limit_s`, 4 h). It leaves
at `resume_v` (placeholder until 3.137.E) and goes back to where it
stopped, with what it kept: explore's visited goals and refusal list, the
survey's covered rooms, the find's no-list and memory. The simulator's
charge stand-in raises `sim/battery.py`'s voltage while docked at a set
rate. A time-limited end never charges (time does not come back).

## B's notes (decided before any measurement, 2026-11-07)

* **The runner's own default is off.** `MissionRunner(return_to_dock=...)`
  defaults False; D2's default-on is the brain config's
  (`brain.return_to_dock`, passed by `control/brain_server.py`). On in the
  runner changed every in-process harness's end (`test_goal_ids_3136`,
  `test_explore_ask`, the pinned sweeps).
* **The staging point is 0.30 m behind the dock, not 0.40** (D4): the
  simulator's straight verbs move whole 0.30 m cells, so a 0.40 m creep
  cannot close there. The car's verbs are continuous; step C may restore it.
  "Behind": the staging point is 0.30 m back along the dock's heading, so the
  creep is forward, ending facing the dock's heading.
* **Recovery on the way home:** a nav2 goal that aborts is followed by one
  guarded REVERSE (the runner's 3.31 back-off, as nav2's own recovery) and
  the goal again; two to the staging point, then two to the dock itself.
  Turns refused at the staging point (the pivot guard: too tight) go to the
  dock itself, where the robot started and so has turned before.
* **The dock pose is the start pose under every world** (`source: start`);
  the saved map's `dock_pose` through the bridge is 3.156.E (the bridge is
  outside B's Touches and beside C8's cancel rules). Under a reload the start
  is the dock within 3.50.D3's 0.5 m.
* **A refused goal retries every 1 s** (through the runner's `idle`) for
  `return_wait_s` (30 s), then `skipped`, `no_nav2`.
* **`room_reached` returns too**: a goal reached, like `found` (not in D2's
  list; the same reasoning).
* **The in-process body's verbs are exact** (`MockRobot(exact_verbs=True)`,
  opt-in, the sweep and its tests only): a straight verb moves its asked
  distance, as the car's do. Every other harness keeps whole cells.
* **The in-process navigator** is `tests/drop_sweep.py`'s VettedNav (it
  drives through the veto) planning through cells with a cell of clearance
  (nav2's inflated costmap, 0.35 m), its own plain path when none exists.
  Plain VettedNav hugged walls and its pivots were vetoed (3 of 4 traced
  returns aborted); the trip itself docked within 0.006 m where it arrived.

## D: recharge and resume (3.156.D; written 2026-11-08, before any code or measurement)

**Decisions, sharpening D7.**
* **D7a. Off until the Rover.** `recharge_resume` in `brain:` config, default
  false (the runner's too): `resume_v` is a placeholder until 3.137.E and
  nothing yet sees a charger. Off is B's end exactly.
* **D7b. The cause.** Only a battery end charges: the return reserve's
  `battery:` reason or the floor (`battery_floor_v`). Never time, calls, the
  step backstop, or Reserve / Power-off (the server refuses autonomy).
* **D7c. The decided end is the fallback.** The trip home is B's `ReturnTrip`
  with the `out_of_budget` end held in `_pending_end`. Anything that stops
  the charge (a failed return, a Stop, no rise, the wall limit) ends the
  mission with that end. Only reaching `resume_v` drops it.
* **D7d. Charging.** `status.charge`. The time limit is paused only while
  charging, not on the drives (3.88's `_paused_s`), under
  `mission_wall_limit_s` (4 h, total). It leaves at `resume_v` (11.8 V,
  placeholder), two distinct samples at or over it. No rise of 0.05 V in
  `charge_stall_s` (300 s): `no_charge`. Moved over 0.25 m from the dock:
  `left_dock`. Less than `RESUME_MIN_S` (120 s) of the limit left on
  arrival: `no_time`, no charge. At most `MAX_CHARGES` (3) a mission.
* **D7e. No separate drive back.** The mission resumes at the dock with what
  it kept (explore's visited goals, refusals and retry list, the survey's
  rooms, the find's memory), minus the goal in flight, which is forgotten
  without counting as that place's failure. Its next goal is the drive
  back. A frontier search has no sequence to rejoin as a vacuum's rows do,
  and a leg to the stopping point is wasted when that point was a dead end.
* **D7f. A person during charging.** Nothing moves, so nothing would be
  refused: every 2 s the brain sends a `LOOK_CENTER` under its own driver
  name (the camera is already centred; no wheel moves), which the server
  arbitrates like any brain command. A Stop's latch or a D-pad's hold refuses
  it: `charge: cancelled`, the decided end, the robot left where it is.
* **D7g. The drain estimate stays B's 60 s span** (the orchestrator's
  condition: only if D needs it). C5 failed from an 11.0 V start, where the
  pack is already low before the first estimate; D's missions start at 12.0
  V or leave at `resume_v`. After a charge the samples restart, and the rate
  measured before it stands until a new 60 s span exists, so a resumed
  mission is never blind. E5 tests that reasoning; if E5 fails, an
  amendment comes before any re-run.
* **D7h. The stand-in.** `SimBattery(charge_v_per_s=...)`, rising only while
  `MockRobot` is at rest on a pad at its start pose (0.15 m). A pack never
  charged reads exactly as before.

**Harness.** `tests/resume_sweep.py`, in process, as `tests/dock_sweep.py`.
The first 30 of its starts (`scaled_house` x 10 starts x {find, survey},
`home_first_floor` x 5 starts x {find, survey}), `mission_time_limit_s`
1200, floor 10.5 V, 12.0 V at the start, charge 0.02 V/s. Arms: `base` (no
drain), `charge-0.002`, `charge-0.004`, `charge-0.008` (V/s, recharge on),
`nocharge-0.004` (B's end). Live: `demo_explore.stack()` through the
brain's HTTP API.
