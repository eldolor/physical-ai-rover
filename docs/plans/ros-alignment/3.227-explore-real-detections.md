# 3.227 Explore sees the target on real frames

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.227; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.76 review row E12 (acknowledged by the user 2026-10-13): the
`explore` policy reads `frame["detections"]` (sightings, `ExploreAgent._hits`)
and `frame["objects_visible"]` (found, through `sensed_scene` and
`MissionMemory`), which only the simulator fills. On the Rover a plain
explore find can never see its target and never ends `found`. 3.80.G already
gave the *tiered* policy's explore search (`tier_search: explore`) sightings
from the tiers' perception; plain `explore` -- the policy of ask mode (3.88 A,
`explore_ask`, plain only) and of a find run without the tiers -- has none.
This section gives it the same on-board perception (`brain/perceive.py`:
YOLOE crops -> CLIP -> the P >= 0.8 gate) on frames that carry no
`detections`. It unblocks 3.140's L5.2 (record what explore does on the car);
L4 and L5 themselves are not this section's.

## A. The design and the bars (written before building)

**Design.**

* **Where the source is chosen: the brain server, once, at the start**, by
  frame provenance (`_frames_are_simulated`, the rule 1.12 already uses for
  the tiered policy). A new key `brain.explore_sight`:
  * `auto` (default): a simulated camera keeps dev's `frame` source,
    unchanged; any other camera, on a find (a survey has no target), gets
    `perception`.
  * `perception`: perception everywhere; in the simulator that is the
    simulator's reported detections through `FrameReportedPipeline` (1.12:
    the sim tests the consumer, never the detector) -- the arm this section
    measures with.
* **What perception is: the tiered policy's own pipeline**, built by the same
  `pipeline_for` with the same `perception_*` keys, one copy per mission
  (a mission is either tiered or explore, never both, so no second YOLOE/CLIP
  is ever resident beside the tiers'), closed at the mission's end and on a
  refused start. A missing install refuses the start with a 400 naming the
  pip command, before any motor turns, as the tiered policy does.
* **How it reaches explore:** a vision_fn (`brain/explore_sight.py`,
  `PerceptionSight`) that returns the agent's own `sensed_scene` plus
  `_perception`, so it runs under B3.2's guard like every vision call, and
  explore runs with `sight_from: perception` -- 3.80.G's path, unchanged.
* **Identity at arrival:** plain explore has no identity tier. An arrival
  (centred, lidar under 0.40 m for two frames, 3.11) is never `found` on
  perception alone (3.47's rule). The mission ends **`arrived_unconfirmed`**
  at the first such arrival, its reason naming the missing tier, no paid
  call made and no late confirmation started (3.53's reconfirmer asks the
  cloud; this policy has none). In the simulator under `perception`, the
  geometry judges identity when `sim_arrival_confirm: geometry` (3.98), so the
  sweep can also read `found`.

**Measurement** (`evaluations/explore-sight-3227/sweep.py`, in process, through
`MissionRunner` -> `ExploreAgent` -> `SafetyController` -> `MockRobot`, nav2 as
`FakeNav`; the 21 houses of 3.80.G's sweep (`SWEEP_HOUSES`), target `red
backpack`, 1,200 sim-s limit; free, no model, no cloud):

* **F** -- dev's plain explore on sim frames (the baseline).
* **S** -- this branch, `explore_sight: auto`, on sim frames.
* **B** -- the gap as it stands: dev's plain explore on a *real-camera
  stand-in* (the same `MockRobot`, its frames stripped of `detections` and
  `objects_visible` and their provenance not `sim`).
* **R** -- this branch on the stand-in: the sightings from a perception
  stand-in that reads the stripped detections (`FrameReportedPipeline` on the
  unstripped frame -- the detector replaced by truth, the consumer real), no
  identity tier.
* **G** -- `explore_sight: perception` in the simulator with the geometry's
  identity (3.98).

"At the target": the robot's centre within 0.9 m of the nearest target cell's
centre when the mission ends (3.80.G's measure).

**Criteria.**

1. **The simulator unchanged.** S equals F on all 21 houses: the same
   outcome, sim seconds and goals sent, per house. Pinned in a test.
2. **The gap is real (the "before").** B: 0 of 21 missions end `found` or at
   the target with a sighting -- recorded as measured, whatever it is.
3. **Explore sees on real frames (progress).** R: missions ending
   `arrived_unconfirmed` at the target >= 90% of F's `found` count (rounded
   down), and >= 1.
4. **Never a false arrival or a false find (stability).** R: 0 missions end
   `found`; 0 end `arrived_unconfirmed` farther than 0.9 m from the target;
   0 paid calls (no cloud exists in the run).
5. **Time.** On houses where both F ends `found` and R at the target, R's
   median sim seconds <= 1.5 x F's median.
6. **G, reported** (not a bar): found count and median sim seconds beside F,
   so the cost of the perception source's stricter arrival (0.40 m, centred)
   against sim truth's sighting range is on record.
7. **The start's guards** (tests): a plain explore find on a non-sim camera
   with no perception install is a 400 naming `requirements-perception.txt`
   with no navigator or pipeline left open; a survey on a non-sim camera
   loads nothing; the pipeline is closed at the mission's end.
8. **A real-model run** in this container (YOLOE + CLIP on CPU): *reported
   only, if the optional install fits*; CPU rows never merged with GPU rows
   (P24). Its bars are 3.140 L4.2's, not this section's.

## B. Built and measured (2026-11-27)

**Built.** `brain/explore_sight.py` (`PerceptionSight`); `control/brain_server.py`
`_perception_pipeline()` (the tiered build, now shared) and
`_explore_sight_fn()`; `control/mission_runner.py` (`sight_from` from the
vision_fn, `bind_base`, `arrived_unconfirmed` on an untiered arrival, no late
confirmation for it); `brain.explore_sight` in `config/robot.yaml` and
`control/brain_config.py`; the policy and mission ENGINEERING specs.
`tests/test_explore_sight.py` (18).

**The sweep** (`evaluations/explore-sight-3227/sweep.py`, records in
`results/`; 21 houses, in process, free; wall 32-100 s an arm on this
4-core container):

| Arm | found | arrived_unconfirmed (at target / away) | exhausted | sighted missions | median sim s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F dev, sim frames | 20 | 0 | 1 | 17 | 18.5 |
| S this branch, `auto`, sim frames | 20 | 0 | 1 | 17 | 18.5 |
| B dev, real-camera stand-in | 0 | 0 | 21 | 0 | 93.5 |
| R this branch, stand-in | 0 | 19 (19 / 0) | 2 | 20 | 18.6 |
| G `perception` in the sim, geometry identity | 20 | 0 | 1 | 17 | 18.6 |

**Criteria.**

1. **Met.** S equals F on 21 of 21 houses (outcome, sim seconds, goals);
   `test_the_recorded_sweep_meets_section_a`, and the brain server's choice
   (`test_auto_on_a_simulated_camera_loads_nothing`).
2. **Met (the gap was real).** B: 0 sightings, 0 `found`, 21 `exhausted`
   (one ended 0.6 m from the target by chance, with no sighting).
3. **Met.** R: 19 `arrived_unconfirmed` at the target; bar >= 18 (90% of F's 20).
4. **Met.** R: 0 `found`, 0 `arrived_unconfirmed` away from the target
   (farthest 0.55 m), 0 paid calls (no cloud in the run).
5. **Met.** On the 19 houses where F found and R arrived, median sim s
   18.3 (F) vs 18.6 (R): 1.02x, bar 1.5x.
6. **Reported.** G found 20, median 18.6 s; 7 of 21 houses differ from F in
   sim seconds or goals (the perception source's close-in and arrival hold),
   none in outcome. G's found still comes from the frame's
   `objects_visible`, as in the `frame` mode: in the simulator the sim's
   sighting range ends the mission before the arrival rule's 0.40 m does.
7. **Met** (tests): a real-camera find with no install is a 400 naming
   `explore policy` and `requirements-perception.txt`, its navigator closed;
   a real-camera survey loads nothing; the pipeline is closed at the
   mission's end and on a start refused after the build.
8. **Not attempted.** The perception extras (`ultralytics`, `open_clip`,
   `torch`) are not installed in this container's `.venv`, and the
   detector's weights are fetched from GitHub releases, not huggingface.co;
   the pipeline is the tiered policy's own, whose CPU and GPU numbers stand
   (P22-P24, 3.33). L4.2 measures it on the Rover's frames.

**Known limits.**
* R's two misses: `procthor:val:27` (F also `exhausted`) and
  `procthor:val:9`, `exhausted` 0.67 m from the target, never centred under
  0.40 m, where F's sighting-range found succeeded.
* On the car the end is `arrived_unconfirmed`, by design: plain explore has
  no identity tier. Identity on the car is the tiered policy's
  (`tier_search: explore`, 3.140 L5.1); giving plain explore the local VLM's
  identity is a later decision, not taken here.
* The stand-in's detector is truth (1.12); how often YOLOE + CLIP sees the
  target on the Rover's frames is L4.2's to measure.
