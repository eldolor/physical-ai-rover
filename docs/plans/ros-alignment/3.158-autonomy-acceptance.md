# 3.158 Full autonomy accepted: missions and surveys in the house, unattended

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.158; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** The objective (the user, 2026-11-06): give the robot a mission or
send it on a survey and it does what it needs to do. Each piece has its own
section and bars; nothing yet says when the whole is done. This section is
that definition and the runs that test it.

**What it gathers** (each stays in its own section):
* safe ends on every failure: 3.145, 3.146, 3.147, 3.153 (a dead or hung
  brain, ROS or camera stops the robot);
* return to the dock and the return reserve: 3.156;
* explore's sightings from real perception: 3.157;
* surveys that finish and name rooms right: 3.70.S2 (labels on real
  frames), and the live survey that ran out of time (3.70 S1-S3);
* memory across missions: 3.50.D5, 3.50.D, 3.70.M1-M3;
* the Rover's numbers: braking and speed (6.9 (d)), 3.137.E's battery
  levels, 3.81.F's memory, 3.90.C's frames;
* the chaos soak (3.135.D) and the intervention log (3.99);
* from a robot vacuum's features (2026-11-06): no fall at a stair (3.159,
  required for C), rooms a person names (3.160), no-go zones a person draws
  (3.161), what it saw with photos (3.162), schedules (3.163, after D).

**Criteria (written in step A, before any run; CLAUDE.md section 7).**
Below, in 3.158.A. A missed bar is recorded as missed, never re-scoped; an
amendment is written before the run it affects.

## 3.158.A: what "fully autonomous" means, counted (written 2026-11-07, before any run)

**One sentence.** On the user's word ("find the X" or "survey the house"),
from the dock, the robot does the job, comes back to the dock, touches
nothing, falls down nothing, and needs no person, as often as the bars
below say, counted in the user's house.

### What is counted, and how each fact is known on the car

* **A mission** is one find or survey a person starts through the twin (or,
  after D, a schedule, 3.163), from the dock, to its end state (3.70) and
  its return (3.156's `return`). A retry is a new mission. Every mission
  started during a counted session is counted; none is dropped after the
  fact (a mission ended by a fault is a failure, not a non-run).
* **The house sheet**, written by the user with the session before C's
  first counted mission and frozen for C and D: the floors and rooms the
  robot may enter, by the user's names (3.160 where built), each drawn on
  the saved map; the stair edges and no-go areas (3.161); the dock; and the
  **target list** -- at least 10 objects that stay put during a session,
  with where each one is (room and a note), at least 4 of them small (a
  shoe or smaller) and at least 4 in a room other than the dock's. Plus 2
  **absent targets**: named objects that are not in the reachable house.
* **Truth on the car** (there is no simulator truth; each fact names its
  source):
  * `found` on the target: the user, in person, confirms the robot is at
    the named object when it reports `found` (the arrival frame shown on
    the twin beside it). A `found` at anything else is a **wrong found**.
  * a **contact**: any touch of the chassis on anything, seen by the user
    or on the run's video (a phone recording the run, kept on the user's
    machine), or read in the board's logs as a stall (3.35) or a vetoed
    creep that moved the robot; one contact per incident.
  * a **fall** or **overhang**: seen by the user or on the video (3.159's
    definitions: a wheel or the chassis over a drop).
  * **docked**: 3.156.C's car reading (charging seen on the board, 3.137.D),
    not the pose alone.
  * **interventions**: 3.99's log (`control.intervention_report`), each one
    tagged by the user with a reason -- `safety` (the robot was about to
    touch, fall or hurt), `stuck` (it was not getting anywhere), `wrong`
    (it was doing the wrong thing), `other` -- in the session's sheet.
  * **wall-clock and battery**: the mission's start and end (status), the
    board's pack voltage at start, end and docking (3.137's samples), and
    the energy from the board's input power (3.136's P14 way) per mission.
  * **rooms entered** (surveys): the SLAM pose's track against the house
    sheet's rooms on the saved map.
* **Records**: one row per mission in `evaluations/autonomy-3158/<step>/`
  (counts, outcomes, times, volts, tags). Maps, arrival frames, videos and
  the house sheet's drawing are the user's home: they stay on the user's
  machines and are never committed or uploaded (CLAUDE.md, 3.50.D).

### C: on the Rover, the user present (counted)

**The counts.** At least **30 find missions** (each of the 10 targets at
least twice, each absent target twice) and at least **5 surveys**, over at
least **3 separate days** (morning and evening light both present), all
from the dock. More missions may be run; all are counted.

touch fails, and a robot that touches nothing because it hardly moves
fails C1 and C14. Paid cloud calls per mission and their cost are recorded,
no bar (the tiers' cost bars are 3.80's).

### D: unattended (the stretch)

Only after every C bar is met, and only on the user's word (the section's
safety rule). **The stretch:** at least **10 missions in a row**, at least
2 of them surveys, started remotely by the user (the twin) or by 3.163's
schedule once built, with **no person in the house's rooms the robot may
enter**, over at least **4 hours** from its first start, the robot
recharging on the dock between missions (3.156.D where built; otherwise
the missions are spaced for the charge). A stretch passes only if:

| # | Metric | Bar |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Interventions | **0** (any intervention, of any tag, ends the stretch as failed) |
| D2 | Contacts, falls, overhangs (the video, the board's logs, the user's walk through the house after) | **0** |
| D3 | Wrong found | **0** |
| D4 | Find success in the stretch | **>= C1's measured rate minus 10 points**, and at least 6 finds |
| D5 | Every mission ends docked | **all** (a mission left off the dock ends the stretch) |
| D6 | Reserve never reached; every service up at the end (the board's units, `/health` both halves) | yes |
| D7 | Faults | **0** missions `failed`; a fault ends the stretch |

**D passes on two stretches that pass, on different days.** A failed
stretch is recorded with its cause and becomes its own section's fix (as
3.135 rules); the next stretch starts from zero.

### B: the rehearsal in the simulator (the protocol; B is not started here)

**Where and with what.** On the Jetson in sim mode with the board's own
configuration: `picar-stack`'s environment (the tiered policy,
`tier_search: explore`, the local tier on VE, `drive: ros`, `world: ros`,
the saved map of 3.50.D, `return_to_dock` (3.156.B), the drop guard
(3.159.B), `explore_goal_policy` as `dev` ships it), the cloud a $0
stand-in (3.80.E's). If the board is on the Rover and cannot run sim mode,
the desktop with a VE stand-in instead, recorded as such (its identity
numbers then say nothing of VE's). The house: `home_first_floor`, and
`home_first_floor_stairs` once 3.159.B builds it; a frozen **sim house
sheet** (its rooms, its 10 targets chosen by C's rule from its objects, 2
absent names) written before B's first mission.

**The runs.** C's counts (30 finds, 4 of them absent targets; 5 surveys),
started through the brain's HTTP API exactly as the twin starts them, from
the dock stand-in, with `sim/battery.py` draining at a placeholder rate;
then one D-style stretch (10 missions in a row, 2 surveys, no harness
action between them but starting the next). Truth is the simulator's:
`found` on the target from the sim's object at the arrival, contacts from
the footprint truth (`tests/footprint_sweep.py`), falls from 3.159's truth,
docked within 3.156's 0.10 m / 10 deg.

**B's bars are C's and D's numbers** (C1-C5, C7-C14 and D1-D7, read on sim
truth; C6 reported; C15 on the board only). A B miss is a finding for the
section that owns it, fixed or recorded before C; B meeting its bars is
**not** evidence for C.

**What the simulator cannot show** (why C and D exist, and why no B number
stands in for one):

| The sim | So B cannot speak for |
|---|---|
| Renders its own frames; its detections and depth are kinder than a camera (M1.5-M1.11: rays pass diagonal corners, odometry never drifts, turns run ~6x the car's rate) | C1's find rate (3.157.C), C7's contacts at real speeds |
| Sees a dropped object or a drop at once in its depth grid (3.136.C amendment 8) | C8 on a real stair edge (3.159.C), any stop latency |
| A dock stand-in pad, no charging, no physical entry (3.156 D6) | C10's docking, C13's battery and the reserve on a real pack |
| No lighting, no clutter that moves, no rugs that slip, few people or pets (`SIM_MOVERS`) | the variation C's three days and two lights are for |
| `home_first_floor` is a furnished test house, not the user's (CLAUDE.md 3a) | C5's rooms, C12's times in the user's house |
| The cloud a stand-in | the tiers' real cloud answers (cost, C2 on the cloud's word) |

**Safety.** No motor motion without the user present until step C's bars
are met; step D (unattended) only on the user's word.
