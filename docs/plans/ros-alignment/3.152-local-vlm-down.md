# 3.152 A dead local VLM: the brain tells unreachable from unable, and the unit restarts with a delay

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.152; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.135.B (amendment 17's open item) found that `picar-local-vlm`
allows 4 starts in 900 s (`StartLimitBurst=4`, `StartLimitIntervalSec=900`,
`install_services.sh`). After a fourth crash in 15 min, systemd leaves VE
stopped until someone runs `systemctl reset-failed`. VE is back 40.7-41.3 s
after each restart systemd does allow. The user said "you decide"; the
orchestrating session chose both fixes, in this one section (2026-11-04).

**What dev does today (read from the code before writing the criteria).**
* `brain/local_vlm.py` already tells absence from a hang:
  * a refused or dropped connection, or a 503 (loading, driver gone),
    raises `LocalVLMAbsent` at once;
  * only a hang, the client's own timeout (`deadline_s` + 2 s), comes back
    as status `timeout`, which `tier_local_max_unable` (2) counts as
    unable.
* `TieredVision` (`brain/tiered.py`) takes `LocalVLMAbsent` per question:
  * on a frame's question it counts `absent` and asks the cloud about the
    same frame (paid, within the cap);
  * on the arrival's question it escalates to the cloud.
* Nothing remembers the absence. Every later question tries VE again,
  at the local tier's pace (3.80.P's `tier_local_cold_search_after` 1).
  Nothing probes VE's free `/health`, and nothing tells the operator.
* So a stopped VE does not cost a 3 s wait per question, as the brief
  assumed; criterion 2 measures this on dev rather than assuming either
  way. The cost is a doomed request per question, and no sign of any of
  it on the twin. *Corrected by the records' review (2026-11-04):* this
  section first said each such question was also paid to the cloud at the
  local pace. The dev record shows otherwise: a question passes to the
  cloud only where its `cloud_ok` allows (the cloud's own pace), so dev's
  cloud calls and triggers already equal the no-local-tier mission's.

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

*The brain (step A), in process unless stated:*

1. **Down is remembered, and down is "no local tier".** The first
   `LocalVLMAbsent` from any question (a frame's, the direction or the
   arrival's) puts `local_vlm` in state `down`. While it is down:
   * no question is sent to VE: 0 requests to `/identity` or `/direction`
     at a stub VE over a down window of at least 30 frames;
   * every decision is the one a mission with no local tier configured
     makes on the same frames (its pace, its candidate steer, its arrival).
     A whole mission, VE down from its first question to its end, against
     the same mission with `tier_local_vlm_url` unset: the outcome, every
     arrival verdict, the cloud-call count and every trigger count are
     equal. (The question that found VE down is the one difference
     allowed: it counts `absent` and is answered by the cloud, as on dev.)
2. **No wait.** In the down window, a frame's local phase (the time from a
   question's dispatch to its decision) has p90 <= 0.2 s. It is measured
   on dev too, against a stub refusing connections, and both are recorded.
3. **Back on the first good probe.** While the state is `down`, the tier
   probes VE's free `/health` every `tier_local_probe_s` (default 5 s;
   `brain:` config; 0 or less is refused). The first reply with
   `ready: true` puts it `up`, and the next question goes to VE first. A
   reply with `ready: false`, an error, or no reply within the probe's 2 s
   timeout keeps it `down`. Bar: once the stub turns ready, `up` within one
   probe interval plus 1 s, and VE then answers the next frame question.
4. **Slow but alive is still the unable rule.** A stub whose `/health` says
   ready and whose questions time out: the state stays `up`, each question
   counts `timeout`, and the cloud is asked after
   `tier_local_max_unable` (2) unable answers, exactly as on dev (the
   counts equal dev's on the same frames).
5. **The cloud's guards hold.** In a down window: cloud calls never exceed
   the cap, and never two at once (the most calls open at the stand-in at
   any moment is 1).
6. **The rules are unchanged.** `HARD_RULES` equals dev's, and so does
   `describe_*tree()` for the three trees.
7. **Probe and thread hygiene.** Probes run only while the state is
   `down` and the tier is open (0 probes while `up` over 30 frames). No
   probe thread is alive 1 s after `TieredVision.close()`. A Stop during a
   probe pays nothing.
8. **The operator sees it.** The mission's status carries `local_vlm`:
   `{state: up | down | off, since, checked_at, probes}` (`off`: no local
   tier configured). The twin shows it:
   * in operator mode in plain words, worded in `web-twin/strings.js` per
     3.132 (M5 jargon 0 in the new states);
   * in Engineer mode by name.
   A UI test at 390 px, red first, and a phone-size screenshot of both.
9. **Live.** One tiered mission through the brain's HTTP API on the live
   local stack, with a stand-in VE stopped mid-mission and started again:
   `local_vlm` reads `down` and then `up`, VE answers again after `up`, the
   mission ends with an outcome, and 0 contacts.

*The unit (step B, on the board, only in a slot the orchestrator gives):*

10. **No hard start limit; restarts spaced by a measured delay.** The board
    runs systemd 249, so there is no `RestartSteps`. `picar-local-vlm` gets
    `StartLimitIntervalSec=0` and `RestartSec=D`, shipped through
    `install_services.sh` and read back from the board
    (`systemctl show`). D is chosen from a measured crash loop:
    * the loop: `memory_hog.py` holds enough memory that VE's load is
      OOM-killed each time, for 15 min, with the robot stack running a
      tiered mission;
    * each candidate D in {30, 60, 120} s; the smallest that meets every bar
      is shipped;
    * bars, as 3.80.E criterion 6: every OOM kill names a process of VE's
      (`oom_kill` delta and dmesg); 0 late wheel ticks over the 15 min; the
      robot server and the brain answer throughout; the stack's
      `MemAvailable` minimum outside each kill's own squeeze (5 s either
      side of it) is >= 500 MB;
    * after the hog ends, VE is back (`/health` ready) within D + 60 s
      with no `reset-failed`, and the brain's state reads `up` (criterion
      3).

    Every run, valid or not, goes into `docs/evaluations/BOARD-BENCH.md`.

**Amendment 1 (2026-11-04, after the first review of step A and before
criterion 9's live run or any recorded result of criteria 3 or 7).** The
review found that a probe blocked in its `/health` GET waits out the probe's
timeout, so with criterion 3's 2 s timeout a probe could outlive
criterion 7's "no probe thread alive 1 s after `close()`" (close no longer
joins it: a join held every Stop up to 1 s). The probe's timeout is
**0.8 s** (`brain/local_vlm.py` `PROBE_TIMEOUT_S`); criterion 3 reads "no
reply within the probe's 0.8 s timeout". No bar moved. Also from the
review, in criterion 8's states: with a local tier and no probe
(`local_health_fn` unset, a client without `healthy`) the state is
`unwatched`, not `up`, since nothing could ever say down.

**Amendment 2 (2026-11-04, after the harness's review and before any board
run of criterion 10).** The review of `evaluations/local-vlm-3152/` showed
that the criterion as written would partly measure the harness. Changes:
* *The drop-in is a runtime one*, in
  `/run/systemd/system/picar-local-vlm.service.d/`, which a reboot clears.
  A run killed hard can never leave the candidate on the production unit
  past a boot. The as-found snapshot compares only unit states, so it
  would not see one in `/etc`.
* *A run is valid only if it is a crash loop.* Each of these makes the run
  invalid, re-run once, not failed:
  * fewer than 3 kills of VE in the window;
  * `NRestarts` not rising by at least as many;
  * a mission that errored.
* *Kills are attributed by the kernel.* Each `oom-kill:` line's
  `task_memcg` must be `picar-local-vlm.service`. This replaces matching
  PIDs sampled at 1 Hz, which misses a short-lived driver.
* *The hog runs in its own process at `oom_score_adj` 900*: above the
  stack's -900 and the ROS container's 0, below VE's 1000. With VE
  resident, VE is the kernel's pick; between VE's restarts, the hog is.
* *The memory bar* is 3.80.E criterion 6's 500 MB, applied where 3.80.E
  defined it: a loaded window with VE resident and no drill. That window
  is the 60 s after VE is ready again once the hog has ended. During the
  loop each VE load drives `MemAvailable` toward 0 for many seconds before
  its kill, a ramp the loop itself makes (VE is left 256 MB short). So the
  loop's own floor is recorded but has no bar; the stack's health during
  it is judged by the kills (VE only), the wheel loop and the servers.
* *Servers answering* is sampled only while a mission runs: a mission's
  own start and stop are not outages.
* *Late wheel ticks* are summed over the missions that overlap the window.

No bar was loosened for any candidate after seeing a number: none has
run.

**Amendment 3 (2026-11-04, after the harness's second review, still before
any board run).**
* *Whose kill counts.*
  * A kill of VE (`task_memcg` `picar-local-vlm.service`) is the loop.
  * A kill of the hog is the harness's own process, so it fails no bar,
    but it ends the window early (below).
  * A kill of any other process is a **failure** of "every OOM kill names a
    process of VE's", recorded as failed, never as invalid. That holds in
    the hog's growth too.
* *Invalid (re-run once), the complete list:*
  * fewer than 3 VE kills (the kernel's lines) or fewer than 3 restarts
    (`NRestarts`) in the window;
  * a window shorter than its 15 min (the hog killed or gone);
  * a mission overlapping the window that errored, or that has no
    `wheel_loop` reading;
  * the dmesg baseline unread at the start;
  * fewer `oom-kill:` lines than the kernel's `oom_kill` count rose by: the
    ring buffer dropped some, so a kill could be unattributed (third
    review);
  * fewer than 50 samples of a running mission in the loaded window (the
    window fell between missions; third review);
  * a harness error before the scoring, exit 3.

  The 3 VE kills must fall in the window: the kernel's count rising at
  least 3 times inside it, every line attributed.

  Amendment 2's draft code also had an 80%-of-samples "missions ran" rule;
  it is dropped, since it was never written here.
* *Servers answering* is sampled from the first moment a mission is seen
  running until that mission returns, whether or not the brain still
  answers then. A dead brain reads as down, never as "not running".
* *The loaded window* for the memory bar and the brain's state is the 60 s
  after a mission is running with VE ready again, which is at most 300 s
  after VE is back.

**Amendment 4 (2026-11-04, after the harness's fourth review, still before
any board run).**
* *The hog's `oom_score_adj` is 100, not 900.* The kernel's badness is
  rss + adj x RAM / 1000. At 900 a hog holding several GB outscores a VE
  that is still loading (adj 1000, small rss), so the kernel would kill the
  hog and no loop could form. At 100, VE is the pick whenever it is
  running; while VE waits out `RestartSec` there is no pressure, since its
  memory is free.
* *Kills are read from the kernel's journal* (`journalctl -k` since the
  run began), with their wall times. The ring buffer `dmesg` reads can
  drop early lines after many task dumps.
* *Invalid* also: a kill before the hog started (the candidate cannot
  have caused it), or a kill line the journal gives no kernel time for
  (it cannot be placed). The loaded window's floor is 30 samples, not 50,
  because the sampler's own reads lengthen its period under pressure.
* *Servers answering* is scored through the loaded window as well as the
  crash-loop window.

**Amendment 5 (2026-11-04, the orchestrating session's, after d30's run and
before d60's).** d30's 4 late ticks look like each kill's own squeeze
(3.80.E criterion 6 saw a 0.127 s tick the same way), not like the restart
delay; a longer D only spaces the squeezes out.
* *Recorded for every candidate:* late ticks in total, per VE kill in the
  window, and per hour of window, from the missions overlapping it.
* *If every candidate fails "0 late wheel ticks"*, the section says so, and
  recommends the D with the fewest late ticks per hour (ties: the
  smaller D). Nothing is shipped by default: the choice goes back to the
  orchestrating session.
* d30's run is scored as it ran; nothing in its bars changes.

**Amendment 6 (2026-11-04, after d60's first run and before any re-run).**
d60's first run was invalid, with 2 VE kills (1 in the window). The hog
held a fixed size (its first kill + 256 MB), but the stack's memory moves:
m000 ended 81 s into the window, the memory its stack freed let VE's next
load fit, and VE then stayed up to the end. d30's loop paused the same way
for ~270 s. Change: while it holds, the hog watches VE's /health. If VE has
been ready for 20 s, it grows again until VE's next kill and adds 256 MB
(each top-up is recorded). A top-up grows no further if VE leaves without
an OOM kill, and stops at the hold's end. A kill of anything but VE while it
holds or tops up ends the hog at once, releasing everything (as in its
growth), and the harness scores the kill as amendment 3 says. Nothing else changes. d30's run was
under the old hog and is kept as it ran. d60 is re-run once under this one
(amendment 2's re-run). Whether d30 is re-run under it too, for like for
like, is the orchestrating session's call.

**Step A, results (2026-11-04): criteria 1-9 met.**
