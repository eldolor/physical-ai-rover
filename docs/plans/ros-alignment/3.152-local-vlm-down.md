# 3.152 A dead local VLM: the brain tells unreachable from unable, and the unit restarts with a delay

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.152; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.135.B (amendment 17's open item) found that `picar-local-vlm`
allows 4 starts in 900 s (`StartLimitBurst=4`, `StartLimitIntervalSec=900`,
`install_services.sh`). After a fourth crash in 15 min, systemd leaves VE
stopped until someone runs `systemctl reset-failed`. VE is back 40.7-41.3 s
after each restart systemd does allow. The user said "you decide"; the
orchestrating session chose both fixes, in this one section (2026-11-04).

**What dev does today (read from the code before writing the criteria).**
* `brain/local_vlm.py` already tells absence from a hang:
  * a refused or dropped connection, or a 503 (loading, driver gone),
    raises `LocalVLMAbsent` at once;
  * only a hang, the client's own timeout (`deadline_s` + 2 s), comes back
    as status `timeout`, which `tier_local_max_unable` (2) counts as
    unable.
* `TieredVision` (`brain/tiered.py`) takes `LocalVLMAbsent` per question:
  * on a frame's question it counts `absent` and asks the cloud about the
    same frame (paid, within the cap);
  * on the arrival's question it escalates to the cloud.
* Nothing remembers the absence. Every later question tries VE again,
  fails and is paid for, at the local tier's pace (3.80.P's
  `tier_local_cold_search_after` 1, not the cloud's 6). Nothing probes VE's
  free `/health`, and nothing tells the operator.
* So a stopped VE does not cost a 3 s wait per question, as the brief
  assumed; criterion 2 measures this on dev rather than assuming either
  way. The cost is a doomed request per question, the cloud asked about
  each at the free tier's pace, and no sign of any of it on the twin.

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

*The brain (step A), in process unless stated:*

1. **Down is remembered, and down is "no local tier".** The first
   `LocalVLMAbsent` from any question (a frame's, the direction or the
   arrival's) puts `local_vlm` in state `down`. While it is down:
   * no question is sent to VE: 0 requests to `/identity` or `/direction`
     at a stub VE over a down window of at least 30 frames;
   * every decision is the one a mission with no local tier configured
     makes on the same frames (its pace, its candidate steer, its arrival).
     A whole mission, VE down from its first question to its end, against
     the same mission with `tier_local_vlm_url` unset: the outcome, every
     arrival verdict, the cloud-call count and every trigger count are
     equal. (The question that found VE down is the one difference
     allowed: it counts `absent` and is answered by the cloud, as on dev.)
2. **No wait.** In the down window, a frame's local phase (the time from a
   question's dispatch to its decision) has p90 <= 0.2 s. It is measured
   on dev too, against a stub refusing connections, and both are recorded.
3. **Back on the first good probe.** While the state is `down`, the tier
   probes VE's free `/health` every `tier_local_probe_s` (default 5 s;
   `brain:` config; 0 or less is refused). The first reply with
   `ready: true` puts it `up`, and the next question goes to VE first. A
   reply with `ready: false`, an error, or no reply within the probe's 2 s
   timeout keeps it `down`. Bar: once the stub turns ready, `up` within one
   probe interval plus 1 s, and VE then answers the next frame question.
4. **Slow but alive is still the unable rule.** A stub whose `/health` says
   ready and whose questions time out: the state stays `up`, each question
   counts `timeout`, and the cloud is asked after
   `tier_local_max_unable` (2) unable answers, exactly as on dev (the
   counts equal dev's on the same frames).
5. **The cloud's guards hold.** In a down window: cloud calls never exceed
   the cap, and never two at once (the most calls open at the stand-in at
   any moment is 1).
6. **The rules are unchanged.** `HARD_RULES` equals dev's, and so does
   `describe_*tree()` for the three trees.
7. **Probe and thread hygiene.** Probes run only while the state is
   `down` and the tier is open (0 probes while `up` over 30 frames). No
   probe thread is alive 1 s after `TieredVision.close()`. A Stop during a
   probe pays nothing.
8. **The operator sees it.** The mission's status carries `local_vlm`:
   `{state: up | down | off, since, checked_at, probes}` (`off`: no local
   tier configured). The twin shows it:
   * in operator mode in plain words, worded in `web-twin/strings.js` per
     3.132 (M5 jargon 0 in the new states);
   * in Engineer mode by name.
   A UI test at 390 px, red first, and a phone-size screenshot of both.
9. **Live.** One tiered mission through the brain's HTTP API on the live
   local stack, with a stand-in VE stopped mid-mission and started again:
   `local_vlm` reads `down` and then `up`, VE answers again after `up`, the
   mission ends with an outcome, and 0 contacts.

*The unit (step B, on the board, only in a slot the orchestrator gives):*

10. **No hard start limit; restarts spaced by a measured delay.** The board
    runs systemd 249, so there is no `RestartSteps`. `picar-local-vlm` gets
    `StartLimitIntervalSec=0` and `RestartSec=D`, shipped through
    `install_services.sh` and read back from the board
    (`systemctl show`). D is chosen from a measured crash loop:
    * the loop: `memory_hog.py` holds enough memory that VE's load is
      OOM-killed each time, for 15 min, with the robot stack running a
      tiered mission;
    * each candidate D in {30, 60, 120} s; the smallest that meets every bar
      is shipped;
    * bars, as 3.80.E criterion 6: every OOM kill names a process of VE's
      (`oom_kill` delta and dmesg); 0 late wheel ticks over the 15 min; the
      robot server and the brain answer throughout; the stack's
      `MemAvailable` minimum outside each kill's own squeeze (5 s either
      side of it) is >= 500 MB;
    * after the hog ends, VE is back (`/health` ready) within D + 60 s
      with no `reset-failed`, and the brain's state reads `up` (criterion
      3).

    Every run, valid or not, goes into `docs/evaluations/BOARD-BENCH.md`.

**Amendment 1 (2026-11-04, after the first review of step A and before
criterion 9's live run or any recorded result of criteria 3 or 7).** The
review found that a probe blocked in its `/health` GET waits out the probe's
timeout, so with criterion 3's 2 s timeout a probe could outlive
criterion 7's "no probe thread alive 1 s after `close()`" (close no longer
joins it: a join held every Stop up to 1 s). The probe's timeout is
**0.8 s** (`brain/local_vlm.py` `PROBE_TIMEOUT_S`); criterion 3 reads "no
reply within the probe's 0.8 s timeout". No bar moved. Also from the
review, in criterion 8's states: with a local tier and no probe
(`local_health_fn` unset, a client without `healthy`) the state is
`unwatched`, not `up`, since nothing could ever say down.
