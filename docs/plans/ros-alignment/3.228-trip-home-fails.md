# 3.228 The trip home and frontier goals fail on the Rover: why

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.228; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** In the Rover's supervised explore surveys (3.140: 2026-11-26
night and 2026-11-27 morning) the trip home failed 4 of 4 times
(`nav2_aborted`; 1.61 m in 168 s and 1.39 m in 149 s on 11-27; on 11-26
"a FORWARD vetoed at 0.0 cm"), and survey 2 of 11-27 sent 10 goals of
which 9 failed. This step finds out why from the run logs, with evidence,
and proposes fixes. A diagnosis: no code changes here.

**Data.** The brain's `/mission/status` (`b`), the robot server's
`/health` (`h`) and `/pose` (`p`), sampled every ~5 s:
`~/survey3224a.jsonl` (survey 1, 120 samples, 601 s) and
`~/survey3224b.jsonl` (survey 2, 111 samples, 568 s), plus
`~/survey3224b-odom.jsonl` (survey 2's `/odometry` every 0.56 s), copied
from the board under its lock to the desktop outside the repo (they hold a
home's floor plan: never committed). Only the 11-27 surveys have traces;
the 11-26 traces were in the board's `/tmp` and are gone, as is its
journal from that boot (volatile) and the ROS container (removed after the
run). **So nav2's own status, the planner's and controller's results and
the costmaps were not recorded anywhere**: what nav2 did is inferred from
what reached the robot server (its refusal counts and last stop reason),
the pose, and the brain's log tail (the last 20 lines, de-duplicated across
samples).

**Method (written 2026-11-27 before the counts below; a first read of
both log tails came before it, so this fixes what is counted, not what was
looked at).**

* **Goals.** One row per goal in `b.explore.sent`, its window from the
  sample whose `last_event` names it to the next goal or the end. Counted:
  kind; how it ended (`reached`, `stalled` per explore's rule -- under
  `STALL_M` 0.15 m in `STALL_S` 20 s --, or cut by the time reserve);
  seconds; displacement from its start; the stop reasons the robot server
  recorded inside the window, by class (`astern_occluded`, `retrace`, a
  forward clamp off `scan_footprint`/`scan_path`, a turn's corner clamp,
  `preempted`, a verb `Blocked`), with the `safety_distance` count's rise
  attributed to the class of the sample's newest stop.
* **Trips home.** The same for each `b.return` from its `decided_at_s`:
  metres, seconds, the stop classes, and the brain's back-off line.
* **Escapes** (explore's REVERSE, CREEP, OPEN after a stall): the REVERSE
  and FORWARD refusals' distance and source, the degrees the OPEN turns
  added up to, and whether the pose then moved 0.15 m before the next goal.
* **Pose jumps:** any 5 s step faster than nav2's 0.20 m/s cap plus
  0.05 m/s (> 1.25 m), and survey 2's SLAM yaw against wheel yaw.
* **A finding is a cause only if** the traces show it on the failed goals
  or trips home, and the code says why; otherwise it is named as a
  hypothesis with the record that would settle it.

## Results (2026-11-27; the counting script and the repro beside the traces, outside the repo)

**The counts.**

| Survey | Goals sent | Reached | Stalled (explore's 20 s rule) | Cut by the reserve | Escapes | Trip home |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (06:42) | 7 | 3 | 3 | 1 | 3 | `nav2_aborted`, 1.61 m in 168 s, 0.53 m net |
| 2 (06:55) | 10 | 1 | 9 | 0 | 9 | `nav2_aborted`, 1.39 m in 149 s, 0.46 m net |

* **Survey 2's 9 failures are two places.** It started where survey 1's
  trip home died, (8.38, -1.74): goals 1-4 stalled there with the robot
  moved 0.02-0.43 m (140 s), until goal 5 got out and was reached. Goal 6
  drove 2.8 m into a second spot, (4.37, -4.60), under forward clamps
  (17.4-18.6 cm, `scan_footprint`); goals 7-10 stalled there (moved
  0.01-0.33 m), and the trip home began there. Both spots are where a
  `small_room` goal took the robot (goal 7 of survey 1 at (8.35, -1.60);
  goal 6 of survey 2 at (4.40, -4.95)).
* **Every escape was the same, 12 of 12:** REVERSE refused at 0.0 cm
  `scan_rear` (bar 20, then 3 at creep), FORWARD refused at 0.0 cm
  `scan_footprint` (bar 3), then the OPEN turns adding up to 183-381 deg
  (median 363; 4 quarter turns in 11 of 12): a full turn on the spot that
  ends facing where it began. Both trips home's back-offs: `REVERSE
  refused ... 0.0cm < min=20cm (scan_rear)`.
* **What nav2 did on the trips home** (the robot server's side): 6 and 4
  bursts of about 200 `astern_occluded` refusals each (1,399 and 800 in
  all) -- nav2's BackUp recovery at 20 Hz for its 10 s time allowance,
  refused every time; 16 and 15 five-second steps turning more than 30 deg
  (its Spin recovery, carried out); forward clamps in 0 and 5 samples
  (15.5-18.5 cm). Survey 1's trip home sent no forward that reached the
  vet after 455 s. The default behaviour tree (no custom XML in
  `picar_bringup`) retries spin/wait/back-up up to 6 times, then aborts;
  the brain's one back-off was refused, its second goal aborted the same
  way.
* **No pose jumps:** the fastest 5 s step is 0.19 m/s (cap 0.20); stuck,
  the pose holds within 0.03 m. Survey 2's wheel odometry stays still
  while the pose does (no slip into an obstacle). Wheel yaw differs from
  SLAM's by up to 25-95 deg per 5 s while driving (skid steer,
  `track_scrub` unmeasured): not a cause here, but 3.224's turn gate trusts
  that yaw.
* **Not recorded, so not counted:** nav2's goal status codes, the
  planner's and controller's results, the costmaps, and the keepout spots
  the robot server's wedge watch marked (its log lines went to the board's
  volatile journal).

**Causes, ranked by evidence.**

1. **The brain's safety layer has no self box, so every brain-side move
   reads the mast as an obstacle at 0.0 cm (certain: code, the traces and a
   repro).** 3.218.A gave the brain's controller the server's box through
   `RemoteRobot.lidar_self_box_cm()`, read by `_body_self_box()` off
   `type(robot)`. But `MissionRunner` hands the agent `_HaltGate(robot)`
   (`control/mission_runner.py` 1238), which has no such method: the
   agent's `SafetyController` gets `self_box_cm = None`, and so does
   `ReturnTrip`, built on `self.agent.safety` (2773). Unmasked, the mast's
   front edge (the box reaches x = +3.2 cm, ahead of `base_link`) lies
   "beside" the chassis in the forward corridor (0.0 cm), and astern the
   rear cone reads it (`scan_rear` 0.0) where the server says
   `astern_occluded` or `retrace`. So explore's escape is a no-op on the
   Rover: REVERSE and CREEP are refused by the brain before reaching the
   server, `_still_wedged()` is always true, and OPEN turns a full circle;
   `_front_open()` is never true; the return's back-off and creep are
   refused the same way. Repro (in process, `scaled_house`, the sim body's
   scan plus returns inside the Rover's box, open floor): the server's
   controller and a box-aware brain read forward 274.5 cm `depth_grid`,
   reverse 0.0 `astern_occluded`; the brain's as built reads **forward 0.0
   `scan_footprint`, reverse 0.0 `scan_rear`** -- the Rover's 12 of 12.
   3.218's test (`tests/test_lidar_self_box.py`) built a bare
   `RemoteRobot` and never went through the runner, so the suite stayed
   green. 11-26's "a FORWARD vetoed at 0.0 cm" fits this (no trace left to
   check it).
2. **nav2 has no way out of a tight spot on the Rover (strong, inferred
   from what reached the server).** It never drives astern
   (`allow_reversing: false`) and its BackUp recovery is refused
   `astern_occluded` (3.218); 3.224's retrace almost never applies, because
   by then the Spin recovery has turned the body off its track (two
   `retrace` stops sampled in survey 1, none in survey 2). Forward, the robot server
   holds a 20 cm bar that nav2's costmap does not know: nav2 drives into a
   nook until the server clamps it at 15-20 cm (both trap spots), then has
   only spinning left. The trips home spent about 70 s and 40 s on refused
   back-ups, and the trip began already in such a spot (survey 2: the spot
   where four goals had just stalled; survey 1: beside the `small_room`
   goal it was driving to).
3. **Why nav2 then sends no forward at all (hypothesis; not in the
   record).** After its spins, survey 1's trip home sent nothing forward
   that reached the vet in 145 s. Candidates, each consistent with the
   traces: (a) Regulated Pure Pursuit's collision check
   (`use_collision_detection`) or the planner refusing because the
   footprint sits in inscribed/lethal cost in a nook (inflation 0.35 m);
   (b) the robot server's wedge watch fencing the robot in with its own
   keepout discs -- one 0.30 m ahead after every 3 s of forward clamps,
   r 0.15 m, 300 s, up to 8, at whatever heading it faced (survey 1 had
   about 400 forward clamps in 20 s at (5.33, -2.33)). The record that
   settles it: nav2's goal results and behaviour-tree log, and the keepout
   spots, in the run's trace.
4. **Contributing: `small_room` goals lead into nooks.** 13 of 17 goals
   were `small_room`; both trap spots are at one. Not a cause on its own:
   with causes 1-2 fixed a nook is somewhere the robot can leave.

**Proposed fixes** (next steps, not built here; each with its own bars).

| Fix | What | Files | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 (cause 1) | `_HaltGate` passes `lidar_self_box_cm` through (or the runner gives the agent's controller the box explicitly), and a test that builds the agent through `MissionRunner` on a body reporting a box and asserts the agent's and `ReturnTrip`'s controllers carry it; the mast repro above as its red-first test | `control/mission_runner.py`, a new test (or `tests/test_lidar_self_box.py`), `docs/engineering/mission/ENGINEERING.md` | S: ~2 h with reviews. Risky path (safety): `/code-review` and the Thermos pass |
| F2 (cause 3's record) | The survey trace records nav2's goal result and the behaviour tree's recovery events (the bridge already sees the action result), and the robot server's keepout spots on `/health`; the board keeps its journal for a run (`Storage=persistent`) or the run tees the stack's log to `~` | `service/slam/src/picar_bridge/`, `robot/server.py`, the 3.140 run steps | S-M: ~3-4 h; touches the bridge wall (a registered route) |
| F3 (cause 2) | Give nav2 a way out that the rear's blindness allows: a behaviour tree whose recovery tries the brain-side escape's moves in nav2's place -- spin, then a short forward creep -- and a BackUp no longer than the retrace room (<= 0.10 m) only when the server answers `retrace`; or footprint padding so nav2 stops where the server would (it then never plans into the last 20 cm) | `service/slam/src/picar_bringup/config/nav2.yaml` (+ a BT XML), `robot/wedge_watch.py` if (b) of cause 3 holds | M: 1-2 days; judged on `scaled_house` with a self box, then the Rover |
| F4 (causes 2, 4) | The trip home starts with the escape when the robot is wedged (it does not today), and the time reserve counts that time; `small_room` goals kept a chassis half-length plus the safety bar from walls | `brain/dock.py`, `brain/explore.py` | M: ~1 day |

F1 first: it is small, certain, and every later measurement depends on
the escape doing what it says. Then F2 before F3, so F3 is chosen on
nav2's own record rather than on cause 3's guesses. A simulator run of the
full stack with a mast in the sim's scan would exercise F1 end to end; the
sim draws no mast today, so it needs one fake (a scan wrapper, as in the
repro).
