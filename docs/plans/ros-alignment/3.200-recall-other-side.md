# 3.200 A recall whose view is refused tries another side

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.200; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the user, 2026-11-21.) 3.70.M2's recall (3.172 F5: a find's first goal
goes to where X was last seen) gets one try per place. M2 recorded two finds it
cost:
* **Run 2, `FakeNav`, k 11 (trash can, traced):** the recall goal succeeded
  0.41 m from the trash can, every turn to face it was clamped by the pivot guard
  (90 down to 6 degrees), the place was spent unlooked-at, and on a fully surveyed
  map no frontier brought the robot back: `exhausted` 9.67 m away.
* **Live, k 3 (laptop bag):** the recall goal stalled about 6.6 m short, wedged;
  frontiers took 22 goals to find the bag.
* **Run 1, `InflatedNav`:** surveyed Found 7 of 20, recall first 17 of 20, the
  stand-in nav aborting goal after goal in both arms.

M2 recorded the candidate, not built: a recall whose face is refused or aborted
keeps its place for an approach from another side.

**What 3.175.E already says, and why this is not E again.** E retried a recall goal
that ended `aborted`/`stalled`, once, from band floor at least 0.3 m from the first
point. Every bar it set failed (A3 30 against 23; fresh found 12 of 23 unchanged;
stale and after found fell 21 to 17 and 19 to 14), and it did not ship. Its reading:
the stand-in's aborts at those places are structural, a point 0.3 m over meets the
same failed plan, and two failed goals plus their escapes cost the frontiers.
This section differs in two ways, each measured apart:
* **R1, the look refused** (not in E): the goal *arrived* and the turn to face the
  place was refused down to `FACE_MIN_TURN_DEG`. The place was never looked at.
  Nothing structural about the path is in question; the standing spot is.
* **R2, R1 plus a navigator failure** (E's case, changed): the second approach must
  come from **another side** of the place, band floor whose bearing from the place
  is at least `RECALL_SIDE_DEG` (90 degrees) from the first goal's, not 0.3 m over.
  If no band floor on another side is on the map, the place is spent, as on `dev`;
  never the same side again (E's lesson).

A turn that completes and does not see the target is a look: the place is spent and
the stale rule (3.175.B) stands, unchanged. So are a refusal before the goal is
taken (3.172's rule), a charge trip (3.175.E review), and every non-recall goal.

## 3.200.A: criteria and design (written 2026-11-21, before any code or run)

**Design.**
* `brain/explore.py` only. A recall's FACE carries kind `"recall"`; when its turns
  are refused down to `FACE_MIN_TURN_DEG` (today: "an unseen patch: give up the
  look"), the place goes back first in `recall` with its first goal's point
  remembered. Under R2 the same happens to a recall goal ending `aborted`,
  `stalled` or `rejected` (not `canceled`, `lost`, nor a charge trip: E's review),
  after the failure handling and escape that goal already gets.
* The next goal to a place that was tried goes to band floor
  (`APPROACH_NEAR_M`..`APPROACH_FAR_M`, reachable) whose bearing from the place
  differs from the first goal's by at least `RECALL_SIDE_DEG`; nearest by path among
  those. None: the place is spent (event `recall_spent`).
* **N = 1 retry:** at most `RECALL_SIDES` = 2 recall goals per place per mission
  (a "here" face counts as one), so at most 6 per mission (`RECALL_MAX` 3).
* No line of `robot/`, `control/` or `brain/dock.py` changes. Frozen references keep
  their exact answers; if the rule would change one, stop and report.

**Instrument.** 3.70.M2's sweep (`evaluations/find-last-seen-370/sweep.py`)
unchanged in house, seeds, starts, detector, targets rule, limits and scorer, plus:
* a `rule` argument (`dev`, `R1`, `R2`) that sets the explore constant before the
  survey and every find (the survey sends no recall: the survey row must match);
* row fields `recall_unlooked` (recall faces given up), `recall_retries` (second
  goals to a place), `recall_spent` (places with no other side), and the most
  recall goals to one place.

Runs (rows under `evaluations/recall-other-side-3200/`, 4 workers on this cloud's
4 cores, no bar on wall-clock):
* `dev-inflated`, `dev-fake`: today's `dev` (`b673d89a`) as the control, since
  `dev` changed after M2's runs (3.191 among others);
* `R1-inflated`, `R1-fake`, `R2-inflated` (R2 under `FakeNav` too if R2 is the one
  to ship).

| # | Metric | Bar | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | `InflatedNav`, surveyed Found | **>= 9 of 20** (run 1's 7 + 2) **and >= today's `dev` + 2** | progress |
| A2 | `InflatedNav`, recall first (M2's first-goal read) | **>= 17 of 20** (run 1's). This rule acts only after the first recall, so it cannot raise it by construction; the bar is that it does not fall | stability |
| A3 | `InflatedNav`, surveyed finds `dev` found and the rule lost (paired by k) | **<= 1** | stability (cost) |
| A4 | `FakeNav`, surveyed Found and recall first | Found **20 of 20** (run 2: 19; k 11 is the traced face refusal), recall first **20 of 20** | progress |
| A5 | Recall goals to one place in any mission (both navs, both arms) | **<= 2** (`RECALL_SIDES`); per mission **<= 6** | stability (cap) |
| A6 | No memory unchanged: the survey row and every unsurveyed row (outcome, search s, goals, steps, distance) | **identical** to today's `dev` under the same nav | stability |
| A7 | Wrong `found`; contacts | **0; 0**, every run | stability |
| A8 | Unit: a refused recall face keeps the place for one goal from another side, then spends it; a completed face spends it; no other side spends it; R2's abort path; the frozen references and related suites pass unedited | yes | stability |
| A9 | One live find on the ROS stack (this section's image via `build-cloud.sh`) on M2's live survey ground, or recorded as not run with why | run, or recorded | evidence |

**Which ships (fixed now).** A rule ships only if it meets A1 and A3-A8. If R1 and
R2 both do, R2 ships only with surveyed Found under `InflatedNav` at least 2 above
R1's (it costs a second failed drive where E saw that cost); otherwise R1. If
neither does, nothing ships, `dev` keeps one try, and the data are the result
(3.175.E's reason holds, or the one this section finds).

**What this cannot show.** The stand-in navs are not nav2: `FakeNav` hops and sets
the robot where nav2 might not; `InflatedNav` aborts more than nav2 did live (M2 live
4 of 5 recalls reached). A pivot guard refusal live depends on the real footprint.
