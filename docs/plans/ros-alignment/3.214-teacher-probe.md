# 3.214 A larger open teacher for the local VLM: Qwen2.5-VL-72B and Alpamayo 2 Super measured on our frames first

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.214; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** The user, 2026-11-22, after a desk evaluation of NVIDIA's Alpamayo
2 Edge and Alibaba's Qwen-Drive-1.0 (neither adopted: both are road-driving
models whose trajectory heads have no job here, and neither fits the
board): "Look at options to fine tune Qwen 2.5 VLM by using the 72B
teacher", then, of Alpamayo 2 Super as the teacher, "Let's do 1&2":

1. measure a candidate teacher on the pinned identity frames before any
   training uses it (the cheap candidate: Qwen2.5-VL-72B);
2. try Alpamayo 2 Super on single frames from our sets, on one rented
   80 GB GPU (about $5-10 estimated; the user's go).

**What this section is.** A measurement only. It trains nothing and
changes nothing on the robot. It answers one question per model: on our
frames, is it better than the model it would teach? Its result is an
amendment proposed to 3.188 (identity) or 3.206 (direction), or a recorded
"no".

**What is known (recorded, not re-tested here):**

* **Every larger model measured on identity did worse than the student.**
  On 3.48's lookalike subset Opus 4.5 scored 67.9% precision and GPT-6 Astra
  56.1% (3.80.Q), against VE's 97.2%. That is why 3.188 forbids a model's
  answers as identity labels. Neither model here has been measured.
* **The student's identity question is a grounding question.** VE is asked
  `tools/jetson/bench_identity_llama.py`'s `PROMPT` ("Output the bounding
  box of the {target} as JSON ... If it is not visible, output []"; on the
  board, `/identity` with `box: true`, 3.92.A). The score is P(a box) at
  the token that opens the list (`p_localised`, same file); the box's
  third is its steer. One question therefore yields both answers.
* **VE on the pinned 2,261 frames** (3.188.A): 26 false yeses and 53
  in-view frames without a yes (79 mistakes), precision 94.9%, recall
  90.1%. Its errors are confident: 0.3% of frames fall in the unable band.
* **Steering with the target in view** (3.80.I, S1's 60 frames): Astra 56,
  the local order 51, VE's box 42.
* **Qwen2.5-VL-72B** (desk research, 2026-11-22; licence texts not read at
  source): open weights under the Qwen License (the 3B is under the Qwen
  Research License); about 148 GB at BF16 and 45-50 GB at 4-bit AWQ
  (third-party figures); the same tokenizer family as the student, so its
  first-token distribution can be a training target directly.
* **Alpamayo 2 Super** (its model card and repository): 34B (a 32B Cosmos 3
  Super backbone and a 2.3B trajectory expert), OpenMDW-1.1 weights. Its
  validated tasks (visual question answering, 2D grounding, auto-labelling)
  take six cameras with four timestamped frames each; auto-labelling also
  takes the ego trajectory. Measured peak 72,115 MiB on one 80 GB H100 at
  seven cameras. **One indoor frame from one camera is outside anything
  NVIDIA validated**, and its tokenizer differs from Qwen's, so only its
  text (a verdict, a box) could ever be a label.

## Design

* **Frames: the pinned sets, unchanged.** No new set is drawn.
  * *I-2261:* 3.188's `evaluations/qwen-finetune-3188/frames.json` (21
    walks, 2,261 frames, 535 in view, keys hash `163dec93a76a`), labels the
    people's.
  * *I-299:* 3.48's arrival frames (`overlap-299.json`; all inside I-2261;
    74 in view).
  * *Lookalikes:* `lookalikes.json`'s 34 teal-bin and 7 taupe-bag frames.
  * *S1 and S2:* 3.80.I's 60 in-view and 40 out-of-view steering frames
    (`evaluations/tiered-380/I/sets.json`, `labels-s1.json`), each also
    mirrored.
* **The question: the student's own.** The identity `PROMPT` above, word
  for word, at VE's image size (128-512 image tokens). A yes is
  `p_localised`'s score >= 0.5 (`LOCAL_YES_AT`), as VE is scored. The
  box's third is the steer, scored by 3.80.I's `score.py` rule.
* **Qwen2.5-VL-72B runs on the rented host by default,** from the official
  4-bit AWQ checkpoint, because the token log-probabilities are needed and the
  frames then stay inside the account. An API provider is used only on the
  user's word: the frames are photos of a home, and the provider must
  return the top token log-probabilities `p_localised` reads.
* **Alpamayo 2 Super is given one frame as a single front-wide camera
  frame,** through its visual-question and grounding modes (its
  repository's `vqa.ipynb` path). Asked: the identity question as a yes/no
  question, and a grounding request for the target. If the packaged
  processor refuses one camera and one frame, the frame is repeated to
  fill the six-camera, four-frame profile; that is recorded as a second
  arm, not hidden.
* **A pilot before each full run:** five frames, to prove the answers
  parse and to measure seconds per frame. The pilot's projected cost for
  the full run is reported before the full run starts.
* **Like for like with the student.** VE's numbers are the board's INT4
  engine on the same frames, read from the committed records
  (`evaluations/tier-calibration-392/`'s `qwen-VE.jsonl`,
  `evaluations/tiered-380/I/qwen-VE.jsonl`). The teacher's precision is not
  compared with any other runtime's.
* **Nothing is uploaded but frames to the host's own disk,** from the
  recordings bucket; nothing is committed but answers, keys and counts
  (never frames).

## Criteria (written 2026-11-22, before any host, call or frame; CLAUDE.md section 7)

**The Qwen2.5-VL-72B, identity, on I-2261:**

1. **Fewer mistakes (progress):** false yeses + in-view frames without a
   yes **<= 59** (0.75 x VE's 79).
2. **The precision bar (stability):** yes-precision **>= 95%**, with recall
   **>= 90.1%** (VE's).
3. **Lookalikes:** **0 yes** on the 34 teal-bin frames and on the 7
   taupe-bag frames (VE: 1/34 and 0/7).
4. **No object worse:** on each of the six objects, false yeses <= VE's and
   missed in-view frames <= VE's (3.188.A's table).
5. **Something to teach:** on the frames where it and VE give different
   answers, it is right on **>= 75%**, over at least 30 such frames. Fewer
   than 30 is recorded as "nothing to teach" and counts as failed.

**The Qwen2.5-VL-72B, direction, on S1 and S2:**

6. **A teacher worth copying** (3.206's criterion 2, its rule unchanged): a
   label is kept when the mirrored answer is the original with left and
   right swapped; **>= 95% right on what is kept** and **>= 70% of S1
   kept**. On S2 (target out of view) a box on **<= 2 of 40**.

**Alpamayo 2 Super:**

7. **It runs outside its contract:** a parseable answer on **>= 95%** of
   the frames asked, in each of the two modes. A pilot that cannot be made
   to accept our frames ends the Super half there, recorded as failed.
8. **Identity, on I-299:** fewer mistakes than VE on the same frames (VE:
   precision 97.2%, recall 94.6%), precision **>= 95%**, and **0 yes** on
   the 34 teal-bin frames.
9. **Direction, on S1 and S2:** criterion 6's rule and numbers.

**Both:**

10. **Cost reported and bounded:** the host's price, hours and dollars,
    measured. **The whole section stops at $12** (the sums the user
    approved) and reports before more is asked. If a pilot projects more
    than that, the full run does not start.

A failed criterion is recorded as failed, not re-scoped.

**What each result leads to (decided now):**

* Criteria 1-5 all met: a soft-label arm is proposed for 3.188.B (the
  72B's P(a box) as the training target, beside the plain
  fine-tune, on 3.188's folds and bars). Any one failed: no model teaches
  identity, as 3.188 stands.
* Criterion 6 met: the 72B is proposed as a teacher arm in 3.206.B.
* Criteria 7-8 or 7 and 9 met: Super is proposed likewise, its text only.
* Each proposal is an amendment in that section's file, made by that
  section's holder or on the user's word; nothing here edits them.

## Amendment 1 (2026-11-23, before any host, call or frame)

**The host is Hugging Face Jobs, not EC2.** The user, 2026-11-22: asked
whether Bedrock or Hugging Face offer the models; 2026-11-23: "Yes" to the
frames going to Hugging Face's machines for this run, and a token saved on
the cloud environment through the secure form (never in chat or the repo).

* **Why.** Neither model is a managed Bedrock model (its Qwen lineup is
  Qwen3; Custom Model Import caps multimodal weights under 100 GB).
  Hugging Face hosts the 72B only through third-party providers, and no one
  hosts Alpamayo. EC2 needed rights and a P-instance quota this session
  lacks. Hugging Face rents GPUs by the minute to any account with credit
  (`GET /api/jobs/hardware`, read 2026-11-23): an 80 GB A100 at $2.50 an
  hour (`a100-large`), a 96 GB RTX Pro 6000 at $2.75, a 141 GB H200 at
  $5.00.
* **One job per model, each with a timeout** (a job that reaches it is
  stopped and so is its billing). `a100-large` first for both; if Super
  does not fit in 80 GB, the 96 GB or the 141 GB flavour, recorded.
* **Frames reach the job by presigned S3 links** (hours-long expiry),
  listed in a manifest the job fetches; nothing is stored in a Hugging Face
  repository, and the job's disk ends with the job. Answers come back by
  presigned upload to the deploy bucket's `gpu-eval/teacher-probe-3214/`
  and are echoed in the job's log.
* **The 72B's runtime:** vLLM serving the official AWQ checkpoint; the
  request is `bench_identity_llama`'s own (the image, then `PROMPT`;
  temperature 0, top 20 log-probabilities, 64 tokens), image size held to
  128-512 image tokens. The job stores the reply and its token
  log-probabilities; `p_localised` and `parse_box` score them here.
* **Super's input:** its repository's `prepare_vqa_inputs` path, which
  builds the prompt from whatever cameras the sample holds, given one
  camera (id 1, front wide) and one frame. **Greedy decoding:** its
  `generate_text` samples at temperature 0.6, and a measurement needs an
  answer that repeats. Two questions a frame:
  * identity: "Is there {target} visible in this image? Answer yes or
    no." A yes is a reply that starts with "yes".
  * grounding, in its own question's form ("Find the white lead vehicle
    ... and return its bounding box in JSON"): "Find the {target} and
    return its bounding box in JSON." The box is read by its repository's
    own rule (`viz_utils`'s parser and `auto` coordinate mode) against the
    processed image's size; the centre's third is the steer.
  * **Its steer counts only where its own identity answer is yes**
    (criterion 9 is judged on that; the ungated figure is reported
    beside it). A grounding question presumes the object is there.
  * The six-camera fill (design) is run only if the single-frame arm
    fails criterion 7 on the pilot.
* **Spend:** unchanged. $12 for the section; each pilot reports its
  projected cost before its full run.
