# 3.163 Missions and surveys on a schedule

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.163; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the user, 2026-11-06: features of a robot vacuum (Dreame) worth leveraging, for full autonomy; 3.158) A vacuum runs on a schedule and comes home. With 3.156 (the
dock) and 3.158 (unattended acceptance), "survey the house every morning"
or "check the back door at 10 pm" becomes a setting, not a session.

**Scope.**
* Schedules kept by the brain (a mission or a survey, days and a time),
  set and shown on the twin; a run starts only from the dock, with the
  battery above Low (3.137) and no person holding the robot, else it is
  skipped and logged with the reason.
* A person's Stop ends a scheduled run like any other (3.84); every run
  ends on the dock (3.156).
* Off by default; on the Rover only after 3.158.D (unattended) is met, on
  the user's word.

**Criteria (written in step A, before building).** At least: scheduled
starts on time (bar), every skipped start logged with its reason (100%),
and in the simulator a week of scheduled runs at accelerated time with 0
contacts and every run ending docked.

## 3.163.A: criteria and design (written 2026-11-07, before building)

**What it rests on.** 3.156's return (the dock pose, `status.dock`, a
mission's `return: docked`; on the car "docked" is charging seen, 3.137.D),
3.137's battery ladder (`GET /battery`: Normal, Low, Reserve, Power-off),
3.84's latch (a person's Stop keeps autonomy out until a person starts a
mission) and 3.132's `authority_holder` (a person holding the robot), 3.133's
nav2/SLAM holds, 3.99's intervention line, 3.162's report, the brain's
`POST /mission/start` path, and the twin's Settings and Home (3.132).

**D1. A schedule.** `{id, job: find|survey, target (a find's), days (a set
of weekdays), at (HH:MM, the board's local time zone), enabled}`, at most 10,
plus one master switch. Kept by the brain in one local file
(`<schedule_dir>/schedules.json`, atomic and fsynced); a home's routine, so
never uploaded or committed.

**D2. When a run fires.** A brain thread reads the wall clock every 10 s. A
schedule is **due** at its time on its days; each due time (date, id) fires
**at most once**, recorded in `<schedule_dir>/runs.jsonl` before the start
is asked (a clock stepped back never fires it again). A due time is tried
until it starts or its **grace** (10 min) runs out; a brain that was down at
the time runs it on start-up if still inside the grace, else logs it
`missed_down`.

**D3. Preconditions, each a named skip.** At every try, in this order; the
first that holds is the reason, and a run never starts under any of them:
`disabled` (the master switch or the schedule off), `not_allowed_here`
(`brain.schedules_on_robot` false: the car's default until 3.158.D is met,
on the user's word), `mission_running` (anything running: tried again
until the grace ends, then `busy`), `person_stop_latched` (3.84: **a
schedule never lifts a person's Stop**; only a person's own start does),
`person_holding` (a person's authority), `not_docked`, `battery_not_normal`
(Low or worse, 3.137), `ros_not_ready` (3.133's hold past the grace).
Every due time ends as one line: `fired` (with the mission id) or
`skipped` (with the reason) or `missed_down`; the twin shows the last 10.

**D4. A scheduled run is an ordinary mission.** It starts through the same
path as `POST /mission/start` (the brain driving, `x-driver: brain`), with
`started_by: schedule:<id>` in its status, 3.99's line and 3.162's report.
So a person's Stop ends it `cancelled` (3.84), a D-pad preempts it, it
returns to the dock (3.156), its budget is the mission's own
(`mission_time_limit_s`, `survey_time_limit_s`). Nothing is sent outside
the house when it ends (no message, no upload); the twin shows it when
opened.

**D5. The twin.** Settings: "Schedules" -- the master switch, the list,
Add (Find or Survey, the target typed, the time, the days), each with its
last result. Home's card adds one line: "Next: survey at 9:00 tomorrow"
(or "Schedules off", or why the last run was skipped, in plain words).
Strings in `strings.js`, both voices.

**Not a safety veto, and never around one.** The scheduler only asks for
a start through the existing path; the robot server's arbitration,
`robot/safety.py` and the latch decide as they do for a person's start.
Rule 4 unchanged.

**Ties to 3.158.** D's unattended stretch may be started by schedules once
this is built; on the car the switch stays refused until 3.158.D is met
(3.163.C); scheduled runs are counted in 3.158's records through 3.99's line
(`started_by`).

## 3.163.B amendments (written 2026-11-12, before building or measuring)

Found reading the code; each one is a choice B makes where A left it open.

1. **A schedule never lifts a Stop, so it cannot use `POST /mission/start`
   as it stands.** That route calls `/autonomy/resume`, which lifts a
   person's latch. A scheduled start uses the same build and loop through
   one shared function. It asks the robot server for
   `POST /autonomy/resume {"lift": false}`, which opens the mission (3.164's
   record, 3.171's `mission_id`) only when no Stop is latched, atomically, and
   otherwise answers `refused: person_stop_latched`. So a Stop pressed between
   the brain's check and its start is never lifted. This is a `robot/server.py`
   change; the Touches are widened below.
2. **`not_docked` in the simulator.** A run is on the dock when the last
   mission this brain ran ended its return `docked` and the odometry has moved
   less than `DOCK_TOL_M` (0.10 m) since. With no mission yet, the odometry
   must be within `DOCK_TOL_M` of its own origin (3.156 D1: the start pose is
   the dock). The saved map's dock (3.156.E) and "charging seen" (3.137.D) are
   C's.
3. **`person_holding`** is the robot server's `/health` `authority_holder`
   ranked as a person. **`ros_not_ready`** applies only to a policy that
   needs nav2 (a survey's `explore`); it is the start's own 503 `hold`
   (3.133), tried again until the grace ends.
4. **The policy.** A schedule names only its job. A find runs `tiered` and a
   survey `explore`, as 3.158.B's rehearsal does: two config keys,
   `schedule_find_policy` and `schedule_survey_policy`, plus
   `schedules_on_robot` (false) and `schedule_dir`, moved with
   `control/brain_config.py`.
5. **The accelerated clock.** The scheduler reads its time through a clock it
   is given. Only when `SCHEDULE_CLOCK_FILE` is set (the harness, never a
   unit) is the wall time shifted by the seconds in that file, read every
   tick, so the harness can jump the idle gaps. T1 in the week is measured
   from the moment that clock reaches the due time to the mission reading
   `running`, in wall seconds.
6. **The injected battery Low.** The simulator's pack is set over a sim-only
   robot-server route, `POST /sim/battery {"v": V}` (404 outside sim mode,
   behind the secret, as `/sim/objects` is). It is set back to 12.0 V after
   the skip; Low does not latch (3.137).
7. **T3's pairing, in numbers.** The same 21 jobs (7 surveys, 14 finds, the
   same targets in the same order) are started by hand through
   `POST /mission/start` in the same harness and house. "Equal" means the
   scheduled week's finds are within **2** of the hand set's (of 14), and its
   survey coverage median (rooms entered of the house sheet's, as 3.158.B
   scores) within **10 points**. The two sets are about 8-16 hours of exclusive
   desktop time between them, run in lock windows as 3.158.B was. A set cut
   short is reported as cut short, never extrapolated.
8. **The injected skips in T3**, each at a due time:
   * not docked, twice: a person's D-pad drives the robot 0.3 m off the dock
     between runs, and drives it back after the skip;
   * a person holding: `twin-dpad` holding authority with a camera-only
     verb, which moves no wheel;
   * a latched Stop: a person's `POST /stop`, lifted by a person's own start
     afterwards;
   * the brain down: the brain killed over a due time and restarted after
     its grace, giving `missed_down`. T5's "inside the grace" case is a
     second kill, restarted within it.

   The day each one falls on is chosen before the run and written in the
   harness.
9. **Corrections found while building, before any measurement (2026-11-12).**
   * **Amendment 2 replaced.** The simulator's odometry is a path length
     and a heading, not a position, so "moved less than 0.10 m since" could
     never read a robot a person drove off and back on as docked. `not_docked`
     instead compares the SLAM pose with the dock the brain knows: the last
     mission's `status.dock`, or with none yet, the pose at the scheduler's first tick
     (every simulated run starts docked, 3.156 D1). It is docked within
     `DOCK_TOL_M` (0.10 m) and `DOCK_TOL_DEG` (10 deg).
   * **`not_allowed_here`** holds when the robot server's mode is not `sim`
     and `schedules_on_robot` is false. A simulated robot always runs
     schedules; the car only on the user's word.
   * **`ros_not_ready`**: no usable SLAM pose, whatever the policy (whether
     it is docked cannot then be known), checked where `not_docked` is. The
     brain's start never answers 503 for nav2's hold: explore waits for it
     inside the mission (3.105), so nav2's own readiness is not checked here.
   * **Every reason is tried again at each tick until the grace ends** (D2);
     the reason recorded is the last one seen, and `mission_running` is
     recorded as `busy`.
   * **Two reasons added:** `robot_unreachable` (the robot server's `/health`
     cannot be read, or it is too old to open a mission without lifting a
     Stop) and `start_refused` (the brain refused the start for anything
     else, its message kept). A start that fails after its `firing` line ends
     `skipped`, never tried again (at most once).
10. **T3's week, fixed before any run (2026-11-12).** It runs in
   `evaluations/schedule-3163/week.py` on 3.158.B's stack (ROS, nav2, the
   cloud and VE stand-ins at $0, footprint truth), `SIM_MAP=home_first_floor`.
   The week is Monday 2026-11-16 to Sunday 11-22, with three schedules: a
   survey every day at 9:00, a find of the red backpack (kitchen) every day at
   13:00, and a find of the side table (living room) every day at 17:00. The
   injected skips:

   | Day and time | Injected | Expected end |
   |---|---|---|
   | Tue 13:00 | driven off the dock | `not_docked` |
   | Wed 17:00 | the pack set to 10.3 V | `battery_not_normal` |
   | Thu 9:00 | driven off the dock | `not_docked` |
   | Fri 13:00 | a person holding the camera | `person_holding` |
   | Sat 9:00 | a person's Stop | `person_stop_latched` |
   | Sun 13:00 | the brain down, back 5 min after | fired (T5 inside the grace) |
   | Sun 17:00 | the brain down through the grace | `missed_down` |

   So **15 runs fire** (5 surveys, 10 finds) and 6 do not. **Amendment 7's
   pairing is corrected** to those that fire: the hand set is the same 15 jobs
   started through `POST /mission/start`, in the same order, on a fresh stack.
   Its finds are compared out of 10 (within 2), not 14, because a skipped due
   time is no mission. A person's Stop is lifted after its skip the way a
   person's start does it first (`POST /autonomy/resume`), and no mission is
   started for it. T1 at real wall time: 3 finds, each scheduled 2 minutes
   ahead with no clock file, on the same stack, after the week.

11. **Week run 1 was invalid; the harness changed before run 2 (2026-11-12).**
   Run 1 (code `c32628f8`, `week-run1-invalid*.jsonl`) was stopped on its
   Saturday. What it showed, recorded and not counted:
   * Every due time that fired, fired on time: 12 runs, 0.5-8.3 s after the
     due time, each with 0 contacts and docked.
   * Injected skips that held: Tue and Thu `not_docked`, Fri `person_holding`.
   * Wed 17:00 (the injected Low) ended `missed_down`. **A harness fault:** it
     jumped the clock to 20 s before the due time and waited only 25 s before
     jumping past the grace, so with a tick every 10 s it was luck whether any
     tick saw the due time. The scheduler recorded correctly what it saw.
   * The pack stopped charging after Tuesday's off-dock nudge. The D-pad
     return left the robot slightly off the charger stand-in (inferred), and
     each later mission took its own start pose as the dock (3.156 D1). By
     Friday the pack was at Power-off, so Fri 17:00 was rightly
     `battery_not_normal`, and nothing more could run.
   * The harness also counted a past mission's `started_by` as a start.

   Run 2's harness, on a fresh stack:
   * waits until the scheduler lists the due time as waiting, with a reason,
     before jumping past the grace;
   * counts a start only while a mission runs;
   * tops the pack up to 11.9 V over `/sim/battery` when the charger stand-in
     has not brought it there by the next run, recording `topped_up_from_v`.
     The charger is 3.156.D's, not under test here;
   * records how far the D-pad return lands from where the nudge began
     (`nudge_back_err_m`).

   Nothing else changes: the schedules, the injections and the bars stand.

## 3.163.B build and reviews (2026-11-12)

**Built.** `control/scheduler.py`; in `control/brain_server.py` the shared
`launch()`, D3's checks, `GET`/`PUT /schedules` and the tick task;
`RemoteRobot.open_mission()` and `get_health()`; in `robot/server.py`
`/autonomy/resume {"lift": false}`, `/health`'s `autonomy_latch` and
`POST /sim/battery`; `started_by` in status, 3.99's line and 3.162's report;
the twin's Schedules (Settings) and Home's line; four config keys. Tests:
`tests/test_schedule.py` (37) and `tests/test_ui_schedule.py` (10). Every D3
reason test failed with the checks planted off, and all ten UI tests failed on
`dev`'s page, before passing here.

**Reviews.** Two rounds on Sonnet, each with `/code-review` and the Thermos
pass (round 2 as one reviewer with both rubrics). Neither found a way for a
schedule to lift a Stop or start twice. Round 1's fixes, red first where a test
could show it:
* the dock is guessed only in the simulator;
* a line torn by a power cut never swallows the next;
* a file that is not schedules reads as off;
* a lapsed lease is its own reason, `autonomy_lapsed`;
* a failed start closes the mission it opened;
* a change waits for a tick to finish its pass;
* disk work moved off the event loop;
* the twin never writes one brain's list to another, and drops a load begun
  before a save;
* the suite gets its own schedule directory.

Round 2's four findings were fixed after the last allowed round, **so they are
unreviewed**: a re-save repairs a schedule with no start time; a load error
keeps the list, so the error stays in view; a refused start closes only a
mission it opened; and on a real robot the mode is asked once.

**Limits, recorded.**
* At most once means a start that fails after its `firing` line is not retried,
  even when it lost a race with a person's start by a moment (`mission_running`
  then ends at once, without the grace).
* Turning schedules on inside a due time's grace starts that run, up to 10
  minutes late (D2 as written).
* On the spring-forward night, a due time inside the skipped hour reads
  `missed_down`, and `in_s` is an hour off across a change. Times are the
  board's local time.
* The twin's list is last-write-wins across phones; Home's line shows the last
  skip until the next run fires; a phone in another time zone may name the
  wrong day.
* A tick holds its lock through a start's build, so a person's change can wait
  tens of seconds. If `runner.start()` itself raised after the open, that
  mission's record would stay open.
* A twin that switches brains while a load is in flight shows the new brain's
  list one poll later.

**Finding: the pack was topped up 15 times, and no battery or charging bar
counts as met on them.** From Wednesday on, the charger stand-in (3.156.D's
pad: charging only within 0.30 m of where the robot first stood) mostly stopped
charging. The harness then topped the pack up to 11.9 V before 15 of the 24
due times (the week's 21 and the 3 at wall time), from 10.67-11.74 V; the first before Wednesday 13:00. Why, inferred and not measured:
each return docks within 0.10 m of its own mission's start pose (3.156 D1),
which is where the last mission ended, so the dock can walk off the pad one
return at a time. The harness did not record the true position against the pad,
so the drift itself is not shown. The off-dock nudges returned to within
0.001 m, so they are not the cause in run 2, and the hand set, with no
schedule and no nudge, needed 9 top-ups in its 15 jobs too: the charger
stand-in's, not the scheduler's. Nothing in T3 judges the pack;
Wednesday's Low skip was the injected 10.3 V, not a drained pack.

**Limit, named (for 3.156 and 3.163.C): the dock drifts.** The dock is each
mission's start pose, so a robot moved by a person, or one whose returns add
error, carries the dock with it. In the simulator the charger then stops
charging. On the car "docked" is charging seen (3.137.D, 3.156.C), which
catches it: 3.163.C must read docked from the charger, never the pose.
