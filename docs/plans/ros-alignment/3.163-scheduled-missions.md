# 3.163 Missions and surveys on a schedule

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.163; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the user, 2026-11-06: features of a robot vacuum (Dreame) worth leveraging, for full autonomy; 3.158) A vacuum runs on a schedule and comes home. With 3.156 (the
dock) and 3.158 (unattended acceptance), "survey the house every morning"
or "check the back door at 10 pm" becomes a setting, not a session.

**Scope.**
* Schedules kept by the brain (a mission or a survey, days and a time),
  set and shown on the twin; a run starts only from the dock, with the
  battery above Low (3.137) and no person holding the robot, else it is
  skipped and logged with the reason.
* A person's Stop ends a scheduled run like any other (3.84); every run
  ends on the dock (3.156).
* Off by default; on the Rover only after 3.158.D (unattended) is met, on
  the user's word.

**Criteria (written in step A, before building).** At least: scheduled
starts on time (bar), every skipped start logged with its reason (100%),
and in the simulator a week of scheduled runs at accelerated time with 0
contacts and every run ending docked.
