# 3.205 A find on a saved map with no memory of the target searches the unseen floor

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.205; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the orchestrator's brief, 2026-11-21, from 3.70.M3's record on `dev`
65f6e841.) On a carried (saved) map the floor the camera has not seen is one
connected patch, which `find_view_gaps` offers as one view gap, looked at once
from its middle; then explore has no frontier left and the find ends
`exhausted` with most of the house unseen (3.70.M3: 10 of 20 found, traced on
k 2). M3's fix, the re-search by sight (`resight`, `_sight_map()`: the free
floor the camera has not seen read as unknown, so frontiers lead the camera to
it), fires only for a find that was handed a recall (`_recalled`). 3.192.B
(`dev` d34300c0) added a look again at places the target was seen this
mission (`reseen`). Neither covers a find on a carried map whose inventory
holds no memory of X and that never sees X: it still ends `exhausted` early.

**The change, as designed before measuring.** The re-search's gate widens from
"handed a recall" to "on a carried map": the runner already knows, when it
seeds the inventory from the chain saved on the pose's saved-map frame, that
it carried landmarks there (`n > 0` in `_seed_inventory`, the same moment and
the same `n` that decides whether a recall is offered at all). It tells explore
(`carried_map()`, a new optional agent hook, as `recall_from()` is). Explore's
condition becomes `(self._recalled or self._carried) and not self.resight and
not self._survey() and self._unseen_floor(m, pose)`. M3's sight map is re-used
as it is; no second one is written. Every find handed a recall is on a carried
map (a recall is only offered when `n > 0`), so finds with a recall are
unchanged; a find with nothing carried (no saved map, or nothing saved on it)
and a survey are unchanged by construction. The bars below check that.

**Reviews** (`/code-review` and the Thermos pass on Sonnet, on `8148204c`):
both raised that the gate is "a landmark was carried" (`seed() > 0`), not
"the map was reloaded": a saved map whose chain carries nothing (or whose read
failed) is still not searched by sight. Kept as a written limit (the policy
spec says so): keying on the runner's `saved_map.reloaded` would need a pose
`dock`, which no in-process world gives, so the sweeps could not judge it, and
a survey always saves its landmarks. Code review alone: the red test now
asserts the stub held, the survey test asserts the survey's end, a missing
licence header (fixed). Thermos alone: the person's line chosen by
`_recalled` (kept: correct for both cases it names); one extra pass costs
time on a find whose target is absent (the bar's purpose; on the car explore
cannot see the target until perception on real frames is built, 3.76 E);
harness `rmtree` paths are under the given work directory (checked).
