# 3.221 SLAM on the real Rover, mapping while driven by hand

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.221; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** Frontier exploration on the car needs a map, and the map has only
ever been built in the simulator and over the fake motor board (R5 3.14,
G4 3.33). This section runs the ROS container (slam_toolbox) on the Jetson
against the real D500 while the user drives the Rover by hand with the
twin's D-pad. No autonomous motion. Two things stand in the way first:

* **The mast.** The D500 sees the pan-tilt mast 2-14 cm behind it in every
  scan (3.218). `robot/safety.py` masks it for forward moves
  (`ROBOT_LIDAR_SELF_BOX_CM=-7.1,3.2,-10.7,8.5`), but the scan the bridge
  relays to ROS still carries those returns. SLAM would lay a trail of
  occupied cells along the robot's own path and scan-match against points
  that move with the body. nav2's costmaps and `collision_monitor` would
  read the footprint as occupied.
* **The stamp.** `/scan`'s `stamp_unix` is the time of the REQUEST, not
  of the capture (`robot/server.py`). A real revolution is 50-150 ms old
  when it is served, so SLAM pairs it with the wrong odometry: at the
  D-pad's turn rate (~1 rad/s) that is 3-9 degrees per scan.
  Decided 2026-11-07: fix it before raising nav2's speed (3.42 / 3.76 A4,
  `HANDOFF-2026-10-07-lidar-and-speed.md`). Mapping while turning needs it
  now, so it is done here, with its own bar.

**Design (the mast approach decided by the user, 2026-11-26: the bridge masks the box, over a global `range_min` or a ROS filter node).**

* **The mast stays in the robot server's scan, and leaves only the ROS
  copy.** The robot server's `/scan` payload adds `lidar_self_box_cm` (the
  box the safety layer already uses). When it relays a scan, the bridge
  publishes each beam whose return falls inside the box as `-inf`:
  "too close to measure" (REP 117). Below `range_min`, slam_toolbox,
  costmap_2d (laser_geometry) and `collision_monitor` all drop it. It
  neither marks a cell nor clears one, so the space behind the mast stays
  UNKNOWN in ROS, not free. `+inf` would mean "no return", which SLAM
  and the costmaps may raytrace as clear; that is the error 3.218 refused.
  The robot server's veto reads its own scan, which is unchanged, so
  REVERSE stays `astern_occluded`. A global `range_min` of ~15 cm was
  rejected: it would also hide every real return under 15 cm at every
  bearing.
* **The stamp is the capture time.** The lidar knows each revolution's
  oldest and newest point times (monotonic). It fixes each revolution's
  midpoint on the wall clock once, when the revolution completes
  (`capture_unix` in its scan; review, below). The robot server stamps
  `stamp_unix` with it and names the source (`stamp_source`: `capture` | `request`). A body
  with no capture clock (the simulator) keeps the request time. The bridge
  already stamps the ROS scan with `stamp_unix` when the two clocks agree.
  It also stops republishing a revolution it has already sent: the same
  capture stamp twice is the same scan, where request stamps made one scan
  look like two.

**Criteria (written 2026-11-26, before building; CLAUDE.md section 7).**
Ground truth on the Rover is a tape measure, the user's, and a floor mark.

1. **The mast never reaches ROS; nothing else is removed.**
   (a) Offline: every self return recorded in 3.218
   (`evaluations/lidar-self-box-3218/selfhits-20261126.json`) is published
   as `-inf`. For 10,000 seeded random scans, the beams masked are exactly
   the ones `robot/safety.py` places inside the box: 0 extra, 0 missed.
   With no box, the ROS ranges are identical to before.
   (b) On the Rover at rest, 60 s of `/scan` as ROS receives it: 0 finite
   returns inside the self box.
   (c) The finished map: at most 1% of the trajectory's poses (sampled at
   2 Hz) have an occupied cell inside the footprint shrunk by one cell
   (5 cm) on each side.
   Progress: the map covers the driven room (criterion 3's area).
2. **The robot server's veto keeps the mast.** The robot server's own
   `/scan` still carries the self returns: unit test plus the live session.
   During the session every REVERSE is refused `astern_occluded`, and
   forward refusals are counted and named.
3. **The map matches a measured room.** The user tapes two lengths in the
   mapped room (two wall lengths, or wall to wall across it). Each length
   measured on the saved map is within **5 cm, or 2% if that is larger**,
   of the tape.
   Progress: the room's free area on the map in m², reported.
4. **The pose holds still at rest.** With the Rover stationary for 60 s
   after mapping, the maximum excursion of `/world/pose` (map frame) from
   its median is **at most 2 cm and 1 degree**.
   Progress: the distance driven before the hold, at least 10 m.
5. **A loop closes on a return to the start.** The start is a floor mark
   (tape at both front wheels). The user drives a loop of **at least 8 m**
   that comes back to it and stops on the mark, facing the starting way.
   The map pose at the end is within **10 cm and 5 degrees** of the map
   pose at the start. Odometry's own error over the same loop is reported
   beside it (the progress half), together with whether slam_toolbox logged
   a loop closure.
6. **The stamp is the capture time.**
   (a) Unit, with an injected clock: `stamp_unix` is the revolution's
   midpoint within 1 ms; a body with no capture clock keeps the request
   time, marked `request`.
   (b) On the Rover, 200 consecutive `/scan` replies: every one
   `capture`, request time minus `stamp_unix` between 0 and 250 ms, and the
   median gap between successive distinct stamps 90-110 ms (the 10 Hz
   revolution).
   (c) The bridge publishes no stamp twice. On the Rover:
   `scans_published` over 60 s is at most the revolutions completed.
   Progress: criterion 3, mapped while turning on the stamped scans.
7. **Default off and nothing else changes.** The fast tier passes. With no
   self box the bridge's scan is unchanged (1a). The simulator's stamps are
   unchanged. The frozen references are untouched.
