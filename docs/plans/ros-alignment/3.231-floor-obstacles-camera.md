# 3.231 Floor obstacles from the pan-tilt camera and local perception, on the Rover

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.231; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** The user, 2026-11-27 evening, after 3.229.B's survey: "We need to
use the pan and tilt camera to detect obstacles on the floor and navigate
around it. The local perception tier can be used for this."

The D500 scans one plane about 12 cm up (3.27). A shoe, a sock, a cable, a
toy or a mat's edge lies under it. Two pieces are planned and neither runs
on the Rover:
* 3.222, the OAK-D floor band, a veto: its K7 failed on the Jetson.
* 3.74 Part C, a camera hazard class that steers and never vetoes:
  planned 2026-10-12, never built.

This section is 3.74 Part C brought onto the Rover:
* the eyes are the **pan-tilt camera** (`ROBOT_CAMERA`, `/dev/video0`
  today), tilted down at the floor ahead;
* the detector is the **local perception tier**, YOLOE on the Jetson (3.33:
  median 61 ms, p90 110 ms a frame at 15 W), with no cloud call;
* what it finds becomes places nav2 plans around.

**What stays as decided** (3.74, rule 3-4):
* Recognition can be wrong, so it **steers and never vetoes**. Hazards
  reach motion only as rejected explore goals and as keepout spots in
  nav2's costmap, through the bridge.
* The veto stays geometric: the lidar and, once 3.222 passes, the OAK-D
  floor band, in `robot/safety.py`.
* A hazard is never placed by a guess dressed as a measurement. Its range
  is the floor-contact range from the camera's measured height and tilt,
  marked as such, or OAK-D depth where 3.222 provides it.

**What is in the way, and how this section handles it.**
* **The camera's angle.** 3.223: the board moves the pan-tilt servos but
  never reads them back, so `ROBOT_GIMBAL` is off and 3.220 refuses frames
  without a measured pan. Two options were open:
  * a fixed downward tilt commanded once and checked by tape (open loop,
    an amendment to 3.220's rule for this use, written before it is
    used);
  * waiting for 3.223's read-back.

  **Decided (the user, 2026-11-27):** a fixed downward tilt on commanded
  angles, with servo reads off (3.223 option 2), until the replacement
  board reads the servos. Step A measures that tilt by tape (A1-A2). The
  OAK-D floor band (3.222) is the veto; this camera only steers.
* **The classes miss things.** A YOLOE prompt list (3.74 C: `cable`,
  `sock`, `shoe`, `pet bowl`, `toy`, plus `rug edge` / `mat` from 3.230)
  only finds what it names. Step B also measures a class-free check:
  anything that is not floor inside the floor region of the frame. The
  data decide which ships.
* **Explore cannot see real frames yet** (3.76 E). 3.227.B ran YOLOE on
  real frames for a find's target; this section reuses that path for
  hazards.

**Criteria.** Written in each step before it is measured (CLAUDE.md
section 7). 3.74's C1-C5 are the starting bars and are restated in each
step with this camera's numbers. A's are below.

* **A1.** The camera's floor view at the chosen tilt, by tape on the
  Rover, with the user: the nearest and farthest floor visible ahead, the
  width at 0.5 m and 1.0 m, and the lens height. Recorded, not barred.
* **A2.** Floor-contact range against a tape at 0.3, 0.6, 1.0 and 1.5 m,
  for a box's bottom edge: median error **<= 10 %** of the range (3.74
  C2).
* **A3.** Frame cost on the Jetson with the full stack running: YOLOE on
  the camera's frames at the rate explore needs (decided in A from nav2's
  0.20 m/s and A1's view), p90 **<= 150 ms**. The wheel loop is
  unchanged: 0 late ticks.
