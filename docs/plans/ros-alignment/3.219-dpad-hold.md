# 3.219 Twin D-pad: press and hold to drive

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.219; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** The user (2026-11-26): the twin's D-pad should drive while a
button is HELD and stop on release, like Waveshare's own page. Until now
each tap sent one short verb (`/action` FORWARD, 0.5 s at speed 50, or a
turn of the chosen step), so driving across a room was a dozen taps.

**Design (decided before building).** No new movement route. A hold uses
the existing standing command, `POST /wheels` as driver `twin-dpad`:
arbitrated by M4 exactly like `/action`, vetted by
`SafetyController.vet_wheel_velocity()` on arrival and re-vetted by the
20 Hz `wheel_loop()` every period, zeroed by the 1 s watchdog (B3.1) on
silence. The twin re-posts the command every 250 ms while held and posts a
zero on release (a zero from the holder stops the wheels; from anyone else
it is a no-op, so it cannot cut off another driver). Speeds are the verbs'
own: 0.30 m/s straight (a FORWARD verb at speed 50,
`robot/safety.py` `verb_speed_m_s`) and 1.2 rad/s body rate turning (the
turn verb's, `robot/hardware_robot.py`), converted to wheel rad/s with the
`wheel_radius_m` / `track_width_m` the robot's `GET /wheels` reports. A
backend that reports no geometry (a phone, a replay) gets no hold: the
press stays a tap. No server change.

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7).** Measured in a
real Chromium at a 390 px phone viewport against a live `robot.server`
(the simulator), by `tests/test_ui_dpad_hold.py`. "Wheels zero" means the
robot's own `GET /wheels` reports both commanded velocities 0, polled by
the test every 20 ms.

1. **Holding drives continuously, through arbitration and safety.**
   (a) A 2.0 s hold of FORWARD posts `/wheels` as `x-driver: twin-dpad`,
   with no gap between posts above 400 ms, and every post is answered
   `executed: true` by the route that arbitrates and vets.
   (b) Progress: the robot's odometry advances **>= 0.40 m** over a 2.0 s
   FORWARD hold (nominal 0.30 m/s x 1.7 s = 0.51 m), and a 2.0 s LEFT hold
   turns it **>= 60 degrees**.
   (c) A hold toward a wall is stopped by safety, not by the twin: the
   wheels zero (or the post is clamped) before contact, with `clamped`
   or `safety_distance` reported.
2. **Every way a hold can end stops the wheels.** Time from the event to
   wheels zero:
   * release (pointer up): **<= 300 ms**
   * pointer cancel: **<= 300 ms**
   * the finger sliding off the button (touch, with the browser's implicit
     capture): **<= 300 ms**
   * the page hidden (`visibilitychange`): **<= 300 ms**
   * the tab closed (`pagehide`, a `keepalive` zero): **<= 300 ms**
   * the network dropped (nothing can reach the robot): the watchdog,
     **<= 1.5 s** after the last post (1.0 s `watchdog_timeout_s` + 0.1 s
     poll + slack).
   The watchdog stays the backstop for all of them.
3. **A tap keeps today's behaviour.** A press released before 300 ms sends
   exactly one `/action` verb (with the turn step's angle for LEFT/RIGHT)
   and **zero** `/wheels` posts; a hold sends **no** `/action` verb (its
   trailing click is swallowed). Keyboard activation stays a tap.
4. **STOP wins at once.** STOP pressed (second finger) during a hold: wheels
   zero **<= 300 ms**, and **no** drive post is sent after the STOP press.
5. **Works on a phone.** Touch input (`hasTouch`, real touch events through
   CDP) at 390 px: a touch hold drives and touch end stops (bar 2's
   bounds). The D-pad buttons compute `touch-action: none`,
   `user-select: none` and `-webkit-touch-callout: none` (iOS Safari: no
   scroll, selection or callout steals the long press), and a long press
   opens no context menu. A phone-size screenshot (deviceScaleFactor 2) is
   kept in `evaluations/dpad-hold-3219/`. Real iOS Safari is not available
   here; it is checked in step B.

**Steps.**

* A. Build, measure 1-5, review (code-review + Thermos), push to `dev`.
* B. **Open: on the real Rover, wheels off the floor**: hold and release
  each direction, slide off, lock the phone mid-hold, iOS Safari. Needs the
  user's go, their presence and the board lock. Not done by 3.219.A.

Each stop (after "stays zero for 0.6 s" was added to the check) was
re-introduced and its test seen to fail: pointerup, pointercancel,
pointerleave, the touch capture release, `visibilitychange`, STOP's
`holdEnd`, the click swallow, the CSS. Release and tab close each have two
paths (pointerup and the trailing click; `pagehide` and `visibilitychange`):
each pair removed together fails, either alone does not. The first touch
run failed for real: the implicit capture lands on the button's icon, not
the button, so `releasePointerCapture` is called on `e.target`.

Every speed is the verbs' own, so safety's bars are unchanged; no server
code changed.

**Review (code-review + Thermos bug pass, Sonnet).** Both found: (1) the
1 s click window let a finger lifted 2 s after STOP send a step; now the
click is swallowed per button that drove. (2) Under `drive: ros` every
hold was refused and a long press did nothing; now a press there stays a
tap (`/health`'s `drive.mode`, or the first `not_the_actuator`). (3) The
post chain grew without bound over a slow tunnel; now at most one refresh
waits. Code-review only, verified: (4) an HTTP-error post sent no zero,
leaving the last command to the watchdog; (5) the wheel geometry outlived
a reconnect. Bug pass only, verified: (6) `visibilitychange` ends the hold
before `pagehide`, so the `keepalive` zero never ran on a close; now both
send it; (7) a refused hold sent no zero; now every end but STOP queues
one (harmless from a non-holder). Each of (1), (2), (4) and (6) has a test
seen to fail on the first commit's code. Not taken: a maximum hold
duration (no reachable trigger found; the bug pass did not claim one).
After the fixes, the module again: release 69 ms, others 17-27 ms,
network 1094 ms, rejected post to zero 92 ms, progress 0.506 m and 117.4
degrees.

Known limits: a drive post in flight when the tab closes can land after
the `keepalive` zero (the queued zero or the watchdog then stops it); the
`keepalive` zero cross-origin needs a preflight a browser may drop at
unload (the watchdog, 1 s); under `drive: ros` a held press is one step,
not a hold.
