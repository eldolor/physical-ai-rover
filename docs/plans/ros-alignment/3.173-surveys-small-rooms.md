### 3.173.A results (2026-11-10; `evaluations/small-rooms-3173/`)

**A1** (`a1_surveys.py`, the 9 surveys' brain logs): every survey missed laundry and
pantry. No survey sent a room goal: the robot held no room names during a survey, so
3.70's room goals never ran. Pantry: frontier goals reached its door in 9 of 9 surveys,
the robot's nearest pose 0.54-0.64 m from the room, and none went inside. Laundry: a goal
near it in 1 of 9, nearest pose 0.61-0.76 m. Survey 12 (3.172.B's B4) sent no goal near
the dining room (nearest pose 0.75 m), the room its two carried set-aside places lay in;
consistent with B4's set-aside cause, not proof (3.172.D's `set_aside_at` logs it from now on).

**A2** (`a2_reach.py`, `A2-reach.jsonl`; a 5 cm truth raster, distances within 2.5 cm).
Explore uses two clearances: a path passes where the robot's centre keeps `PASS_CLEARANCE_M`
(0.15 m), and a goal stands only where it keeps `leave_clearance_m` (0.3265 m, 7 cells of
SLAM's 5 cm map: 0.35 m).
- **On truth every room of all 23 houses (120 rooms) has floor that keeps 0.3265 m and that
  the dock reaches through passes.** So truth walls do not keep surveys out.
- Laundry and pantry are the tightest of the 120 rooms: 0.366 m at best, the only two under
  0.45 m.
- **Inferred, not measured:** on SLAM's map (walls smeared 0.03 m, the goal clearance rounded
  up to 0.35 m), neither room keeps a cell a goal may stand on, so a survey's goals stop at
  the door. This fits A1. Reading a survey's SLAM map needs the ROS stack, which waits for
  the desktop (no live runs before 11-11 evening). B's first task measures it.

**Correction to 3.172.D:** it said a centre kept 0.3265 m reaches neither room from the dock.
That required the whole path to keep the goal clearance; explore's paths need only 0.15 m.
The rooms are reachable; what a survey lacks is a place inside them for a goal to stand.

**A3:** the chassis' circumscribed radius is 0.171 m, so a centre that keeps 0.181 m sweeps no
contact at any heading. Paths into laundry, pantry, den and half bath keep 0.27 m on truth,
which is met. Paths that already exist today keep 0.17-0.18 m into seven rooms of ProcTHOR
houses 8 and 9; those rooms have 0.58-1.68 m of goal room, so B does not touch them.

**B's clearance, proposed before B is built (the orchestrator decides):** a survey only, a

about 0.08 m to spare. Nav2 and the robot server vet every move, unchanged. Below 0.3265 m
some headings cannot drive straight out: nav2 turns first, and the pivot guard (3.19) vets
the turn. So S6 (`failed`, docked) and S4 (contacts) are the bars that would show a wedge.

### 3.173.B: the build (go 2026-11-10, the orchestrator: surveys only, 0.25 m, the veto unchanged)

`ExploreAgent._small_room_goal()`, first in a survey's goal choice, at most every
`SMALL_ROOM_S` (5 s):
- The map is cut into rooms by `room_labels.segment()` (no names needed).
- A room qualifies when it holds no stop of the choice's own reach at the goal clearance,
  and the robot has not stood in it (its own track, `STOOD_M` squares).
- The goal goes to the most open cell in it that keeps `SMALL_ROOM_CLEARANCE_M` (0.25 m)
  and that passes reach. The nearest such room goes first, and each room gets one goal.
- On arrival the robot looks around, as at a frontier.
- A failure is any goal's failure: the retry book and the escape, unchanged.
- No line of `robot/` changes.

**B's in-process cost (`b_sweep_small.py`, `b_label_cost.py`; `tests/survey_sweep.py`'s 9 surveys).**
- `SMALL_ROOM_MIN_M2` = 0.7 was set from home_first_floor's own map pieces: pantry 0.72-0.90 m2
  and laundry 1.08, against the furniture gaps of the dining chairs and kitchen at 0.09-0.63. That
  makes it fitted, not independent: S3's 23-house sweep is its independent check.
- A breakfast piece (0.90-0.99 m2) still qualifies in some starts. If S3 or S4 shows it costs
  anything, that is reported, not tuned away.
- Label calls, off against on:
  - rooms mode: median 10 and max 19 both ways; good rooms 22 -> 24.
  - spacing mode (the default): median 29 -> 31, max 44 both ways; good rooms 46 -> 48.
- **`tests/test_survey_sweep.py`'s spacing median pin was raised from 29 to 31** (the orchestrator,
  2026-11-10). It is a regression guard on cost, not a 3.173 bar. The two calls are the newly labelled
  pantry and laundry, and calls are free under the default `frame` source.
- All 9 surveys end `exhausted` in both arms.
