### 3.173.B fix: a held refusal is waited through (2026-11-11, from 3.172.E)

**What 3.172.E found.** Over its 7 live surveys the small-room goal was tried 30 times, at
(10.5, 4.2) and (7.5, 11.9) on the SLAM map, and the robot server refused every one
`preempted`: the mission's own last verb (the look around at a goal's end) still held
authority, which lapses on silence. Review round 1's item 3 counted that refusal toward
`SMALL_ROOM_REFUSED`, and each next try came a cut (5 s or more) later, again just after a
verb. So every room was given up after 3 tries and no small-room goal ever drove. Every other
kind meets the same refusal (frontier 899 times, recall 66, approach 54, view 54) and waits
through it. In process `FakeNav` refused nothing that way, so the tests passed.

**The change.** A `preempted` refusal keeps the pick (`_small_held`), and the next choice sends
it again at once, with no cut and no count. After `SMALL_ROOM_HELD_S` (10 s, as recall's
`RECALL_REFUSED_S`) of such refusals it counts as one refusal toward `SMALL_ROOM_REFUSED`, as
any other reason does. A person who holds the robot is not a new case: every other goal kind
waits on that reply too, and a person's Stop ends the mission.

**Red first.** `test_3172e_a_held_refusal_is_waited_through_not_counted` runs a whole
`home_first_floor` survey through a nav double that refuses each place's first 5 sends
`preempted`, every goal kind alike. On `dev` `69099bc3` it failed with no small-room goal sent;
with the change it sends one each into pantry and laundry. A unit test pins the uncounted
re-send and the count past the wait. The given-up test now uses a reason other than `preempted`.

**Review (Sonnet, one round, 2026-11-11):** no finding on `df664b38`; recorded. Noted, not
changed: each held re-send logs a `goal_refused` event, at tick rate for up to 10 s. The same
pass on 3.172.E's harness (`1088b907`, the orchestrator's ask) found no bug. Noted: a
`battery_low` refusal on a fresh stack would loop batches with no progress guard (it did not
happen).

### 3.173.B amendment 3: a small room is the part of a piece no goal stop reaches (written 2026-11-11, before any code; the orchestrator's decision)

**Why.** On SLAM's map the cut puts laundry and pantry in one piece with parts of the kitchen,
breakfast room and garage hall, and the whole piece holds goal stops, so B's rule skips it (the
first task, above).

**The rule, replacing "a piece with no goal stop".**
- **Goal stops:** the cells of `reach(m, pose, clearance_m=leave_clearance_m(...))`, the reach
  B already computes for the choice (`rch`).
- **"Reached by a goal stop":** a cell whose centre lies within `need * res` of a goal stop's
  centre. `need = ceil(clearance / res)` is the cell count `reach` itself keeps clear: 7
  cells, 0.35 m, on SLAM's 5 cm map. Every cell in that disc is free by the stop's own
  definition, so the reach never passes through a wall. It is the floor a goal there already
  covers.
- **Candidate:** inside each piece of `segment()`, the free cells that no goal stop reaches,
  split into 8-connected regions. A region qualifies if its free area is at least
  `SMALL_ROOM_MIN_M2` (0.7 m2, unchanged) and it holds a cell of the 0.25 m reach
  (`SMALL_ROOM_CLEARANCE_M`, unchanged) to stand on.
- **The goal:** at the region's most open 0.25 m-reach cell, the nearer first, as before.
- **Unchanged, judged per region instead of per piece:** "stood in", "tried", the retry book,
  the held-refusal wait, each region once, and the look around on arrival.

**Bars, written before measuring:**
1. Red first: a 5 cm test map shaped like SLAM's (a hall with a column of goal stops, two

   it stands and passes with this rule.
2. On `picar-maps-3172e`'s saved map (`b_slam_map.py`), the rule picks a region in laundry and
   one in pantry. Any other region it picks is reported, room by room.
