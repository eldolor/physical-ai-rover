*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.173; index: [README.md](README.md).*

3.172.B's 2, 3.158.B's 7), and every one ended `out_of_budget` (3.158.B's C4, not

only on their way through. At 0.25 m all 13 rooms are reachable. Finds do enter laundry,
by an approach to a sighted target, which a survey never makes. So a survey's goals,
not its time, keep it out of those rooms. Written 2026-11-10 at the orchestrator's ask:
steps and bars only, **not built**, to be scheduled.

each stability metric is paired with a progress one; everything runs through the real path
(`MissionRunner` -> `ExploreAgent` -> nav2 -> `robot/safety.py`). This step is near the
motion path, so:
