# 3.199 A person's goal beside a no-go zone never lands in its drawn margin

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.199; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the user, 2026-11-21, through the orchestrator: 3.195's "Left (a)")
3.161's D5 refuses a person's goal tap inside a no-go zone (reason
`no_go_zone`), testing the bare zone (`world/nogo.py zone_at`). Since 3.195 the
bridge draws each zone grown by `keepout.GROW_M` (0.05 m) with rounded
corners, and marks every cell the grown polygon touches (3.193), so a drawn
cell reaches up to 0.05 m plus a cell's diagonal, **0.121 m**, from the zone
(`3.195-nogo-corner-cut.md`, C6). A person's goal up to that far outside a
zone is accepted and can land in a lethal cell. NavFn then searches its goal
`tolerance` (0.10 m, `nav2.yaml`) for a free cell, and from a goal on the
zone's edge the nearest free cell can be 0.10 m away, at the limit of its
float loop (`goal - tol` stepped by the resolution while `<= goal + tol`). One
of 3.195's reviewers simulated that loop and saw 1 in 7,200 such goals fail
to plan.

## 3.199.A: criteria and design (written 2026-11-21, before building)

**D1. Refuse, not clamp.** A person's goal (a manual-rank `x-driver`, as
today) within `GOAL_CLEAR_M` of a zone, inside it included, is refused by the
robot server with the same reason, `no_go_zone`, the same fields and
`retryable: true`; the detail says "inside or beside". Why refuse:
* D5 is already a refusal, and the twin already words it ("That's in a no-go
  zone"); a person who taps at a zone's edge sees why nothing moved, and taps
  again further out.
* A clamp would move a person's goal by up to 0.125 m without saying so, and
  the honest clamp target is a free cell: the robot server does not have the
  drawn cells (the lattice phase, `margins_off`, D4's suspensions are the
  bridge's), and asking would cost a bridge route (budget 16 of 16). A
  distance clamp would just reproduce the refusal band's edge.
* Autonomy's goals stay unrefused (3.161's Thermos finding: the trip home
  must never be refused for a zone over the dock); explore's own margin is
  3.195's Left (b), not this section.

**D2. `GOAL_CLEAR_M` = 0.125 m** (`world/nogo.py`). The drawn reach is
`GROW_M / cos(step / 2)` (the arc's polygon, 0.050242 m) plus a cell's
diagonal (0.070711 m) = 0.120953 m; 0.125 m clears it by 4 mm, so a goal more
than 0.125 m from the zone (distance is the same in the house and the map
frame: the anchor is a rigid motion) lies in a cell no part of which is
within 0.0543 m of the zone, a cell the grown polygon never touches, on any
lattice phase and any anchor rotation. It is a fact defined across the ROS
wall (the margin is the bridge's), so it is registered in
`tests/test_wall_linters.py`'s `DUPLICATES` (budget 12 -> 13, said why) with a
check that it covers the bridge's drawn reach computed from `keepout` itself,
and by no more than 5 mm.

**What does not change.** `robot/safety.py` and arbitration (a zone is not a
veto: the D-pad is never refused); `service/slam/` (held by 3.197; the bridge
draws as 3.195 left it); the twin and `strings.js` (same reason, same words);
autonomy's goals; `PUT /world/nogo`; the routes.

**The goal simulation** (`evaluations/nogo-3199/goals.py`, seeded, in
process). 3.195's reviewer's simulation is not in the repo, so this rebuilds
it from its description. Per goal: an axis-aligned zone in the house frame
(sides 0.20-3.0 m, as `world/nogo.py` takes them, 1 in 4 a 0.10 m wall), an
anchor (a uniform rotation and a translation of up to 50 m) into the map frame,
a uniform lattice phase; the zone drawn by the bridge's own code
(`keepout.with_margin`, `keepout.mask(zones=..., phase=...)`); a goal at a
uniform distance d in (0, 0.15] m outside the zone (a uniform point on the
offset curve: along an edge or round a corner). The robot server's
refusal is the real `nogo_refusal` rule (`world/nogo.zone_at`, D5's grow).
An accepted goal **lands in a drawn cell** if its cell is lethal in the mask;
it **fails to plan** if its own cell and every point NavFn's tolerance loop
visits (Humble's `navfn_planner.cpp`: x and y each from `goal - 0.10` in
float steps of 0.05 while `<= goal + 0.10`) lie in drawn cells (the rest of
the map free: only the mask is modelled, so this counts the mask's failures
alone). 7,200 goals, as the reviewer's; then a second set of 7,200 at d in
(0.125, 1.0] for G3.

## 3.199.A amendment 1 (written 2026-11-21, after G8's run 1, before run 2)

**G8 run 1** (`evaluations/nogo-3199/g8-run1.json`, a fresh stack, load
peak 1.45): the taps 0.02, 0.06 and 0.11 m out from zone
`x 1.615-2.285, y 2.822-3.478`'s east edge were refused `no_go_zone`, and none
started a nav2 goal. The tap 0.30 m out, at (2.585, 3.150), was accepted. But
nav2 aborted it after 18.7 s with the robot 1.61 m short and 1.16 m from the
zone at its closest. NavFn logged "The goal sent to the planner is off the
global costmap" on every try (`g8-run1-logs/nav2-warnings.log`): the point is
in the room north of the start, which the fresh SLAM map had not yet seen. The
script picked the side from the simulator's floor, not the robot's own map.
**G8 run 1: the refusal half is met; the drive half FAILED as written.** The
cause is the script's choice of goal, not D5 (the goal was taken, which is the
change's half).

**Run 2, once, changed only in the choice of point:** 5 s after the pose is
usable, the robot's own map (`/world/map`, the frame a tap is in) is read. The
zone is the first one of `sweep.place_zones("scaled_house", seed)`, over seeds
0-30, with an edge whose point 0.30 m out has every map cell within 0.20 m
known free, as well as open floor in the simulator. The bars are G8's as
written. Run 2 counts for G8, beside run 1's record.
