# 3.192 No-go zones read as floor by the room cut and the goal features

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.192; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the user, 2026-11-20, through the orchestrator) 3.161.B's Z5 failed
the same way twice: 82 finds of 96 with zones against 87 without, on `dev`
before and after 3.191.A (`3.161-no-go-zones-on-twin.md`, "Part 1's
result"). The trace found a zone in every loss, but not as a goal moved or
refused (one loss excepted): explore makes its whole choice on its masked
map, where a zone's cells, grown by the robot's half-width, are
`CELL_NOGO`, and the room cut (`brain/room_labels.py segment`) and the goal
features (`brain/goal_policy.py coarse`, `goal_context`, `features`,
`room_features`) read that value as a wall (or as neither free nor unknown).
So rooms split at a zone and every candidate's features change, even where
no candidate lies in a zone, and the learned policy picks differently.

**The question 3.161 left, answered here.** A zone is a wall for *reach*
(no frontier, no goal, no path inside one: 3.161 D3, unchanged) and floor
for *understanding* (the room cut, the room labels found during a find, the
goal features and the small-room cut read the map as it is, without the
zones). A person's zone says "do not go there", not "there is a wall".

## 3.192.A: criteria and design (written 2026-11-20, before building)

**D1. Two maps in one choice.** In `Explorer._choose_next`, with zones in
force, the map before masking is kept as the choice's *floor map*; the
masked map is used, as on `dev`, for `reach`, `find_frontiers`,
`find_view_gaps`, the room goal's reach, the small-room candidates and every
send. The floor map is what these read:
* `goal_context` and `goal_features` (the pool's features),
* `goal_policy.extend` (the room features: `coarse` then `segment`),
* `_goal_types` (the map's room labels, and the find's own labels source
  `goal_labels`, which cuts rooms),
* the small-room cut's `segment` (its candidate cells stay the masked map's
  free cells reached on the masked map, so a zone cell is never a goal).

With no zones the floor map is the map itself: nothing changes.

**D2. What does not change.** `brain/room_labels.py` and
`brain/goal_policy.py` get no diff (their `CELL_NOGO` reading stays for any
caller that hands them a masked map; explore no longer does). The frozen
references (`tests/room_cut_reference.py`, `tests/goal_features_reference.py`,
`tests/room_features_reference.py`) keep their exact answers. 3.161 D3's
drop of a candidate inside a grown zone, D4's leaving, and the bridge's mask
are unchanged. `robot/safety.py`, `robot/server.py`, `service/slam/`: no
diff (rule 4: a zone is not a safety veto, and this is not either).

`ZoneNav` for nav2 -- with 3.161.B's harness `evaluations/nogo-3161/sweep.py`
