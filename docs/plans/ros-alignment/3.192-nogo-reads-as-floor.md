# 3.192 No-go zones read as floor by the room cut and the goal features

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.192; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the user, 2026-11-20, through the orchestrator) 3.161.B's Z5 failed
the same way twice: 82 finds of 96 with zones against 87 without, on `dev`
before and after 3.191.A (`3.161-no-go-zones-on-twin.md`, "Part 1's
result"). The trace found a zone in every loss, but not as a goal moved or
refused (one loss excepted): explore makes its whole choice on its masked
map, where a zone's cells, grown by the robot's half-width, are
`CELL_NOGO`, and the room cut (`brain/room_labels.py segment`) and the goal
features (`brain/goal_policy.py coarse`, `goal_context`, `features`,
`room_features`) read that value as a wall (or as neither free nor unknown).
So rooms split at a zone and every candidate's features change, even where
no candidate lies in a zone, and the learned policy picks differently.

**The question 3.161 left, answered here.** A zone is a wall for *reach*
(no frontier, no goal, no path inside one: 3.161 D3, unchanged) and floor
for *understanding* (the room cut, the room labels found during a find, the
goal features and the small-room cut read the map as it is, without the
zones). A person's zone says "do not go there", not "there is a wall".

## 3.192.A: criteria and design (written 2026-11-20, before building)

**D1. Two maps in one choice.** In `Explorer._choose_next`, with zones in
force, the map before masking is kept as the choice's *floor map*; the
masked map is used, as on `dev`, for `reach`, `find_frontiers`,
`find_view_gaps`, the room goal's reach, the small-room candidates and every
send. The floor map is what these read:
* `goal_context` and `goal_features` (the pool's features),
* `goal_policy.extend` (the room features: `coarse` then `segment`),
* `_goal_types` (the map's room labels, and the find's own labels source
  `goal_labels`, which cuts rooms),
* the small-room cut's `segment` (its candidate cells stay the masked map's
  free cells reached on the masked map, so a zone cell is never a goal).

With no zones the floor map is the map itself: nothing changes.

**D2. What does not change.** `brain/room_labels.py` and
`brain/goal_policy.py` get no diff (their `CELL_NOGO` reading stays for any
caller that hands them a masked map; explore no longer does). The frozen
references (`tests/room_cut_reference.py`, `tests/goal_features_reference.py`,
`tests/room_features_reference.py`) keep their exact answers. 3.161 D3's
drop of a candidate inside a grown zone, D4's leaving, and the bridge's mask
are unchanged. `robot/safety.py`, `robot/server.py`, `service/slam/`: no
diff (rule 4: a zone is not a safety veto, and this is not either).

## 3.192.A amendment 1 (written 2026-11-20, before building; the orchestrator's word, option B plus A)

**FINDING 1: the sweep that failed Z5 runs no goal policy.**
`evaluations/nogo-3161/sweep.py run()` builds `MissionRunner` without
`explore_goal_policy`, so it takes the constructor's default `"rule"`.
Checked live on `procthor:val:26` s0, both arms: `agent.goal_policy is None`,
`agent.ask is None`. Under the rule a find never calls `goal_features`,
`goal_policy.extend`, `room_features` or `coarse`; `segment()` runs only in a
survey's small-room goal. So the room cut and the goal features take no part
in Z5's find arm, and 3.161's trace sentence ("the room cut and the goal
features read it as a wall") cannot explain its 7 losses. The shipped chooser
is `explore_goal_policy: rooms` (`config/robot.yaml`), which the sweep does
not measure.

**FINDING 2: the zones change the frontier set itself.** The first choice of
two lost missions (scratch probe; `find_frontiers` on the masked map against
the zone-free map; goal, path m, size m, distance to the nearest zone m):

In val:26 the no-zone arm's best frontier, 0.27 m from a zone, does not exist
in the zones arm: part of its cells fall in the zone grown by 0.12 m, so the
cluster is cut and its pieces, middles and goals move. In val:3 a zone splits
off a new best frontier the no-zone arm never had. This is 3.161 D3's clause
"a frontier cell inside a zone is no frontier" plus frontier cells read off
the masked map.

**3.161 D3, amended here (3.192).** D3's core is unchanged: *explore never
picks a goal in a zone grown by the robot's half-width*, and no reachable
path crosses one. Its clause "a frontier cell inside a zone is no frontier"
is replaced by: **frontiers are found on the zone-free map (their cells,
pieces, middles and sizes); each frontier's goal cell is taken only from the
masked map's reach (a stop the robot can reach without crossing a grown
zone); a frontier whose cells all lie inside grown zones is dropped.**

**D1, widened.** Besides the room cut, labels and features (A), explore's
`find_frontiers` call reads frontier cells from the floor map and goal cells
from the masked map's reach (B): a `Reach` with the masked map's `order`
and `dist` and the floor map's grid. `brain/frontier.py` gets no diff, and
`tests/frontier_reference.py` keeps its answers; if B cannot be done in
explore alone, the work stops and is reported. `known_near` (the retry
book's mapping progress) reads the floor map too: a zone is not mapped
floor. View gaps stay on the masked map.

## 3.192.B: built and measured (2026-11-20, a cloud session, 4 cores)

map before masking as the choice's floor map (`_floor_of`, `_floor(m)`).

**F1's three losses** (`trace2.py`; the first goal where the arms part, and
where the no-zone arm's goal lies):
* `procthor:val:26` s0 parts at the first goal. Both arms now read the same
  frontier (1.2 m), but the no-zone arm's route to its goal (4.35, 5.25)
  crosses a zone, so on the masked map's reach its goal is another stop,
  (4.95, 6.15). This is the reach D3 asks for (no path through a zone).
* `procthor:val:39` s0 parts at goal 7. The no-zone goal lies **0.04 m inside
  a grown zone**: D3's own drop, unchanged here, as written before the run.
* `procthor:val:44` s1 parts at goal 21, at a goal 4.13 m from every zone.
  The 21 goals before it are the same; likely the routes around the zones
  changed what the camera had seen by then. Not traced further.

So under the rule chooser, the losses that came from cut frontiers are gone
(val:3 s0, val:13 s1, val:39 s1 and val:44 s0 now find). What is left is the
cost of the zones themselves, through reach and D3. Under the shipped chooser
the find rate is equal and the time cost of zones falls from +18.5% to
+5.1%, just over the 5% bar.

**Reviews** (`/code-review` and the Thermos bug pass, both on Sonnet, on the
build commit). Both raised the same point: a frontier mostly inside a zone
keeps its full size and score. That is the amended D3's intended reading
(unknown floor behind a zone can still be seen from outside it), so it is
kept, and F10 measures the risk. Fixed: the small-room pick ranked openness
on the floor map, so a cell at a zone's edge counted as open (code-review);
the floor grid was converted from a list on every choice, now once per map
(Thermos); `_floor_of` is now reset before any early return; the spec and the
comment now say only what reads the floor map; `sweep2.py --chooser` is
checked and `_apply` made idempotent.

**Left.** Four failed bars, each recorded as failed:
* F1 is lost by one under the rule chooser, from reach and D3's drop.
* F1' misses its time bar by 0.08 points.
* F10 counts 5 aborted goals at one zone under the rooms chooser, where
  `dev` already has 4.
* F9 live: one 2 mm entry along a zone's edge. The bridge's cell-centre
  rasterisation leaves up to 0.025 m of a zone's edge passable to nav2, and
  nothing here changed it. A fix belongs in `service/slam/` (mark every cell
  a zone touches, or grow a zone by half a cell). Also seen live, outside
  any bar here: in the Z3 part the robot left the zone drawn round it in
  7.7 s, but the script did not see the zone drawn again before its end
  (`restored: null`). Not traced.

Whether to fix any of these (for example, giving up a frontier once its goals
abort next to a zone, or covering a zone's whole edge in the mask) is a
question for a later step.
