# 3.147 A FORWARD verb outlives a dead brain (3.135.B F6)

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.147; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.135.B's row F6 FAILED on the Jetson in its FORWARD case, 5 of 5
(2026-11-04, 15 W, sim mode, `drive: direct`, ground truth). 3.135
criterion 3 makes it its own section. The fix and its criteria are this
section's to write. F6's nav2-goal case (F6g) failed too, and it is 3.145's.

* *The run:* a frontier mission in `scaled_house` (3.135 amendment 2's
  mover). `kill -9` of the brain 0.15 s into a FORWARD verb.
* *The numbers* (the row's bars in brackets):
  * wheels zero **0.82-0.85 s** after the kill (<= 1.1 s: met);
  * **23.8-24.8 cm** of true motion over the 6 s after the kill
    (<= 15 cm: FAILED, every run).
* *What held:* 0 contacts, 0 late ticks, 0 OOM; the robot server took a new
  driver, and a new mission was driving 4.4-4.8 s after the kill; the board
  was left as found.
* *Cause, as far as the records show:* the verb runs to its end after its
  driver is dead. `robot/server.py` `watchdog_loop()` skips while
  `verb_active` ("a busy brain, not a silent one"), so the 1 s watchdog
  never sees the dead brain. A FORWARD is ~1 s and ~30 cm long, and
  nothing in the verb checks that the driver is still alive. The desktop saw
  the same thing before the board did (3.135 amendment 2: 24.0 cm, the
  `watchdog` plant not caught because the shipped build fails the same
  row).
* *For whoever writes the criteria:* the 15 cm bar was set without data
  (3.135 amendment 1, "F6 re-read after its first run"). Whether to shorten
  the verb, bound it by the brain's liveness, or re-read the bar is this
  section's call, made with the user. A person's `/stop` still ends a verb
  (3.22); this case is a brain that dies with no one pressing Stop.
* *Records:* `evaluations/jetson-fmea-3135/board/rows/F6-20261104-0956/`
  (on `plan/3.135.B-single-faults` until 3.135.B merges), and BOARD-BENCH's
  fault table.
* *Re-test:* `run.py row F6 --runs 5` (board). Risky by CLAUDE.md
  section 6 (the safety and motion path, the watchdogs).
