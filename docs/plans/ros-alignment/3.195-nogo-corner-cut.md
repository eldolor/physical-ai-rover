# 3.195 The robot never cuts a no-go zone's corner

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.195; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the user, 2026-11-20, through the orchestrator) In 3.193's run R1
(`3.193-nogo-mask-edge-redraw.md`, E3 "outside the bar") the robot's true
centre was inside zone `z89cc245b57e8` (x 4.344-4.956, y 1.386-2.514) for two
0.25 s samples: 3.6 mm inside the east edge, then 2.9 mm inside the south
edge, near its south-east corner, on the trip home going west. 3.193's D1
mask already covered the zone, so what is left is nav2's controller (the
keepout mask is on the global costmap only, `nav2.yaml`) or SLAM's offset
from the truth. Which is not known: no SLAM pose was recorded beside those
samples. 3.193's runs 1-3 had 0 entries but came 1.8, 4.1 and 14.2 mm from
a zone.

**The scenario.** R1's corner case and 3.192's F9 are one mission: the same
three zones (`sweep.place_zones("scaled_house", 0)`), the same find, and
both near misses at `z89cc245b57e8`'s south edge on legs past it (F9: 2 mm
inside the south edge at (4.898, 1.388)). Every run below is that mission.

## 3.195.A: criteria and design (written 2026-11-20, before building)

**M1. The cause, measured before choosing the fix.**
`evaluations/nogo-3195/live.py` is 3.193's `live.py` with the SLAM pose
(`/world/pose`, the anchored frame the zones are drawn in) read beside every
truth sample. The truth is read before and after the pose and interpolated to
the pose's instant. Per sample it records the true and the SLAM signed
distance to the nearest zone, and the offset SLAM - truth. It runs on `dev`'s
bridge, unchanged (image `vision-picar-ros:plan-3.195-base0`), **3 runs**.
At each run's closest approach (and at every entry):
* the SLAM pose is as close to the zone as the truth, or closer (within 5 mm):
  **controller** (nav2 drove there on its own estimate);
* the SLAM pose is at least 10 mm further out than the truth, and the
  offset points into the zone: **SLAM offset**.

The verdict is the majority over the 3 runs' closest approaches. If it is
SLAM offset and the offset is larger than one cell (0.05 m), no fix inside
`service/slam/` is honest (a margin would have to cover a SLAM error, not a
path), and the section stops and reports (the brief).

**Why not keepout on the local costmap.** The controller (Regulated Pure
Pursuit, `use_collision_detection: true`) checks the chassis footprint, not
the centre, against the local costmap. Zones are lethal to the centre only
and are not inflated, so any plan within the half-width (0.12 m) of a zone
would read as a collision and the controller would stop, not steer. 3.68
amendment 1 saw the same for spots (`ENGINEERING.md`: "a spot near the robot
would make the controller's and the recoveries' footprint checks refuse even
a turn"). Recorded as reasoning, not measured.

**D1. A zone is drawn grown by one cell** (`GROW_M` = 0.05 m), if M1 does not
stop the section. The bridge offsets each zone polygon outward by 0.05 m
(each edge moved out along its normal, the corners mitred, so a rectangle
stays a rectangle 0.10 m longer each way) and draws the grown polygon with
3.193's D1 rule (every cell it overlaps). `keepout.mask()` takes the grown
polygon from the bridge, so its default stays 3.193's and 3.193's unit tests
run unchanged.
* **D4 with the margin.** The depth rule (more than `SUSPEND_DEPTH_M` inside)
  and the leave rule (`LEAVE_M` clear) still measure from the zone itself, as
  a person drew it. The boxed rule tests the cells actually drawn: the grown
  polygon's. A robot in the margin or just inside the zone is then either
  boxed (the zone is suspended, so it plans out) or has an undrawn
  4-neighbour.
* **What does not change.** The robot server's D5 refusal (it still tests
  the zone itself; a tap in the margin is accepted and NavFn's 0.10 m
  tolerance plans to the nearest free cell). Explore's D3 (its 0.12 m growth
  is wider than the margin). Spots (their own loop). The routes. `nav2.yaml`.
  `robot/`, `brain/`, `control/`, `world/`.

**Criteria (CLAUDE.md section 7).** Live runs as M1, but on this branch's
image (`vision-picar-ros:plan-3.195`, `build-cloud.sh`),
`PICAR_ROS_NETWORK=host`, a fresh maps volume and zone store per run, the
shipped chooser, the truth every 0.25 s. **10 runs**, chosen before
measuring. On `dev` 1 run in 4 (R1, then runs 1-3) entered a zone, so 0 in
10 runs from a system that still entered 1 in 4 would happen 0.75^10 = 6% of
the time. The closest-approach bar (C2) asks for a margin as well, not luck.

## 3.195.A amendment 1 (written 2026-11-20, after M1 and the first review, before the C runs counted)

**D1's corners are rounded, not mitred.** The first build mitred the corners
(a rectangle grown into a rectangle). `/code-review` (Sonnet) measured its
drawn cells reaching 0.14 m past a zone's corner. That is past explore's
0.12 m (`world/nogo.py HALF_WIDTH_M`, D3: where explore assumes nav2 may put
the centre), and past C6's bar as written (0.05 m plus a cell's diagonal,
0.121 m). D1 now draws every point within 0.05 m of the zone: its convex hull
offset outward, each corner an arc of radius 0.05 m (8 segments just outside
the arc, 0.24 mm proud). Two C runs had been made on the mitred image (34.5
and 40.9 mm closest, 0 entries, both found in 76.2 and 76.7 s). They are kept
as `mitre-1.json` and `mitre-2.json` and are not counted. The 10 C runs are
made on the rounded build.

**C10 (recorded, no bar): D4 suspensions during each find**, from the
bridge's `suspended_count` before and after the mission. The review found
that a robot just outside a drawn corner (or a slanted drawn edge) can be
boxed, which leaves the whole zone out until the robot is 0.30 m clear.
Measured in process: 21 of 7,426 positions near the corners of axis-aligned
zones, 148 of 7,270 near the corners of rotated ones (mitred: 0 and 257).
Along slanted edges it happens with or without the margin (3.193's
touched-cell rule): 641 of 12,000 positions without the margin, 558 of
12,000 with it. C7's "hugging" test is narrowed to the straight edges of
axis-aligned zones, where it holds (0 of 6,000 on four phases).

## 3.195.A amendment 2 (written 2026-11-20, after review round 2 and the Thermos pass, before the C runs counted)

**D4 with the margin, corrected.** Both reviewers found, and probed, that a
robot inside the margin but outside the zone, along a straight axis-aligned
edge, is boxed by the margin's cells on some lattice phases (at phase (0.049,
0.049), every 0-49 mm outside the edge). D4 then left the whole zone out until
the robot was 0.30 m clear, which would let nav2 plan through the zone. M1
showed the controller riding about 25 mm into the drawn cells, so this case
would be met. **D2 (new). The margin alone comes off.** When the drawn zones
box the robot while it is outside the zone (or no more than 0.05 m inside),
each margined zone that draws one of the robot's five cells is drawn bare,
as 3.193 drew it, until the robot is 0.15 m clear of the zone
(`keepout.margins_off`, logged `zone_margin_off` / `zone_margin_on`, `/health`
`nogo.margin_off`). D4's whole-zone test then reads the zones as drawn. Also
from the reviews: the centre-inside cells are filled a row at a time (a grown
zone's 40 corners had tripled a large zone's redraw time under the bridge's
lock: 0.29 s, now 0.005 s). The scorer counts a run without a closest approach
as invalid, never as 0 entries, and matches only the `return docked:` line.

C7 gains: (unit) a robot 0-50 mm outside a straight edge, on five phases,
never has the zone left out, and every point inside the zone stays lethal;
D4's free-4-neighbour promise holds through the bridge's whole step
(margins, then suspension) over 10,000 robots, two-zone gaps included. C10
also records the bridge's `zone_margin_off` and `zone_suspended` lines from
each mission and its trip home. One C run had been made on amendment 1's
build (30.6 mm closest, found in 74.2 s, 0 suspensions). It is kept as
`round-1.json` and not counted.

## 3.195.A amendment 3 (written 2026-11-20, after review round 3, before the C runs counted)

Review round 3 (`/code-review` and the Thermos pass, Sonnet) on D2:
* **A margin comes back as soon as it would not box the robot**, not only at
  0.15 m (`/code-review`: a robot carried into a margin and then following
  the edge kept the zone bare to the next corner, the case this section is
  for). 0.15 m stays as the outer limit.
* **No neighbour loses its margin for a boxing D4 resolves** (both
  reviewers). Zones D4 leaves out this step (suspended, deeper than 0.05 m,
  fresh within 0.30 m) are not counted. Margins come off one zone at a time,
  nearest first, only until the robot is unboxed, and none come off when even
  the bare zones box it.
* A suspended zone is never also listed bare. A zone re-posted with another
  shape starts with its margin. The scorer and the live script record a
  missing count or log as missing, never as 0, and the log is cut to the
  mission.

One C run had been made on amendment 2's build: 74.9 mm closest, found in
74.2 s, 0 entries. It is kept as `amend2-1.json` and not counted.

## 3.195.A amendment 4 (written 2026-11-20, after review round 4, with C runs 1-3 made and none yet counted)

Review round 4 (Thermos-rubric pass, Sonnet) found two defects in amendment
3's margin return, both only while a margin is off:
* **A margin flapped at 5 Hz** for a robot standing on a cell boundary with
  a mm of pose jitter. The return now needs the margin to box the robot
  nowhere within `HYST_M` (0.01 m) of the pose. Test: 900 still robots
  jittering 1 mm across a boundary change their margins at most once (red
  with `HYST_M` = 0).
* **The greedy take-off could strip one margin more than needed** for a
  tick. Margins taken before the one that unboxed the robot are now put back
  where they are not needed.

**Which runs count.** The C runs carry on with amendment 3's image. Both
changes run only when a margin comes off (`zone_margin_off` in the bridge's
log). With no margin ever off, `margins_off` returns the empty set on both
builds, so a run whose mission log (`bridge_zone_log_mission`, the find and
its trip home) has no `zone_margin_off` line ran the same code path as the
final build, and it counts. A run with one is rerun on the final build. One
more run (`final-1.json`) is made on the final build as a smoke check,
recorded beside C1-C5 and not counted in them.
