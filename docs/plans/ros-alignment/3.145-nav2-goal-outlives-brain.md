# 3.145 A nav2 goal outlives a dead or hung brain (3.135.B F6g, F7g)

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.145; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.135.B's rows F6g and F7g FAILED on the Jetson, 5 of 5 each
(2026-11-04, the harness at `3.135.B`'s branch on `dev` `a23476f4`+, 15 W,
sim mode, `drive: ros`, judged on the simulator's ground truth). 3.135
criterion 3 makes a failed row its own section, and this is theirs: one
cause. The fix and its criteria are this section's to write; the finding is
below.

* *The runs:* a plain `explore` mission in `scaled_house` with the ROS
  container (SLAM + nav2). The fault fires 0.15 s into a FORWARD while the
  robot server holds an active nav2 goal and no brain verb holds the robot
  (amendments 5, 10, 11).
  * F6g: SIGKILL the brain.
  * F7g: SIGSTOP the brain 30 s, then SIGCONT.
* *The numbers* (bars: wheels zero <= 1.1 s, true path over the next 6 s
  <= 15 cm):
  * F6g: wheels zero **2.80-3.33 s** after the kill, **16.9-18.5 cm** moved.
  * F7g: **2.79-3.15 s**, **17.1-18.3 cm**.
  * F7g's other half met: no wheel setpoint in the 10 s after SIGCONT.
  * 0 contacts and 0 late ticks in every run; a new mission driving 4.9-6.6
    s later; the board left as found.
* *The cause:* under `drive: ros`, nav2's wheel commands take the
  actuator's route (`/wheels`, driver `ros`), which is not arbitrated and
  keeps the robot server's watchdog fed. A goal the brain sent therefore
  drives on to its end (~3 s, ~18 cm here) after the brain that owns it has
  died or frozen. The B3.1 watchdog guards a silent driver, and nav2 is not
  silent. On the desktop the planted watchdog changed nothing (3.135.B's
  criterion 4 record, `desktop/b-goal/`).
* *A design question, put to the user by the orchestrating session
  (2026-11-04):* should a brain-owned nav2 goal outlive its brain under
  `drive: ros`? The answer decides the fix; the rows are scored as written.
* *Records:* `evaluations/jetson-fmea-3135/board/rows/F6g-20261104-0825/`, `evaluations/jetson-fmea-3135/board/rows/F7g-20261104-0828/` (on
  `plan/3.135.B-single-faults` until 3.135.B merges), BOARD-BENCH's fault
  table.
* *Re-test:* `python evaluations/jetson-fmea-3135/run.py row F6g --runs 5`
  and `row F7g --runs 5` (board); `plant_bugs.py --rows F6g,F7g` (desktop).
  The safety path and who-drives arbitration make this risky (CLAUDE.md
  section 6).
