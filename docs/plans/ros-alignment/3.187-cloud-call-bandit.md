## The decision

On each frame the tier either asks the cloud (`/navigate`, paid) or does
not. Step A decides whether the learned arms are two (cloud or not, as 3.65)
or three (no one, local VLM, cloud), and records the user's choice:
**three** (the user, 2026-11-18); step A below says what that changes. The
bandit decides from what the robot knows locally, never from labels. Its
context, from 3.65: the best candidate's probability this frame and its
streak, frames since the last call, frames since mission start, turns since
the last call, whether the target has a COCO word, and whether anything
passed the gate in the last few frames. A adds the local VLM's answer and
score where it ran.

**Value of a call (3.65's, from the walks' ground truth).** A call is
*useful* when it changes what the robot should believe:

* the target is visible (`labels.json`), the local tier did not pass the
  gate, and the cloud says visible (a sighting local perception missed); or
* the local tier passed the gate, the target is not visible, and the cloud
  says not visible (a local false positive caught).

Every other call is *spent* for nothing. Reward = useful - lambda x cost,
lambda swept and reported, never chosen after looking at held-out results.

**What stays out.** Learning on the car while it runs (the reward needs
labels); mission start always calls (the tier's 2.5 rule, outside the
bandit); arrival's confirmation (3.80.O: only the cloud confirms a
local-led arrival). A learner only chooses among actions the rules allow.

## Criteria (carried from 3.65 phase 1, 2026-10-09; CLAUDE.md section 7)

Step A may amend these only before C's replay data exists, in writing.

1. **Same help for less money:** on the held-out walks, useful calls
   captured >= today's triggers', with **>= 30% fewer calls**; or more
   useful calls at no more calls.
2. **No walk abandoned:** no held-out walk with a useful call under today's
   triggers ends with none under the bandit.
3. **Mission start kept:** a mission's first frame still calls.
4. **Cost reported** in calls and dollars per walk, beside useful calls per
   walk (stability paired with progress).
5. **Through the real path:** behind `tier_call_policy: learned` (default
   `fixed`), the simulator's tiered sweep keeps 3.46 criterion 1's safety
   property (no new contacts, identical safety vetoes) and its arrival rate
   does not fall; one live tiered mission through the brain's HTTP API runs
   with it on.
6. **Pinned in a test** that a regression fails.

**Split (3.65's):** by walk, never by frame; seed 3651; about a third of the
walks held out, every target kept in both halves where it allows. The policy
is a logistic model, small and readable, trained on the training walks only.

**Baselines.** Today's triggers as recorded (`_tier.trigger` in each
`walk.jsonl`) or replayed through `TieredVision` on the same outputs. A sets
which baseline judges criterion 1 now that the local VLM exists (cloud-only
triggers, or the tree with the local VLM first) and reports both. Value of
information over calibrated confidences (the survey's approach 1) is
reported beside the bandit on the same held-out walks: a comparison, not a
bar.

### A2. Three arms: the reward, a call's value, the baselines

**The decision, twice per frame.** First: no one, the local VLM, or the
cloud. Second, only after a local answer: stop, or also ask the cloud with
that answer in the context. Today's tree makes the same two choices
(`_who_choose_local`, then the disagreement or `local_max_unable`
escalation). The rules still bound both (the call cap, one call at a time,
mission start, arrival).

**The value of an answer.** The belief starts as the gate's (b0), becomes
VE's decision if VE was asked and decided (b1), then the cloud's
`target_visible` if the cloud was asked (b2). Per frame, the answer is
**useful** (+1) when the final belief is right and b0 was wrong (3.65's two
kinds), **harmful** (-1) when the final belief is wrong and b0 was right,
and spent otherwise. The cloud's value is judged against b1, not b0: a cloud
call that only repeats VE's correction is spent.

**Reward** = useful - harmful - lambda x dollars - mu x local seconds.
Dollars are B's measured cost per call; local seconds are each frame's VE
`rtt_ms` in `qwen-VE.jsonl` (p50 1.16 s). Grids fixed now: lambda in {0, 5,
10, 25, 50, 100} useful answers per dollar; mu in {0, 0.01, 0.05} per
second. Every cell is reported. The cell the criteria are judged at is
chosen by leave-one-walk-out on the training walks only.

**The baselines, both replayed by D over the same rows** (`TieredVision`,
today's defaults, `tier_async_cloud` off as 3.91 B6, the cloud a stub that
returns C's answer):

* **The tree:** the local VLM first (`tier_local_vlm_url` set), VE's
  recorded answer as the local tier. This is what the board runs (3.152).
* **Cloud-only triggers:** 3.65's baseline, the local VLM off.

**Criterion 1 is judged against the tree**, with the cloud-only baseline
reported beside it in 3.65's own wording. A learned flag that beats only a
configuration the board no longer runs earns nothing. Against the tree,
3.65's "30% fewer calls" is empty: the tree made 0 paid calls on each of
3.91 B6's four walks. Hence A3's amendment 1.

**The local VLM's answers need neither the Jetson nor the desktop.**
3.92.A put the live frame question (`/identity`, `box: true`, 6 s
deadline) to VE on the Jetson at 15 W for all 2,261 frames on 2026-10-22,

**The context (3.65's, amended).** The best candidate's probability and

### A3. Amendments to criteria 1-6 (2026-11-18, before any C data)

1. **Criterion 1, against the tree.** On the held-out walks: (a) the final
   belief is right on at least as many frames as under the tree (useful -
   harmful no lower); and (b) one of:
   * it corrects >= 50% of the frames where VE decided wrong and the cloud
     is right, with paid calls <= 10% of the frames the tree put to VE
     (3.92 criterion 4b's shape: flag at most 10%, catch at least 50%);
   * or it keeps (a) with >= 30% fewer local questions and no more paid
     calls.

   If the held-out walks hold fewer than 20 frames where VE is wrong and
   the cloud right, the first branch is reported "too few to judge" (3.92
   criterion 4's rule), not met or unmet. **Why:** the cloud's money is
   already off the table under the tree; what is left to win is VE's
   mistakes, or VE's time.
2. **Criterion 2, three arms.** No held-out walk with a useful answer from
   either arm under the tree ends with none under the bandit. The
   cloud-only baseline's version is reported. **Why:** a walk where VE
   alone found the target counts as helped.
3. **Criterion 3's wording.** A mission's first frame still asks, whoever
   the tree asks first (the local VLM when configured, else the cloud).
   **Why:** under the tree, mission start goes to the local tier (B6: 0
   paid calls). "Still calls" would add a paid call the board does not
   make today.
4. **Criterion 4 adds the free arm.** Per walk: paid calls and dollars,
   local questions and local seconds, beside useful and harmful answers.
5. **The reward adds harmful (-1).** 3.65 treated a call's answer as right.
   VE is wrong on 77 frames and the cloud's errors are not yet measured, so
   an answer can turn a right belief wrong.
6. **C replays all 2,261 frames**, not only those without an answer: one

### G0. Value of information: the bars (2026-11-19, written before any scoring)

Free: no `/navigate`, no Bedrock. No product code changes. Files go in
`evaluations/cloud-bandit-3187/voi/`, with the pin in
`tests/test_cloud_bandit_3187_voi.py`. When this was written, no cloud
answer outside B's 20 had been read for G. The local counts below come
from 3.92.A's rows, which were already in the repo.

**What value of information (VOI) decides.** The two choices of A2, per
frame, made only from what the robot knows on that frame:

1. **Who to ask:** no one, the local VLM, or the cloud.
2. **After a local answer:** stop, or also ask the cloud.

It decides by the expected gain in being right, set against the price:

* A calibrated probability that the target is in view, before VE is asked
  (`m0`, from perception) and after VE has answered (`m1`, from
  perception plus VE's answer).
* The cloud's two error rates, from the training walks.
* The rule asks when the gain is at least lambda x $0.010645 per paid call
  (B2's measured mean), or mu x VE's seconds per local question (the
  expected seconds when choosing, each frame's recorded `rtt_ms` when
  scoring).
* The local arm's value looks one step ahead: the expectation over VE's
  yes or no of the better of stopping and then asking the cloud.

**The calibrations, fitted on the training walks' labels only.** Labels
exist on every frame, so no cloud answer is needed for these. They are
logistic fits (3.92's `fit_logistic`):

* `m0`: P(in view | perception), on perception DETECTED and the logit of
  its P. 3.92.A's rows keep no P for an absent frame.
* `mv`: P(VE says yes | perception).
* `m1`: P(in view | perception, VE), adding VE's yes and no, the logit of
  its score, and their products with DETECTED.

VE's answer is 3.92.A's recorded one (VE is deterministic on a frame,
3.80.B). An unable VE answer leaves the belief where it was.

**The comparison: today's fixed rule, on the same frames.**

* **F, the tree.** The real `TieredVision`, built with `config/robot.yaml`'s
  tier keys as `control/brain_server.py` builds it, with these stand-ins:
  * a stub local VLM that returns 3.92.A's recorded VE answer
    (`tier_local_vlm_url` set);
  * a stub pipeline that returns 3.92.A's perception row;
  * a stub cloud that returns the sample's recorded answer;
  * `tier_async_cloud` off (as 3.91 B6 and A2).

  Each walk runs frame by frame, in order, as one mission. Which frames F
  put to VE and to the cloud is read off the stubs' calls.
* **F0, cloud-only triggers.** As F, with the local VLM off. Reported, not
  a bar.
* **Limits of the replay, recorded now:**
  * Perception's rows keep no sub-gate P, so the uncertain-band trigger
    cannot fire.
  * The phone walks are open loop, so an answer changes no later frame.
  * A direction question (`tier_local_direction`, off in the yaml) has no
    recorded answer. If it is asked, it is answered `unsupported`, and the
    frames it touches are counted.

**How a frame is scored (A2's).** The belief starts as the gate's (b0). It
becomes VE's decision if VE was asked and decided (b1), then the cloud's
`target_visible` if the cloud was asked (b2). Against `labels.json`:

* **useful:** the final belief is right and b0 was wrong;
* **harmful:** the final belief is wrong and b0 was right;
* **net** = useful - harmful.

A frame where no one was asked keeps b0. Each policy is also scored on:

* paid calls and dollars (calls x $0.010645);
* local questions and local seconds;
* **D**, the frames where VE's recorded decision is wrong and the cloud's
  recorded answer is right, and how many of them the policy ends right on.

* Each walk is scored by calibrations and cloud rates fitted without it.
* A cell passes on training when it meets G1, G2 and G3 below there.
  Branch (b)(i) counts only if training D >= 20.
* The judged cell is the passing cell with the highest lambda, ties going
  to the highest mu: the cheapest cell that still does the job.
* If none passes, the judged cell is the one with the highest training
  net, and G is judged on it all the same.

Held-out results never choose the cell.

**The verdict, a rule fixed now:**

* **"VOI enough: C is not needed"** when two things hold:
  * on W's held-out walks, G1, G2 and G3 are met;
  * on P's held-out walks, neither G1 nor G3 fails. P's branch (b)(i) is

* **"C needed"** when G1, G2 or G3 fails on W's held-out walks, or G1 or
  G3 fails on P's. VOI then falls short on the answers in hand, and the

* **"Sample too small to tell"** when W's held-out D is under 20 and
  branch (ii) is not met. G2 can then be neither met nor failed.
