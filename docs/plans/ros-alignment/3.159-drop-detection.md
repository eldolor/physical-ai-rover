# 3.159 No fall at a stair: a drop ahead stops the robot

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.159; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the user, 2026-11-06: features of a robot vacuum (Dreame) worth leveraging, for full autonomy; 3.158) Every robot vacuum stops at a stair edge with downward cliff
sensors. This robot has nothing like it: `robot/safety.py` vets a move
against the depth-grid cone, the lidar's swept corridor and 3.74's floor
band, all of which look for *things*, not for *missing floor*. The lidar
scans at its own height and sees a stairwell as open space. The user's
house has a basement and a stair hall (3.122's walks), so for a robot sent
out alone a drop is the likeliest serious accident. Priority: high, after
the dead-brain fixes (3.145-3.147).

**Scope.**
* A drop check in series with the cone, corridor and floor band, by their
  rules (CLAUDE.md rule 4 and section 6 "Safety"): the floor ahead, from the
  OAK-D's depth, lower than the floor plane by more than a threshold, or not
  seen where floor should be, refuses FORWARD (and the swept turn that
  would carry the chassis over it) with a named reason. An unusable zone is
  never clear (M3).
* Blind range: what the camera cannot see under the bumper (3.74's known
  gap) is stated, with the margin it needs.
* A simulated stairwell (a drop cell type in the grid world and the
  renderer's depth) so it is judged on ground truth in the sweeps
  (`tests/footprint_sweep.py`'s way), never on the readings the veto uses.
* nav2: the drop marked into the costmap (keepout) so plans avoid it, not
  only the veto.
* On the Rover: whether the camera's floor view suffices, or cheap downward
  IR sensors on the board's spare inputs are needed (decided by step C's
  numbers).

**Criteria (written in step A, before building; CLAUDE.md section 7).**
At least: 0 falls over the sweep's starts toward a drop at every speed the
robot uses (ground truth), the stop distance from the edge (p99 with a bar),
progress kept (no stop on flat floor, rugs or a door threshold: false-stop
rate with a bar), and one live run.
