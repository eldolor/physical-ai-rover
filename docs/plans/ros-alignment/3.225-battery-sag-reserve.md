# 3.225 The home trip's battery prediction reads motor sag as drain

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.225; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** On 2026-11-26 3.140 ran the Rover's first two autonomous surveys,
each with a 600 s limit. Both ended `out_of_budget` about 100 s in, on
3.156 D3's return reserve:

* 20:09: `return reserve -- battery: 11.13 V now, 10.56 V predicted at the
  dock (floor 10.5 V + 0.1)`, after 222 s (decided at 103 s);
* 20:15: `... 11.06 V now, 10.53 V predicted ...`, after 142 s (decided at
  102 s).

The pack was not low. It read 11.8 V at the first start and 11.36-11.44 V
at rest after both runs, against a 10.5 V floor; 3.137's estimate is about
1-1.25 h a charge. In each run one sample read about 0.5-0.6 V low while
the motors pulled current (11.75 -> 11.13 V; 11.54 -> 11.06 V in one 5 s
step of the trace). `HomeBudget.drain_v_s()` takes the first and the last
distinct sample, so the sag became a drain of 0.0066 and 0.0060 V/s
(about 22 V an hour, impossible for a 3S pack), and `v_now`, the same
sagged sample, was extrapolated from. The real drain is about 2.1 V over
an hour (~0.0006 V/s, 0.035 V a minute), an order below the sag, so no
two raw samples a minute or two apart can measure it.

The traces are 3.140's (`/tmp/survey3140.jsonl`, `/tmp/survey3140b.jsonl`
on the board: the brain's `/mission/status` every 5 s); their voltage
columns are pinned as
`evaluations/battery-sag-3225/survey-traces-20261126.json`.

**Design.** The reserve is a plan, not the safety: the robot server's
ladder (3.137: Low/Reserve/Power-off, its own distinct-samples rule) and
the runner's floor check (`BATTERY_LOW_READINGS` raw readings in a row)
stay as they are and keep reading raw voltages. Only `HomeBudget`
changes:

* **Read the pack, not the motors.** A sag only ever lowers a reading,
  so the pack's own voltage over a window is the window's highest sample.
  `rested_v()` is the maximum distinct sample within the last
  `REST_WINDOW_S` (60 s).
* **Drain between rested values.** `drain_v_s()` is the drop from the
  maximum of the samples in the first `REST_WINDOW_S` of the record to
  `rested_v()`, over the span from the first sample to the last, once that
  span is at least `DRAIN_SPAN_S` (60 s, unchanged); never below 0.
* **Predict from the rested value.** `reserve_reason` uses `rested_v()`
  where it used the raw `v_now`, when it has samples.

A pack that is really failing lowers every reading, the highest too, so
the reserve still sees it, at most `REST_WINDOW_S` later. Known limit:
a mission that starts at rest and then drives without a pause for a whole
window reads its first rested value higher than the rest of the record
(loaded); the drain is then overstated by that sag over the span, which
dilutes as the span grows (criterion 2 measures it).

**Criteria (written 2026-11-26, before building; CLAUDE.md section 7).**

1. **Tonight's two surveys would not have ended.** Replaying each trace's
   voltage samples through `HomeBudget` at the run's own `T_home` (57.5 s
   and 58.7 s), with the runner's sampling (distinct values only), never
   returns a battery reserve, at any sample of the search. On `dev` the
   same replay returns one in both (the test is red first).
2. **A real drain still ends the search, on time.** A synthetic pack
   falling at 0.002 V/s from 11.2 V (rested), sampled every 12 s with a
   0.5 V sag on alternate samples, `T_home` 60 s, floor 10.5 V: the
   reserve fires no later than `REST_WINDOW_S` + 12 s after the noise-free
   prediction (`v - 1.5 * r * T_home`, from the true rested voltage and
   the true rate) crosses 10.6 V, and not before it crosses 10.7 V. The
   known-limit case (rested 11.8 V for 30 s, then every sample 0.4 V low,
   true drain 0.0006 V/s, `T_home` 60 s): no reserve within 1,200 s.
3. **Nothing else moves.** Every existing test of `HomeBudget`, the dock
   return and recharge (`tests/test_dock_return.py`,
   `tests/test_resume_recharge.py`, `tests/test_dock_navigator.py`) passes
   unchanged, or the change is named here with why. The fast tier passes
   (`ready`).
4. **The real path.** One survey on the simulator through the brain's HTTP
   API, `return_to_dock` on and a simulated battery with motor-start sag,
   ends on its time limit or `exhausted`, never on
   `return reserve -- battery`.

**Amendment 1 (2026-11-26, after the first build's review and before
criterion 4's run; criteria 1-3 had run, all met).** The review of
`2ff80eae` found that the window's highest reading is not a rested reading
once the robot drives for more than `REST_WINDOW_S` without a pause, as a
survey can: every reading in the window is then sagged, and the sag reads
as drain again (a pack at 11.2 V with a 0.5 V sag, driving from 10 s,
ends at ~90 s). It also found that `rested_v()` lagged a draining pack by up
to `r * REST_WINDOW_S`, that a window's maximum of noisy readings is biased
high, and that the windows ended at the last distinct sample, not now. The
design changes before criterion 4 is run:

* **The robot server says which readings were taken at rest.** It already
  decides this for its ladder (no wheel turned for `BATTERY_REST_S`,
  0.5 s); `GET /battery` adds `at_rest` (true, false, or null when it has
  not polled), computed with the reading it describes. The ladder is
  unchanged.
* **`HomeBudget` measures between readings at rest.** The runner passes
  `at_rest` to `observe_battery`. When the record holds at-rest readings,
  only they count: the drain runs between the highest in the record's
  first `REST_WINDOW_S` and the highest in the last `REST_WINDOW_S` before
  NOW (or the latest, when none is that recent), at least `DRAIN_SPAN_S`
  apart, and the pack now is that reading projected forward by the drain
  (`v_b - r * (t - t_b)`). A body that never reports `at_rest` (an older
  robot server, an in-process body) keeps the first build's window rule,
  unprojected; so does a record with no at-rest reading yet.
* Windows end at the time `reserve_reason` is given, not at the last
  distinct sample.

Known limits, recorded: the Rover's board measures its pack every 10 s and
repeats the value in every frame, so a reading the server calls at rest
may have been measured while moving (the ladder's own known limit); a
mission that never stops gets no at-rest reading and so no drain (the
ladder's Low/Reserve, judged at rest, stays the safety); a pack just off
the charger reads its surface charge first (3.156.D's recharge, off by
default). The reserve compares a rested prediction with the floor: the
ladder's Reserve and Power-off are judged at rest, so the trip home's sag
does not fire them; its Low (no stop) can.

Criteria added by the amendment, for the at-rest path:

5. **Driving without a pause is not a drain.** At-rest readings at 11.2 V
   for 10 s, then every reading 0.5 V low and not at rest, the true drain
   0.0006 V/s, `T_home` 60 s: no reserve within 1,200 s.
6. **A real drain at rest ends the search within one sample.** Criterion
   2's pack, its sagged readings marked not at rest: the reserve fires
   within 12 s after the noise-free prediction crosses 10.6 V, never
   before it crosses 10.65 V.
7. **`at_rest` on the wire.** `GET /battery` reports `at_rest` false within
   `BATTERY_REST_S` of a wheel command and true after it, on the simulator.

Criterion 4 stands, run on the amended build.

On the Rover (3.140's, not this section's): the next survey reaches its
600 s limit or the ladder's Low, whichever is first.
