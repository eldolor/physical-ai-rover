# 3.175 A find's memory when the target has moved, and asking it without driving

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.175; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the user, 2026-11-12: features of similar products and projects worth
building while the Rover waits on Waveshare.) Since 3.172.C a find goes first to
where earlier missions on the saved map saw the target (8 of 8 found, median
134 s against 502). Two things the closest research systems have are missing:

* **Things move.** 3.172.C says so itself: no target moved in its runs, so a
  recall to a place the target has left was never measured. DynaMem (NYU, the
  follow-on to OK-Robot) exists because a static memory sends the robot to stale
  places; its memory removes what is no longer there. Here a wrong recall costs
  a drive, and nothing measured says whether the landmark is then forgotten or
  recalled again by the next mission.
* **Asking without driving.** NVIDIA's ReMEmbR answers "where did you see X?"
  from its memory. Here the carried landmarks and 3.162's reports hold the
  answer (a place, a room, a time, a thumbnail), but only a mission reads them.
  A person often wants the answer, not a search.

**Scope.**
* Measure, in the simulator on a saved map, a find whose target was moved
  between missions: to another room, within the room, and out of the house.
* If the measurement says so: a recall that sees nothing at its place lowers
  that landmark for the chain (not only this mission), and recalls are ordered
  by when they were last seen as well as by belief. No line of `robot/` changes.
* A read-only brain route that answers where a named thing was last seen, from
  the carried landmarks and 3.162's reports, and a row for it on the twin's
  Home with "Go check" (an ordinary find). Local only, as the inventory is.
* Sim frames name every object; **on real frames the inventory names only the
  target** (3.46.C closed), so on the car the answer covers things once
  searched for. 3.178's watch list would widen it.

**Criteria (written in step A, before building; CLAUDE.md section 7).** At
least:
* moved target: `found` rate and median seconds beside the no-memory baseline
  (3.172.B's first finds) and beside 3.172.C's unmoved runs, with a bar on the
  time a stale recall may cost;
* the mission after a stale recall sends no recall to that place (100%);
* an unmoved target keeps 3.172.C's numbers (stability half);
* the ask route: right place for every carried target (100% in the sim), "not
  seen" for a name never seen, 0 robot calls, and phone-size evidence;
* contacts 0, every run docked.

## 3.175.A: criteria and design (written 2026-11-12, before any measurement)

**The question.** A find whose target moved between missions on the saved map:
what does a stale recall cost against no memory, and against a fresh one, and is
the stale place recalled again by the next mission? 3.172.C measured only a
target that stayed put.

**What `dev` does today** (read, not measured): a carried landmark named as the
target with belief >= `REPORT_BELIEF` (0.8) is recalled, at most `RECALL_MAX`
(3), one goal each. Looking at a landmark's place without seeing it is a MISS
(`L_MISS` -0.4 per independent look, inside the field and within 3 m); the chain
carries a landmark while its belief is >= `CARRY_MIN_BELIEF` (0.5). A landmark
reported at 0.98 needs about three independent misses to fall under 0.8 and
about five to stop being carried, so one look from a stale recall may leave it
recalled again. That is what A6 tests.

**Instrument** (`evaluations/memory-moved-3175/sweep.py`, in process, as
`tests/dock_sweep.py`): `MissionRunner` -> explore -> `MockRobot` on the
simulator's clock, the stand-in nav `InflatedNav`, the frame's detections. The
chain is real: an `InventoryStore` directory per trial and one saved map's
`frame_id` on every pose, so each mission seeds from the head of the chain and
saves its report as the live brain does. Return to the dock off: the stand-in
nav fails in passages (3.156.B C1), and docking is 3.156's. Truth: the target's
cell at the mission's end state; a contact is a simulator `BLOCKED at` (a
drive cut short by a wall or object; a blocked turn is recorded apart).

**Trials.** Every one-cell object the simulator has that no other label
contains (the found rule's substring match): `complex_house`'s small things and
the red backpack in `scaled_house` and `home_first_floor`. From the house's
dock (its start pose), each target four ways: to **another room** (two, by a
seeded draw of reachable floor), **within its room** (at least 1.5 m from where
it was), and **gone** (out of the house). Per trial, five missions:

| Mission | Memory | Target at |
|---|---|---|
| `seed` | none (an empty store) | where it was (P0) |
| `fresh` | `seed`'s chain | P0, unmoved |
| `stale` | `fresh`'s chain (P0 carried) | P1, moved (or gone) |
| `after` | `stale`'s chain | P1 (or gone) |
| `cold` | none (its own empty store) | P1 (not run for gone: it is an absent target's time limit) |

"Search s" is the simulator's seconds from start to the end state, at
`mission_time_limit_s` 1200.

| # | Metric | Bar | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Moved, in the house: `found` in `stale` | >= `cold`'s count minus 1, over the same trials | progress |
| A2 | Moved, in the house: what the stale recall costs, `stale` minus `cold` search s, paired | median **<= 60 s**, p90 **<= 180 s** | progress |
| A3 | `after` acts on no recall for a carried place within 1.0 m of P0 (a recall goal sent for it, or the place faced where the robot stood) | **100%** of trials, gone included | stability |
| A4 | Moved, in the house: `after` against `cold` | `found` >= `cold`'s; median search s **<=** `cold`'s | progress |
| A5 | Unmoved: `fresh` against `seed` | `found` >= `seed`'s; first goal a `recall` in **>= 95%** of trials whose `seed` found; median search s **<= 50%** of `seed`'s | stability (3.172.C's numbers) |
| A6 | Gone: `stale` and `after` end without `found` | **100%** (and A3) | stability |
| A7 | Wrong `found` (the target over 0.9 m away); contacts | **0**; **0** | stability |

A3 is A4's stability half, and A5 is A1's. 3.175.B is needed if A2, A3 or A4
fails; if all hold, B records `dev`'s numbers and builds nothing.

**What this cannot show.** The stand-in nav is not nav2 (`home_first_floor`'s
passages), sim detections name everything at once, and no mission docks: the
live missions (docked, contacts on footprint truth) are B's, and the car is D's.
