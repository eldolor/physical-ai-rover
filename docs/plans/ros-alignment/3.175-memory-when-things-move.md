# 3.175 A find's memory when the target has moved, and asking it without driving

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.175; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the user, 2026-11-12: features of similar products and projects worth
building while the Rover waits on Waveshare.) Since 3.172.C a find goes first to
where earlier missions on the saved map saw the target (8 of 8 found, median
134 s against 502). Two things the closest research systems have are missing:

* **Things move.** 3.172.C says so itself: no target moved in its runs, so a
  recall to a place the target has left was never measured. DynaMem (NYU, the
  follow-on to OK-Robot) exists because a static memory sends the robot to stale
  places; its memory removes what is no longer there. Here a wrong recall costs
  a drive, and nothing measured says whether the landmark is then forgotten or
  recalled again by the next mission.
* **Asking without driving.** NVIDIA's ReMEmbR answers "where did you see X?"
  from its memory. Here the carried landmarks and 3.162's reports hold the
  answer (a place, a room, a time, a thumbnail), but only a mission reads them.
  A person often wants the answer, not a search.

**Scope.**
* Measure, in the simulator on a saved map, a find whose target was moved
  between missions: to another room, within the room, and out of the house.
* If the measurement says so: a recall that sees nothing at its place lowers
  that landmark for the chain (not only this mission), and recalls are ordered
  by when they were last seen as well as by belief. No line of `robot/` changes.
* A read-only brain route that answers where a named thing was last seen, from
  the carried landmarks and 3.162's reports, and a row for it on the twin's
  Home with "Go check" (an ordinary find). Local only, as the inventory is.
* Sim frames name every object; **on real frames the inventory names only the
  target** (3.46.C closed), so on the car the answer covers things once
  searched for. 3.178's watch list would widen it.

**Criteria (written in step A, before building; CLAUDE.md section 7).** At
least:
* moved target: `found` rate and median seconds beside the no-memory baseline
  (3.172.B's first finds) and beside 3.172.C's unmoved runs, with a bar on the
  time a stale recall may cost;
* the mission after a stale recall sends no recall to that place (100%);
* an unmoved target keeps 3.172.C's numbers (stability half);
* the ask route: right place for every carried target (100% in the sim), "not
  seen" for a name never seen, 0 robot calls, and phone-size evidence;
* contacts 0, every run docked.
