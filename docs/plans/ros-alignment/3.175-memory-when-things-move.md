# 3.175 A find's memory when the target has moved, and asking it without driving

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.175; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the user, 2026-11-12: features of similar products and projects worth
building while the Rover waits on Waveshare.) Since 3.172.C a find goes first to
where earlier missions on the saved map saw the target (8 of 8 found, median
134 s against 502). Two things the closest research systems have are missing:

* **Things move.** 3.172.C says so itself: no target moved in its runs, so a
  recall to a place the target has left was never measured. DynaMem (NYU, the
  follow-on to OK-Robot) exists because a static memory sends the robot to stale
  places; its memory removes what is no longer there. Here a wrong recall costs
  a drive, and nothing measured says whether the landmark is then forgotten or
  recalled again by the next mission.
* **Asking without driving.** NVIDIA's ReMEmbR answers "where did you see X?"
  from its memory. Here the carried landmarks and 3.162's reports hold the
  answer (a place, a room, a time, a thumbnail), but only a mission reads them.
  A person often wants the answer, not a search.

**Scope.**
* Measure, in the simulator on a saved map, a find whose target was moved
  between missions: to another room, within the room, and out of the house.
* If the measurement says so: a recall that sees nothing at its place lowers
  that landmark for the chain (not only this mission), and recalls are ordered
  by when they were last seen as well as by belief. No line of `robot/` changes.
* A read-only brain route that answers where a named thing was last seen, from
  the carried landmarks and 3.162's reports, and a row for it on the twin's
  Home with "Go check" (an ordinary find). Local only, as the inventory is.
* Sim frames name every object; **on real frames the inventory names only the
  target** (3.46.C closed), so on the car the answer covers things once
  searched for. 3.178's watch list would widen it.

**Criteria (written in step A, before building; CLAUDE.md section 7).** At
least:
* moved target: `found` rate and median seconds beside the no-memory baseline
  (3.172.B's first finds) and beside 3.172.C's unmoved runs, with a bar on the
  time a stale recall may cost;
* the mission after a stale recall sends no recall to that place (100%);
* an unmoved target keeps 3.172.C's numbers (stability half);
* the ask route: right place for every carried target (100% in the sim), "not
  seen" for a name never seen, 0 robot calls, and phone-size evidence;
* contacts 0, every run docked.

## 3.175.A: criteria and design (written 2026-11-12, before any measurement)

**The question.** A find whose target moved between missions on the saved map:
what does a stale recall cost against no memory, and against a fresh one, and is
the stale place recalled again by the next mission? 3.172.C measured only a
target that stayed put.

**What `dev` does today** (read, not measured): a carried landmark named as the
target with belief >= `REPORT_BELIEF` (0.8) is recalled, at most `RECALL_MAX`
(3), one goal each. Looking at a landmark's place without seeing it is a MISS
(`L_MISS` -0.4 per independent look, inside the field and within 3 m); the chain
carries a landmark while its belief is >= `CARRY_MIN_BELIEF` (0.5). A landmark
reported at 0.98 needs about three independent misses to fall under 0.8 and
about five to stop being carried, so one look from a stale recall may leave it
recalled again. That is what A6 tests.

**Instrument** (`evaluations/memory-moved-3175/sweep.py`, in process, as
`tests/dock_sweep.py`): `MissionRunner` -> explore -> `MockRobot` on the
simulator's clock, the stand-in nav `InflatedNav`, the frame's detections. The
chain is real: an `InventoryStore` directory per trial and one saved map's
`frame_id` on every pose, so each mission seeds from the head of the chain and
saves its report as the live brain does. Return to the dock off: the stand-in
nav fails in passages (3.156.B C1), and docking is 3.156's. Truth: the target's
cell at the mission's end state; a contact is a simulator `BLOCKED at` (a
drive cut short by a wall or object; a blocked turn is recorded apart).

**Trials.** Every one-cell object the simulator has that no other label
contains (the found rule's substring match): `complex_house`'s small things and
the red backpack in `scaled_house` and `home_first_floor`. From the house's
dock (its start pose), each target four ways: to **another room** (two, by a
seeded draw of reachable floor), **within its room** (at least 1.5 m from where
it was), and **gone** (out of the house). Per trial, five missions:

| Mission | Memory | Target at |
|---|---|---|
| `seed` | none (an empty store) | where it was (P0) |
| `fresh` | `seed`'s chain | P0, unmoved |
| `stale` | `fresh`'s chain (P0 carried) | P1, moved (or gone) |
| `after` | `stale`'s chain | P1 (or gone) |
| `cold` | none (its own empty store) | P1 (not run for gone: it is an absent target's time limit) |

"Search s" is the simulator's seconds from start to the end state, at
`mission_time_limit_s` 1200.

| # | Metric | Bar | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Moved, in the house: `found` in `stale` | >= `cold`'s count minus 1, over the same trials | progress |
| A2 | Moved, in the house: what the stale recall costs, `stale` minus `cold` search s, paired | median **<= 60 s**, p90 **<= 180 s** | progress |
| A3 | `after` acts on no recall for a carried place within 1.0 m of P0 (a recall goal sent for it, or the place faced where the robot stood) | **100%** of trials, gone included | stability |
| A4 | Moved, in the house: `after` against `cold` | `found` >= `cold`'s; median search s **<=** `cold`'s | progress |
| A5 | Unmoved: `fresh` against `seed` | `found` >= `seed`'s; first goal a `recall` in **>= 95%** of trials whose `seed` found; median search s **<= 50%** of `seed`'s | stability (3.172.C's numbers) |
| A6 | Gone: `stale` and `after` end without `found` | **100%** (and A3) | stability |
| A7 | Wrong `found` (the target over 0.9 m away); contacts | **0**; **0** | stability |

A3 is A4's stability half, and A5 is A1's. 3.175.B is needed if A2, A3 or A4
fails; if all hold, B records `dev`'s numbers and builds nothing.

**What this cannot show.** The stand-in nav is not nav2 (`home_first_floor`'s
passages), sim detections name everything at once, and no mission docks: the
live missions (docked, contacts on footprint truth) are B's, and the car is D's.

**Why A5's first-goal half fails: the instrument, not `dev`** (found after the

## 3.175.B amendment B0: the instrument carries the map (written 2026-11-13, before B's runs)

A's A5 finding: `MockWorld` mapped every mission from unknown cells, so a recall
waited for frontiers to uncover its place. On the car the saved map is reloaded
(3.50.D), and 3.172.C's live recalls went first 8 of 8. So, for every run of B:

* each mission of a trial's chain starts from the map the previous mission of
  that chain ended with (`seed` -> `fresh` -> `stale` -> `after`); `seed` and
  `cold` start from unknown cells, as A's did. A moved object's old cell stays
  occupied on the carried map until a scan sees it again, as on a saved map;
* the carried map is only what the previous mission's own scans marked (its
  `MockWorld` cells, free or occupied); no cell it never observed, and no
  object truth beyond what a scan returned off, is carried, so the harness
  leaks nothing the car's saved map would not hold;
* B-dev and B-fix run the same 75 trials (A's seeded draw), and the test pins
  the trial list by its hash, so both arms and A's numbers sit side by side;
* the sweep runs in process, 4 workers on this container's 4 cores; no bar is
  on wall-clock, so that load does not affect a verdict;
* everything else is A's: the same 75 trials, the same five missions, the same
  bars A1-A7, the same scorer. A5 is judged again under it.

**B-dev** is `dev` as it is under B0. B builds its two changes only if B-dev
fails A2, A3 or A4, and writes amendment B1 (what is built) before B-fix runs.

## 3.175.B amendment B1: what B-fix builds (written 2026-11-13, before B-fix's run)

* **Stale at one empty look** (`brain/inventory.py`): a carried landmark not
  yet seen this mission, on its first independent miss, is held to at most
  `STALE_BELIEF` (0.7), before the forgiveness of an isolated miss. It falls
  under `REPORT_BELIEF`, so the next mission does not recall it, and stays
  carried (>= `CARRY_MIN_BELIEF`), so one sighting from a new view reports it
  again. A landmark seen this mission keeps the forgiveness.
* **Newest first** (`brain/inventory.py`, `control/mission_runner.py`
  `_recall_carried`'s sort key): each landmark records `seen_seq`, the
  `carry_seq` of the mission that last saw it; recalls go newest first (a row
  without one last), then by belief.
* Review (before the run): `seen_seq` is set only by a sighting under the
  landmark's own label (another object's vote where it was is not one), and a
  carried `seen_seq` that is not an integer from 0 to the saved report's
  `carry_seq` reads None (recalled last). A run started minutes earlier on the code
  before this review was stopped minutes in and its rows deleted unread.
* B-fix runs the same 75 trials (`fe61b6cf72157245`) with the same bars A1-A7,
  scored by the same scorer, rows in `rows-b-fix.jsonl`. Nothing else changes.

## 3.175.E: criteria and design (written 2026-11-14, before any code or run)

**The question.** 3.175.B left A3 at 23 of 75 and found every one to be a
`stale` mission whose one recall goal ended without arriving (the stand-in nav
aborted it), after which `dev` spent the place: nothing looked at it, so the
stale rule never fired. The same abort costs unmoved targets in `fresh`: of the
23 trials whose `seed` found the target, `fresh` found it again in only **12**
(B-fix's rows). Does one more try fix that, and what does it cost when the
object is really gone?

**Design.** A recall goal that ends in any state but `succeeded` (`aborted`,
`stalled`, `canceled`, `rejected` from the navigator; a refusal before a goal
is taken is 3.172's own rule, unchanged) puts its place back at the front of
the recall list, once: at most `RECALL_TRIES` (2) goals per place per mission.
The second goal goes to another approach point: band floor at least
`RECALL_OTHER_M` (0.3 m, one sim cell) from the first goal's point, else the
best band point again (an abort may be the moment's); a place with no band
floor on the map yet is kept, as 3.172 keeps it. (Amended before any run: the
first draft wanted the retry book's 0.6 m, which the 0.35-0.6 m band beside a
wall cannot offer, and spent the place without it -- a unit test showed it
lost unlooked-at.) The escape or failure handling of the first goal runs first, as for any
goal. Nothing in `robot/` changes.

**nav2 on the car.** nav2's `NavigateToPose` ends ABORTED when the planner finds
no path or the controller's progress checker and recoveries give up; the
bridge reports that state and `RosWorld`/`RemoteNavigator` hand it to
`ExploreAgent._goal_ended(state="aborted")` -- the same call the stand-in nav
makes here. 3.83's `stalled` is explore's own cut on either navigator. So the
rule runs on the car on the path measured here; how often nav2 aborts a
recall in a real house is 3.175.D's to measure.

**Instrument.** 3.175.B's, unchanged: the same 75 trials
(`fe61b6cf72157245`), amendment B0's carried map, the same scorer, rows in
`rows-e.jsonl`, 4 workers in process.

| # | Metric | Bar | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | A3: `after` acts on a recall within 1.0 m of P0 | **<= 5 of 75** (B-fix 23; A's own bar 0 is judged too) | stability |
| E2 | Unmoved: of the trials whose `seed` found, `fresh` found | **>= 20 of 23** (B-fix 12) | progress |
| E3 | A1 and A4 `found` counts | **>=** B-fix's (stale 21, after 19) | progress |

| E5 | Recall goals per mission | **<= `RECALL_MAX` x `RECALL_TRIES`** (6) in every mission | stability (cap) |
| E6 | A2 p90 | **<=** B-fix's 573.5 s | progress |
| E7 | A6 and A7 | 0 found when gone; 0 wrong found, 0 contacts | stability |

**What the numbers say.** The candidate is falsified. A second try does not
reach a view of the old place either.
- Recall goals per mission, across all rows: 0 goals in 223, 1 in 52, 2 in 79,
  4 in 2.
- `after` recalls P0 in 30 trials, against B-fix's 23. That means the
  landmark was still not counted as a miss after both goals. The 7 new trials
  are all among the missions that sent 2 recall goals; none went the other way.
- Of the 62 `stale`/`after` missions that sent 2 or more recall goals, 24
  end `exhausted` at one of three identical end states (614.4 s twice and
  470.8 s, eight missions each, whatever the target). The two failed goals and their escapes cost these missions the
  frontiers that B-fix's missions still explored.
- Inferred, not traced: the stand-in nav's abort at these places is
  structural (a path the inflated plan cannot hold), not momentary. A point
  0.3 m over meets it again, so the retry repeats the failure and pays for
  its escape twice.

**nav2's abort** reaches the same `_goal_ended(state="aborted")` (the design
above), so the result would carry to the car as far as nav2's aborts are
structural. How often they are is 3.175.D's to measure.

**Recorded by review against the unshipped retry** (moot with it reverted,
kept for whoever builds a retry again):
- `RECALL_OTHER_M` is exactly one cell. Whether a neighbouring cell counts as
  "other" is decided by float rounding at 0.3 m.
- `canceled` and `lost` spend a try just as `aborted` does.
- One reviewer would rather keep a place on the list until it is resolved
  than pop it on send and re-insert it on failure.

**Candidates, not opened** (the orchestrator puts them to the user):

- **(a) An aborted recall counts as a miss for the stale rule.** When the goal
  to a carried place ends without arriving, the landmark gets the stale cap as
  if it had been looked at, so the next mission does not recall it.
  - It assumes that a place the robot cannot reach now means the object is not
    there worth going for. That cost needs its own bar.
  - To show: A3 at or below 5 of 75, with A1 and A4 found at or above B-fix's
    21 and 19.
  - Also to show: an unmoved target is not forgotten. `fresh` found among seed's
    23 is at or above 12, and the next mission's found on unmoved trials does
    not fall.
  - Also to show: on the car, how often nav2 aborts a reachable recall
    (3.175.D), because each such abort would forget a real place.
- **(b) The recall goal's approach side is chosen with the planner's clearance.**
  Before sending, `_band_point` picks band floor that the inflated plan can
  reach (one cell of clearance, nav2's inflation), not merely free floor.
  - To show: recall goals that abort fall from B-fix's count. Measure it from a
    row field for the recall goal's end state, which is to be added before the
    run.
  - Also to show: E2 at or above 20 of 23, and A3 and A1/A4 as in (a).
  - Also to show: no rise in A2's p90 (573.5 s).
  - Also to show: on the car, it needs nav2's own costmap through the bridge,
    not a copy of the inflation radius.
