# 3.144 Forward moves refused on a stale lidar scan (3.135.B F9)

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.144; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.135.B's row F9 (lidar stops) FAILED on the Jetson, 5 of 5 runs
(2026-11-03, rev `a3382c18`, 15 W, sim mode, judged on the simulator's
ground truth). 3.135 criterion 3 makes a failed row its own section; this is
F9's. The fix and its criteria are this section's to write; the finding is
below.

* *The run:*
  * a frontier mission in `scaled_house` on 3.135's board stack
    (`SIM_LIDAR=fake`, `SIM_MOTOR_BOARD=fake`, no ROS);
  * the fake D500 SIGSTOPped for 10 s, 0.15 s into a FORWARD, then
    SIGCONTed.
* *The numbers:*
  * true path from the fault + 0.3 s (the scan's freshness bar,
    `robot/lidar_ld19.py` `STALE_S`) to the SIGCONT: **101.2, 101.3,
    101.6, 104.3, 104.6 cm** (bar <= 2 cm);
  * wheels zero 11.04-11.06 s after the fault, i.e. only once scans came
    back;
  * 0 contacts (open floor ahead), 0 late wheel ticks, 0 OOM kills;
  * a new mission driving 3.0-3.2 s later; the board left as found.
* *The cause:* `robot/safety.py` `SafetyController.forward_clearance()`
  answers 0.0 (`path_not_observed`) only when **both** the depth cone faces
  away (`depth_grid_facing_away`) **and** the scan is absent (`no_scan`).
  With the camera facing ahead and the scan stale, the swept corridor gives
  nothing, and the move is vetted on the depth-grid cone alone.
  `reverse_clearance()` already refuses on an unusable scan
  (`astern_not_observed`), so the two directions disagree.
* *The FMEA's "guard today" column for F9 is wrong.* 3.135's table says
  "safety refuses a move without a fresh scan". That holds astern, and
  ahead only when the camera also faces away.
* *Seen first* on the desktop in 3.135.A (amendment 2: "F9 kept moving with
  the lidar stopped"), where the shipped build moved 104.5 cm.
* *Records:* `evaluations/jetson-fmea-3135/board/rows/F9-20261103-1202/`
  (`summary.json`, each run's `result.json`, the truth logs gzipped) and
  BOARD-BENCH.md's fault table, on branch `plan/3.135.B-single-faults`
  until 3.135.B merges.
* *Re-test:* `python evaluations/jetson-fmea-3135/run.py row F9 --runs 5`
  (board) and `plant_bugs.py --rows F9` (desktop, criterion 4) run 3.135's
  harness against a fix. Any safety change is judged on ground truth
  (CLAUDE.md section 6). This is the safety path, so `/code-review` and the
  Thermos pass apply.

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. **A FORWARD is refused on an unusable scan.** On a body whose wheels
   report (`_has_wheels()`) and whose scan is missing or unusable,
   `forward_clearance()` answers `(0.0, "ahead_not_observed")` whatever the
   depth cone says, exactly as `reverse_clearance()` answers
   `astern_not_observed` astern. A FORWARD verb issued then raises
   `SafetyViolation` and the body does not move; a forward component of
   `/wheels` is clamped to zero, rotation untouched (turns stay allowed
   blind, as astern). A body with no wheels (a phone walk, a replay) and a
   body with a usable scan answer as before.
2. **A FORWARD under way stops when the scan goes unusable, and stays
   stopped.** In process, judged on the simulator's ground truth:
   (a) a guarded FORWARD verb whose scan turns unusable mid-move covers
   <= 2 cm of true path after it and ends (clamped, or refused if under
   `VERB_MIN_MOVE_M`); (b) through `robot/server.py`'s own wheel loop, a
   standing `/wheels` FORWARD whose scan turns unusable covers <= 2 cm of
   true path after it, and when the scan comes back -- inside the watchdog
   window, with no new command -- the wheels stay at zero and the body
   does not move (the loop writes the clamped command back, so only a new
   command drives again).
3. **Red first.** The tests of 1-2 fail against `dev`'s `forward_clearance()`
   (the bug planted back) and pass with the fix.
4. **The fault itself, live on the desktop** (3.135's harness, the real
   stack: fake D500 on a pty, fake ESP32, frontier mission in
   `scaled_house`): `fmea.py F9`, 5 runs. Each: true path from the SIGSTOP
   + 0.3 s (`STALE_S`) to the SIGCONT <= 2 cm; 0 contacts; the recovery
   (a new mission drives after the SIGCONT) met; 0 harness errors.
5. **Nothing else moves.** `ready`'s fast tier passes, with every sweep pin
   unchanged; `tests/pivot_reference.py` untouched.
6. **On the board (owed, not this section's run):** 3.135.B re-runs
   `run.py row F9 --runs 5` on the Jetson against the merged fix; this
   session does not take the board lock (the orchestrator, 2026-11-04).

## Status

* 2026-11-04: criteria written before building; 3.144 claimed.
* 2026-11-04, **3.144.A built, criteria 1-5 met.**
  * *The fix:* `SafetyController._blind(scan)` -- no usable scan on a body
    whose wheels report -- is the one rule both ways: `forward_clearance()`
    answers `(0.0, "ahead_not_observed")` whatever the cone says, as
    `reverse_clearance()` answers `astern_not_observed`. `path_not_observed`
    is now a wheel-less body's answer only.
  * *Criteria 1-3:* `tests/test_stale_scan_forward.py` (8), red first on
    `dev`'s `safety.py` (5 fail: 244.5 cm read as clearance, a refused verb
    not raised, `/wheels` not clamped, 51.0 cm in a verb after the scan went,
    and through the wheel loop the wheels never stopped), green with the fix.
    Through the server's wheel loop (watchdog raised to 5 s so it cannot be
    what holds the wheels): stopped within 2 cm, and still at zero 0.5 s
    after the scan came back with no new command.
  * *Review (`/code-review`):* a blind clamp read as a wedge, so a stalled
    lidar under a nav2 goal would have marked keepout spots on free floor
    (`robot/wedge_watch.py`). Now a `*_not_observed` clamp neither counts nor
    resets a run (`test_a_blind_clamp_is_not_a_wedge`, red first). Also from
    it: one `_blind()` for both ways, the grid read before the scan as
    before, the server test polls. Thermos: nothing above low.
  * *Criterion 4:* `evaluations/stale-forward-3144/README.md` -- 5/5 pass,
    0.0 cm while stale (bar 2), 0 contacts, 5 FORWARDs refused
    `ahead_not_observed` a run, recovery 2.5-2.6 s, 0 errors. Before: 104.5 cm.
  * *Criterion 5:* `ready` on the merged commit (fast tier).
  * *Known limits:*
    * A body with wheels and no lidar can only turn: forward is refused too
      now (astern since 2026-10-02). On hardware day, a motor bring-up before
      the D500 is wired refuses FORWARD `ahead_not_observed` -- expected.
    * The split simulator's bundle older than `SENSOR_STALE_S`, or its first
      read before a bundle, now refuses forward too (it already refused
      astern); under load this can add refusals to live board numbers.
    * In a lidar outage longer than nav2's stall cut, explore's escape finds
      both ways refused and turns in place, up to 360 deg, with no scan to
      vet the turn (`brain/explore.py` `_still_wedged()`; turns stay allowed
      blind by design). Gating it on a usable scan is a follow-up in explore.
  * *Owed:* 3.144.B, F9 on the board (`run.py row F9 --runs 5`), for the
    session that holds the board lock.
