# 3.180 Explore's view goals: a look-from spot reused for a new direction, then the seen record

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.180; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the user, 2026-11-15, through the orchestrator.) 3.176.B's S7: three
hinted finds ended `exhausted` in houses where the unhinted find succeeded.
Two of them had the right hint. 3.176.B's results put the cause in
`ExploreAgent.seen`; a closer trace, below, puts most of it elsewhere.

**The trace (2026-11-15, before any code).** Each of the three missions was
replayed in process (3.176.B's sweep harness; deterministic). For each one
the trace logged every goal sent, and on every tick whether the target lay
inside the simulated camera's field of view, range and line of sight. At
the end it compared explore's own view-gap candidates, computed with
explore's `leave` clearance (0.33 m) from the final pose, against
`_view_allowed`.

| Mission | Where the target is | The gap that would show it | Why it was never sent |
|---|---|---|---|
| right 195 R (garbage can) | a corner, walls on two sides | the one unseen cell beside it, (3, 39); look-from (4.5, 36.5) | `_view_allowed`: an earlier view goal at (4.5, 37.5), 0.3 m away (< `retry.radius_m` 0.6), had looked at a different patch |
| right 194 U (backpack) | open kitchen floor | the unseen patch around it; look-from (10.5, 23.5) | `_view_allowed`: an earlier view goal within 0.6 m |
| wrong 247 R (house plant) | against walls and unknown space | none: every floor cell around it is in `seen` | named in one frame (85, 1.5 m, -24 deg) that `_sighting_now` did not act on; all 6 remaining gaps refused by `_view_allowed` |

At the end all three had view gaps left (3, 3 and 6), and **every one was
refused by `_view_allowed`**. That rule ("never stand at the same place
twice to look", from the den run's twelve view goals to one spot) bars a
look-from spot for every later patch, whichever way the camera then faces.
So in 195 and 194 the floor beside the target was known to be unseen, a
place to look from was reachable, and the de-dup rule alone kept the camera
away. 247 is the seen record's case: floor beside the target counted as
seen without the target ever being acted on.

**Scope** (the orchestrator, 2026-11-15: option C). Step A changes the
de-dup rule; step B, the seen record, opens only on its trigger below.

## 3.180.A: criteria and design (written 2026-11-15, before any code)

**The change.** `_view_allowed` keeps its first test: a patch whose centre
lies within `retry.radius_m` (0.6 m) of one already looked at is refused.
Its second test changes. Today a gap is refused when its look-from goal is
within 0.6 m of any earlier view goal. After A it is refused only if, from
an earlier view goal within 0.6 m, the camera also faced the same way:
the bearing from the look-from goal to the patch centre within
`VIEW_REUSE_DEG` (30 deg, half `CAMERA_FOV_DEG`) of the bearing that earlier
view goal looked along. The den guard holds: one patch with a drifting
centre seen from one spot lies in one direction. The rule loop's own copy
of the two tests in `_choose` calls `_view_allowed`, so the rule and the
pooled policies agree. Each view goal keeps its bearing beside
`_view_goals`; `ExploreAgent.view_reuses` counts view goals sent from within
0.6 m of an earlier one. Nothing in `brain/frontier.py`, the recall
functions, the goal features or `robot/` changes.

**Instrument.** In process through `MissionRunner`, on 3.176.B's sweep
harness (`evaluations/request-3176/sweep.py`: 3.116's 100 selection houses,
modes R and U). Four arms: `dev` (no names, no hint), `none` (names, no
hint), `right` and `wrong` (names, a hint). Run twice: the baseline from a
worktree of `dev` as it is before A, and A from this branch, paired by house
and mode. Each row adds `view_reuses` and the view goals sent.

## 3.180.A results (2026-11-15; the change at `45278578`)

**Instrument fault found first, and fixed.** The first paired run is void.
Its "baseline" ran this branch's brain, not `dev`'s: its rows carry
`view_reuses`, which only the change has. `evaluations/explore-365/
harness.py` puts `"."` first on `sys.path` (imported through
`goal-tap-388/oracle.py` and `goal-learn-396/train.py`), so a run launched
from one checkout loads that checkout's `brain/` whatever `REPO` names. The
baseline was re-run from inside a `dev` worktree (`2c1041ef`; no
`brain/`, `control/`, `robot/`, `sim/` or `world/` code differs from this
branch's base), and both harnesses now refuse a `brain` from outside `REPO`.
`tests/test_explore_view_3180_sweep.py` checks the baseline rows have no
`view_reuses`.

(`evaluations/explore-view-3180/runs/`, `score.json` by `score.py`).

**What it means.** The change does what it was built for. The three
traced misses now find the target, no mission loses a find, three more
finds appear (right twice, wrong once), and a mission that reuses no spot
is exactly `dev`'s. Its cost sits in the wrong-hint arm: 89 of its 200
missions changed, the median time rose 2.5%, and two missions reused a
spot 7 and 9 times, with more goals than `dev` (30 vs 19, 81 vs 52). That
is what A6 caught, and it is recorded as failed. A bound on reuses per
spot (for example at most one reuse) would be an amendment, written before
its run.

**B's trigger: not met.** 247 now finds, and no A mission ends without
`found` where the baseline found. B, the seen record, is not needed on
these numbers and stays unopened.
