# 3.177 Visual promise: does a frame say the target is near before it is in view

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.177; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** (the user, 2026-11-12: features of similar products and projects worth
building while the Rover waits on Waveshare.) VLFM (Boston Dynamics AI
Institute, ICRA 2024; run on a Spot in an unseen building) is the published
system closest to explore's design. Its one idea this project lacks: each
camera frame is scored for how well it matches the target's text, the score is
painted onto the map where the camera looked, and frontiers are ranked by it.
`docs/policy/ARCHITECTURE.md` already lists "visual promise" as a planned input
to goal choice, and nothing has measured it.

Why it may work where earlier attempts failed: 3.80 found that no model, asked
for a search **direction** from one out-of-view frame, beats naming the
commoner side (best 41% against 43%). A promise score asks less: not "which
way", only "does this view look like where the target would be", summed over
views on the map. And 3.103 closed the learned chooser with "the facts are the
limit": this is a new fact, from real perception, which the simulator's frames
cannot supply.

**Scope.** Offline and free first, on frames already recorded; nothing is
built into explore until the signal is shown to exist.
* A: on 3.92's labelled rig walks (2,261 frames, 21 walks), CLIP RN50's
  image-text score for the walk's target on frames with the target **out of
  view**. Does the score rise in the stretch before the target comes into view,
  against the same walk's frames far from it? No model call is paid; perception
  rows follow P24 (no GPU and CPU rows mixed).
* B, only if A meets its bar: the score painted on the map and given to goal
  choice as one more fact, judged on the Rover's own walks (the sim's renderer
  is not evidence for this).
* The cost on the board is a bar too: CLIP's image tower already runs per crop;
  a whole-frame pass is extra time and no extra model.

**Criteria (written in step A, before measuring; CLAUDE.md section 7).** At
least: the frame set pinned; a ranking measure (AUROC of "target comes into
view within k frames" from the score, per walk and pooled, with a bar above
chance and a per-object floor); the same for a shuffled-target control (must
sit at chance); extra milliseconds a frame on the Jetson at 15 W. A failed bar
closes the section as failed, recorded.

## 3.177.A: criteria (written 2026-11-12, before any CLIP pass over the frames)

**The frame set, pinned.** 3.92's corpus: every frame of the 21 walks with
an adjudicated `labels.json` (`target_visible_labels`), 2,261 frames, 535 in
view. `evaluations/visual-promise-3177/frames.py` writes `set.json` (each
frame's key, its label and the sha256 of its JPEG; no image) and one hash
over it; the CLIP run refuses a set whose hash differs. The frames stay on
the desktop (`PICAR_RECORDINGS`); none is committed or uploaded. The hash is
recorded under Results.

**The score.** CLIP RN50 (`openai`), the shipped `ClipScorer`, one image
encode of the **whole frame** through the shipped preprocess (resize, centre
crop: the pass a board would add), cosine against the walk's own
`description` (3.92's target words). All rows on this desktop's **CPU**
(P24: no GPU row in this step). The same encode is scored against every
description in the set, which gives the control for free; in a control an
object stands for its first walk's words (the red backpack's are
"burgundy backpack").

**The question, as labels.** Frames are a walk's recorded steps, in order,
and k counts steps by frame number (one walk starts at 2 and skips 94
and 210; a skipped number is a step with an unknown label, so a frame
that is not a positive and has one within k is left out);
only frames with the target **out of view** are ranked.
* **Positive (before):** out of view, and the target is in view in one of
  the next k frames.
* **Negative (far):** out of view, and no frame within k on either side has
  the target in view.
* Other out-of-view frames (just after the target left view) are left out.
* **k = 5** for the bars; k = 3 and k = 10 are recorded, no bar.
* A walk counts only with at least one positive and one negative. On the
  labels alone (counted before measuring) that is **9 walks, 97 positives,
  685 negatives**, four objects: charger cable (13 / 35), blue bottle
  (5 / 184), shoes (6 / 13), laundry basket (73 / 453). **Neither backpack
  is judged:** the walks where the bag comes into view have positives but
  no negative (every out-of-view frame is within 5 steps of a sighting),
  and the others never show it. The basket holds 75% of the
  positives, which is why the main bar is balanced by object.

**AUROC is always within a walk** (a positive is only ever compared with a
negative of the same walk, the comparison a mission would make); "pooled"
is every within-walk pair counted together.

**Criteria.**
1. **The set:** `set.json`'s hash matches, and a score row exists for all
   2,261 frames, each `device: cpu`.
2. **The signal (k = 5, the walk's own words):**
   * **2a:** the mean over the four objects of each object's pooled AUROC
     is **at least 0.65**.
   * **2b:** the pooled AUROC's 95% interval from a walk bootstrap (2,000
     resamples of the 9 walks, seed 0) has its lower end **above 0.50**.
   * **2c:** the per-object floor: each of the four objects' pooled AUROC
     is **at least 0.55**.
3. **The shuffled-target control:** the same frames and labels, each
   frame scored with the descriptions of the five **other** objects (the
   AUROC averaged over the five). It must sit at chance: the
   object-balanced mean **between 0.43 and 0.57**, and 2a's value at
   least **0.10 above** it. A control above chance means the score rises
   for anything near the end of a walk (a lower, closer view), not for
   the target.
4. **The cost on the board:** the extra milliseconds a frame of one
   whole-frame RN50 encode on the Jetson at 15 W, bar **at most 15 ms at
   the median**. **Not measured in A** (the board is out of this step's
   scope, the orchestrator 2026-11-12); it is B's first measurement and B
   is not built if it fails. This desktop's CPU time is recorded, no bar.

**Recorded, no bar:** k = 3 and 10; the per-walk AUROCs; the mean score
against frames-to-onset (does it rise, and from how far); the margin score
(the target's cosine minus the mean of the other five) through 2a-2c; the
whole frame as three square tiles (left, centre, right; the best tile) in
place of the centre crop.

**A is met** only if 1, 2a, 2b, 2c and 3 are all met. Any one failed closes
the section as failed (B is not started), recorded as such.
