# 3.224 Reverse along the Rover's own track: backing out over ground it just drove

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.224; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** On the Rover's first autonomous survey (2026-11-26, 3.140) the
explore policy drove into a nook and stuck: about 600 of nav2's reverse
commands were clamped `astern_occluded`. The D500 cannot see behind the
pan-tilt mast, and 3.218 refuses every reverse with a self box configured
(unseen is never clear, M3). The user chose, 2026-11-26, over a blind
creep and a hardware fix: **reverse only over ground the Rover itself just
drove across.** The body occupied that ground moments ago, so it was clear
then; the accepted risk is something that moved in behind since (a person,
a pet), bounded by a short distance and a time window.

**What it is.** The robot server keeps the body's recent track: a pose
dead-reckoned from the signed wheel travel (`get_wheel_state()`) and the
body's heading (`get_odometry()`), sampled whenever a clearance is asked.
With a self box configured, `reverse_clearance()` answers the RETRACE
room instead of `astern_occluded`: walking back through the track from
now, the farthest point behind the body (along its heading) that the body
occupied while staying within `RETRACE_LATERAL_CM` = 3 cm of the line and
`RETRACE_HEADING_DEG` = 10 deg of the heading, at most `RETRACE_MAX_CM` =
50 cm and no older than `RETRACE_WINDOW_S` = 120 s; source `retrace`.
That room is treated as a wall at its end, so the existing bar (3.44,
20 cm at normal speed) leaves at most 30 cm of travel. Under `retrace`
the ROS path's reverse speed is capped at `RETRACE_V_M_S` = 0.10 m/s. No
track (a pivot, drift off the line, a gap, a jump in the wheel travel):
`astern_occluded` as before. Without a self box nothing changes.

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. **Never behind its own track (ground truth).** In the simulator with a
   self box configured, over 200 seeded runs (forward 5-80 cm, sometimes
   with drift or a pivot, then reverse as far as allowed, on both the verb
   and the wheel-velocity paths): the rear bumper never goes more than
   1.0 cm behind the rearmost point it occupied on the recorded track
   (the truth, from the sim body's pose, never the track), and the reverse
   travelled never exceeds `RETRACE_MAX_CM` - 20 cm + 1.0 cm.
2. **Progress.** In the same sweep, after a straight forward of >= 40 cm,
   a reverse at normal speed moves >= 15 cm (it is not refused outright).
3. **The track ends where it should.** Unit tests: a pivot of more than
   10 deg, lateral drift over 3 cm, samples older than 120 s, a wheel
   travel jump (a board reboot) and unusable wheels each give
   `astern_occluded`.
4. **Nothing else changes.** Without a self box, every existing safety
   test passes and the frozen references (`tests/pivot_reference.py`,
   `tests/vet_reference.py`) are unchanged; forward, pivots and the
   watchdog are untouched.
5. **The ROS path's speed.** A wheel-velocity reverse under `retrace` is
   capped at 0.10 m/s (unit test).
6. **On the Rover** (the user present, their go): a stuck explore backs
   out under `retrace` at least once and the survey goes on; no contact
   (by eye). Recorded met or failed.
