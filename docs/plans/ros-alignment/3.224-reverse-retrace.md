# 3.224 Reverse along the Rover's own track: backing out over ground it just drove

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.224; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** On the Rover's first autonomous survey (2026-11-26, 3.140) the
explore policy drove into a nook and stuck: about 600 of nav2's reverse
commands were clamped `astern_occluded`. The D500 cannot see behind the
pan-tilt mast, and 3.218 refuses every reverse with a self box configured
(unseen is never clear, M3). The user chose, 2026-11-26, over a blind
creep and a hardware fix: **reverse only over ground the Rover itself just
drove across.** The body occupied that ground moments ago, so it was clear
then; the accepted risk is something that moved in behind since (a person,
a pet), bounded by a short distance and a time window.

**What it is.** The robot server keeps the body's recent track: a pose
dead-reckoned from the signed wheel travel (`get_wheel_state()`) and the
body's heading (`get_odometry()`), sampled whenever a clearance is asked.
With a self box configured, `reverse_clearance()` answers the RETRACE
room instead of `astern_occluded`: walking back through the track from
now, the farthest point behind the body (along its heading) that the body
occupied while staying within `RETRACE_LATERAL_CM` = 3 cm of the line and
`RETRACE_HEADING_DEG` = 10 deg of the heading, at most `RETRACE_MAX_CM` =
50 cm and no older than `RETRACE_WINDOW_S` = 120 s; source `retrace`.
That room is treated as a wall at its end, so the existing bar (3.44,
20 cm at normal speed) leaves at most 30 cm of travel. Under `retrace`
the ROS path's reverse speed is capped at `RETRACE_V_M_S` = 0.10 m/s. No
track (a pivot, drift off the line, a gap, a jump in the wheel travel):
`astern_occluded` as before. Without a self box nothing changes.

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. **Never behind its own track (ground truth).** In the simulator with a
   self box configured, over 200 seeded runs (forward 5-80 cm, sometimes
   with drift or a pivot, then reverse as far as allowed, on both the verb
   and the wheel-velocity paths): the rear bumper never goes more than
   1.0 cm behind the rearmost point it occupied on the recorded track
   (the truth, from the sim body's pose, never the track), and the reverse
   travelled never exceeds `RETRACE_MAX_CM` - 20 cm + 1.0 cm.
2. **Progress.** In the same sweep, after a straight forward of >= 40 cm,
   a reverse at normal speed moves >= 15 cm (it is not refused outright).
3. **The track ends where it should.** Unit tests: a pivot of more than
   10 deg, lateral drift over 3 cm, samples older than 120 s, a wheel
   travel jump (a board reboot) and unusable wheels each give
   `astern_occluded`.
4. **Nothing else changes.** Without a self box, every existing safety
   test passes and the frozen references (`tests/pivot_reference.py`,
   `tests/vet_reference.py`) are unchanged; forward, pivots and the
   watchdog are untouched.
5. **The ROS path's speed.** A wheel-velocity reverse under `retrace` is
   capped at 0.10 m/s (unit test).
6. **On the Rover** (the user present, their go): a stuck explore backs
   out under `retrace` at least once and the survey goes on; no contact
   (by eye). Recorded met or failed.

**Amendment 1 (2026-11-26, before any run).** Criterion 1's 200 seeded
runs go through the wheel-velocity path (`vet_wheel_velocity()`, the path
explore's nav2 drives); a verb runs on the wall clock in the simulator, so
the verb path gets one seeded run (forward, then a REVERSE verb) held to
the same truth bar. Variants: about 60 % straight, 20 % with drift (one
wheel 3 % fast) and 20 % with a 20 deg pivot before the reverse.

**Results** (2026-11-26, the simulator; `tests/test_reverse_retrace.py`,
23 tests; the numbers re-measured after the review fixes, unchanged):

1. **Met.** 200 seeded wheel-velocity runs (118 straight, 44 drift, 38
   pivot; `scaled_house` and `home_first_floor`): the rear bumper's worst
   distance behind its own track 0.00 cm (bar 1.0); the longest back-out
   30.5 cm (bar 31.0). The verb path's seeded run: 120 cm forward, 30.6 cm
   back, then refused `retrace`, within the bar. The first run failed the
   travel bar (one back-out of 40.0 cm after a long forward: the cap was
   per answer, not per back-out); fixed before recording, by cutting the
   room by how far the body has already backed up.
2. **Met.** 27 straight runs of >= 40 cm forward backed out 20.5-30.5 cm
   (median 30.0; bar 15).
3. **Met.** A pivot over 10 deg, drift off the line, the 120 s window, a
   wheel-travel jump and unusable wheels or odometry each end the track
   (unit tests); every run with a 20 deg pivot was refused (0.00 cm).
4. **Met.** No box, no track (`_retrace` None) and the old answer; 3.218's
   26 tests pass unchanged; `tests/pivot_reference.py` and
   `tests/vet_reference.py` untouched.
5. **Met.** A wheel-velocity reverse asked at 0.4 m/s under `retrace` went
   out at <= 0.10 m/s.
6. **Open:** on the Rover.

**Reviews** (`/code-review` and the Thermos bug pass, on Sonnet, on
`bc148fd7`). Both found, all fixed: the track's sampling could raise out
of `forward_clearance()` on a remote body's lost request (now any failure
drops the track); motion across a gap the track did not watch (the
fallback controller's track across a ROS spell, the brain's sparse asks,
an idle reboot) was read as a straight chord (now `GAP_S` = 0.5 s starts it
afresh). Both also noted `robot.retrace` missing from the brain's pinned
modules (`tests/test_brain_server.py`): not changed -- that file sits in
another session's claimed step, and the pin checks import time, where the
lazy import does not load; for 3.224's merge or the next owner of that file. `/code-review` alone, verified and fixed: under `retrace` a
return the lidar DOES see astern was ignored (the corridor astern with the
mast masked now binds); the 0.10 m/s cap was skipped when drop astern was
the nearer reading (the source is now tagged ` (retrace)` and the cap keys
on it). The bug pass alone, verified, not fixable in the simulator and so
stated as known limits for criterion 6 to watch: wheel slip or a stall
makes the track longer than the ground covered; the turn gate trusts the
body's yaw (wheels and the unmeasured `track_scrub` on the Rover). Noted,
not changed: a rear corner can swing about 2 cm outside the old footprint
at the tolerances' edge; a direct-mode REVERSE verb keeps its speed (the
bar follows it, 3.44).
