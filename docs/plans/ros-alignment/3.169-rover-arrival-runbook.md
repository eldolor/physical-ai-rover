# 3.169 The hands-on runbook for the Rover's arrival

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.169; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** The Rover is delivered 2026-11-08. The user has that day and two bench
days after it (11-09, 11-10); bench work then pauses. They need one
document to follow with the car on the bench. The orchestrator's brief,
2026-11-08. Docs only: no code, no run.

**Criteria (written before writing; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. `docs/hardware/ROVER-ARRIVAL-RUNBOOK.md` places every L0 and L1 row of 3.140
   on a day, or marks it as not fitting, with the reason.
2. Every step says what the person does, what they read back, and when to stop.
3. The measurement sheet covers every L1.1 and L1.2 value, with units and how to
   measure it.
4. The hard rules are at the top, and it says what stays powered while away.
5. It is in `docs/README.md`.

**Done 2026-11-08.** All five met (checked by reading; the row check is a grep
over L0.1-L0.12 and L1.1-L1.9).

**What the sources got wrong, found while writing. Not fixed here.**

* 3.140 L0.4 and `HARDWARE-READINESS.md` 5.6 say the board must be in `mainType`
  3. That was written for the General Driver. The ROS Driver the Rover ships
  with is closed loop in every `mainType`, and the stock one is 2
  (`robot/hardware_robot.py`, `sim/fake_esp32.py`, 3.25). The runbook checks the
  board's name and for `odl`/`odr` before any motion, instead.
* L0.4's "flash after the return-window checks" cannot be done in the two days,
  since two of those checks are floor runs. The runbook keeps the rule: dump
  now, flash on return.
* L0.2's stock app: the Acce kit ships with no Jetson, so our image should not
  have it. The row still runs, as a check.

**3.169.B, the Jetson comes out on Tuesday.** The away-power question is
settled (the user, 2026-11-08, through the orchestrator): "I will pull the
jetson out of the rover next Tuesday", read as Tuesday 11-10, the last day home.

Criteria (written before writing):

1. Tuesday ends with the pull-out, each step in the runbook's Do / Read back /
   Stop if form: the board cleared and the services stopped by Claude, the
   shutdown by hand; every lead off and the Jetson unmounted, its hardware kept
   together; the Jetson on the desk on its 19 V adapter, ssh and services
   confirmed by Claude; the Rover unpowered, cells out, charger unplugged.
2. The return starts with the re-mount and re-wire by the M steps, then the
   serial route and the board compare again, before the two floor checks.
3. No open away-power choice is left anywhere in the runbook, and the
   2026-12-08 section's timing counts the re-mount.
4. The runbook says the motor board cannot be reached from 11-11 until the
   user's return.

**Done 2026-11-08.** All four met (checked by reading). U9 to U12 replace the
old U9, which left the Jetson in the car; R1 and R2 are the return's first
steps. The order in R1 is this section's choice: the leads go on with no cells
in the car, then the cells, the charge and first power.

**3.169.C, what the first night on the Rover showed (2026-11-08).** Docs only;
no board use. The results go in 3.140's status as results so far, none of them
met. The kit has no ribbon: the Waveshare "Jetson Nano Adapter (B)" and a
10-wire cable replace it in M3 and in `UGV-ROVER-MOUNTING.md`. The runbook also
gains the board's clock, journal and Wi-Fi facts and a jumper wire. 3.140.B
refused its claim (it still waits on the Rover), so 3.169 holds the step and
section 3.140 was claimed whole, only for the status entry.
