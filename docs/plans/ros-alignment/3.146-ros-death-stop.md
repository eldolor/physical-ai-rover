# 3.146 A dead ROS container: the stop, and ROS named (3.135.B F4)

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.146; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.135.B's row F4 FAILED on the Jetson, 5 of 5 (2026-11-04, 15 W,
sim mode, `drive: ros`, ground truth). 3.135 criterion 3 makes it its own
section; the fix and its criteria are this section's to write.

* *The run:* a plain `explore` mission in `scaled_house`; `docker kill` of
  the ROS container 0.15 s into a FORWARD on an active nav2 goal.
* *The numbers* (the row's bars in brackets):
  * wheels zero **1.08-1.14 s** after the kill (<= 0.5 s);
  * **8.3-9.4 cm** moved (<= 5 cm);
  * the mission ended `failed`, but its reason was a bare "Server error
    '503 Service Unavailable' for url .../world/goal", ROS not named
    ("ends `failed` naming ROS");
  * no `ros_unavailable` refusal counted, and the readout's last refusal
    was `watchdog` ("status reads ROS DOWN").
* *What held:*
  * the recovery, every run: a new container, then an explore mission
    `found`;
  * 0 contacts, 0 late ticks;
  * the board left as found.
* *Causes, as far as the records show:*
  * the stop: the dead container's last wheel command stands until the
    1 s watchdog. `ros_up()`'s 0.5 s silence (`ROS_SILENCE_S`) refuses new
    verbs, but does not zero a standing command;
  * the reason: `control/remote_navigator.py` `set_goal()` calls
    `raise_for_status()`, which drops the robot server's 503 detail
    (`ros_unavailable: ...`, with its fault headers);
  * the readout: no refusal is recorded for the dead ROS. The watchdog
    writes the last record (3.86's open item).
* *Records:* `evaluations/jetson-fmea-3135/board/rows/F4-20261104-0816/` (on `plan/3.135.B-single-faults` until
  3.135.B merges), BOARD-BENCH's fault table; the desktop's
  `desktop/b-goal/`.
* *Re-test:* `run.py row F4 --runs 5` (board), `plant_bugs.py --rows F4`
  (desktop). Risky by CLAUDE.md section 6 (the safety and motion path).
