## What 2B is

Split `web-twin/app.js` (one IIFE of about 6,800 lines) into modules,
**one behaviour-identical move at a time**. The plan's Stage 2 text has the
detail. Write 2B's criteria into the section **before** starting. Suggested:

* **Every UI test passes at every step:** 318 today, in their modules' real
  order. `ready` passes.
* **The pixels don't move:** `evaluations/ux-3132/pixels.py` says identical
  for the 5 static states after each move (amendment 7's masks).
* **The harness doesn't move:** two runs give Stage 2A's numbers. M1-M7 are
  unchanged.
* **No new global.** ES modules (`<script type="module">`), or a short
  ordered list of classic scripts. Each new file goes in `assets.json`, gets
  a robot-server route with `no-cache`, and an ALB pattern.

**Watch out for:**
* **The ALB rule limit.** Twin rule 14 is full at 5 values, so new files
  need a new rule (priority 15 is free); teleop's rule 45 holds 3.
* **Module scripts are deferred and strict.** The init order at the end of
  `app.js` relies on running top to bottom. The `qrHideTimer` crash in the
  original audit is the kind of thing to expect.
* **`t` and `engineerMode`** are defined just after the error capture, at
  the top. Keep them available everywhere: the voice is read at call time.
* **Tests pin ids and classes.** No test reaches into `app.js` globals,
  because there are none.
