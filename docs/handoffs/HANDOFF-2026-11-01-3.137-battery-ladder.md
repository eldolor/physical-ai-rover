# Handoff 2026-11-01: 3.137.A, the battery ladder

Merged into `dev`. The plan section has the criteria, both amendments and the
numbers: `docs/plans/ros-alignment/3.137-battery-ladder.md`. How it is built:
`docs/engineering/safety/ENGINEERING.md`, "The battery ladder (3.137)".

For the next steps:

* **3.137.B (the twin):** read `GET /battery` `level` and `ladder`
  (`poweroff_in_s` under Reserve; `shutdown` once powering off), and
  `/mission/start`'s 409 `battery_low: ...`. Refusal reasons to word:
  `battery_reserve`, `battery_poweroff`, `battery_check`.
* **3.137.C (one real power-off):** `BATTERY_SHUTDOWN_CMDS` / `BATTERY_POWEROFF_CMD`
  in `picar-stack`'s environment. `service/tunnel/run.sh` exits when any of its
  servers does, so a step that kills the brain also takes the robot server
  (and the rest of the sequence) down: stop the brain's mission
  (`POST /mission/stop`) rather than its process, or restructure the units.
* **Known limits** (safety spec, Known gaps): nothing reads the pack with the
  robot server dead; a repeated reading after 12 s may predate the stop; a
  check holds nav2 still up to 15 s and its progress checker may abort the
  goal.
* **In-process harnesses:** sim time moves only with the robot, and there is
  no battery loop without a lifespan -- `evaluations/battery-3137/ladder_sim.py`
  passes 0.1 s a tick and polls the ladder in `idle` (amendment 2).
